# Home

Home is the first screen when you open ThreadCloud from your Shopify admin. It greets you by name and has two desks, each with clear paths for its work. Switch between them with the **Merchandising | Sales floor** toggle at the top right, next to **Settings**.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-03</div>
If a card or label here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Which desk opens first</div>
ThreadCloud remembers the desk you last chose, on this device. The first time, people who can create buying orders land on <strong>Merchandising</strong>, and everyone else lands on <strong>Sales floor</strong>.
</div>

## Search

The search bar under the title reads **Search orders, POs, styles, customers, brands**. Click it, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Windows). Results are grouped: **Needs you**, **Orders**, **Styles**, **Customers**, **Brands** and **Go to** (pages and Settings destinations).

- Use the arrow keys to move, <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open, <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close.
- With nothing typed, it shows what needs you and your recent orders.
- Try a brand, a PO number, an article code, or a customer name.

## The Merchandising desk

For buyers.

### Start here

| Card | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **New buying order** | "Drop a linesheet, build the order." See [Parse & Build](/workflows/build-order/). Shown only to people who can create orders. |
| **Receive** | "Pick the order, count what came in." The Receiving log, with a count of orders in their delivery window. See [Receiving](/workflows/receiving/). |
| **Merchandising board** or **Money** | Merchandising. It reads **Money** until your first buying season exists. Shown only with Merchandising access. |
| **Logistics** | "POs, labels, transfers", with a count of POs not sent. See [Logistics](/workflows/logistics/). |

While a new store is still finishing setup, the account owner also sees setup tiles here, such as **Confirm delivery calendar**, **Finish storefront** and **Refine catalog**. See [First-time setup](/setup/onboarding/). They go once the store is ready; after that, setup lives in Settings.

### Needs you

What's waiting on a person, grouped into cards. Each card counts its items, names the first few, and has one button. A card with a single item opens that item; otherwise it opens the list.

| Card | Button | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Supplier responses** | **Review** | The supplier's answer, or the list of them. See [Supplier confirmations](/workflows/supplier-confirmations/). |
| **POs not sent** | **Send** | The purchase order, or Logistics' unsent POs. The card names the oldest and how many days it has waited. |
| **Ready to push** | **Push** | The order's Enrich tab, nearest delivery window first |
| **Catalog enrichment** | **Review** | [Catalog setup](/settings/legacy-enrichment/) |

When nothing is waiting it reads **Nothing waiting on you.**

### Open orders

Your orders, grouped by buying season. Each season shows how many orders and their cost, and a bar: **Received**, **In delivery window** and **Still out**. Click a season to open or close it.

Each order row shows the brand, the season code and PO number, one line on where it stands, and one button:

| Line, for example | Button |
|---|---|
| 12 styles · no quantities yet | **Build** |
| 4 of 12 approved | **Enrich** |
| 12 approved · 8 pushed | **Push 12** |
| 40 of 120 units received | **Receive** |
| 12 pushed · 120 units received in full | **View** |

## The Sales floor desk

For the floor team.

### From your suppliers

When a supplier has told you something new, it shows at the top of the Sales floor. Shortages, unavailable items and boxes that arrived short come first. Each row has a chip, one plain sentence, the order's PO number and when it came in, for example "**Short** The brand can send 4 of 6 units of the navy jacket."

| Chip | Means |
|---|---|
| **Short** | The supplier can send fewer than you ordered |
| **Unavailable** | The supplier can't send a product |
| **Arrived short** | A box arrived with less than the supplier said |
| **Backordered** or **New date** | A later date for a product or the order |
| **Substitute offered** | The supplier offered something else |
| **Shipped**, **Confirmed** | Good news |
| **Box in** | A box was received and units are still to come |
| **Note** | Anything else the supplier wrote, such as their order number |

- <strong>Open</strong> takes you to the order: a reorder's page, the receiving page for a box, or the purchase order.
- <strong>Seen</strong> clears the row once you've dealt with it. <strong>Mark all seen</strong> clears every row. If it fails, the list says "Couldn't mark that seen. Try again." and the rows come back.

Marking updates seen is a per-person permission. It is on for every staff member unless the account owner turns it off in [Team access](/settings/users-roles/); the account owner always can. Without it, you see the updates but no Seen buttons.

The section is hidden when there is nothing new.

### On the floor

| Card | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Receive** | "Pick the order, count what came in." The Receiving log, with a count of orders in their delivery window. See [Receiving](/workflows/receiving/). |
| **Reorder** | "One style or a full store restock." The Reorders list. See [Reorder](/workflows/reorder/). |
| **Custom order** | "Made to measure, special order", with a count of custom orders received or in review. See [Custom orders](/workflows/mtm/). |
| **Staff playbook** | ThreadCloud basics for the team. |

### Customer orders

"Not yet received in store." Two cards, each with a count and how long the oldest has waited, for example "Oldest ordered 41 days ago":

- **Custom orders**: <strong>Receive custom orders</strong> opens the custom orders list on its **Ordered** tab.
- **Reorders**: <strong>Receive reorders</strong> opens a list of reorders waiting to be received, each with a **Receive** button. "No reorders waiting to be received." when there are none.

### Delivery windows open now

Orders in their delivery window with units still to come, each with its window dates, a bar for how much is received, and a line such as "25% received, 8 days left". Click one to open its Receive page. A long list ends with **All N orders in their window**, which opens the Receiving log. When there are none it reads **No delivery windows open.**

**Past their window** lists orders whose window has closed with units still out, so late shipments stay in view.

## Catalog enrichment banner

If your existing catalog has products waiting for a decision, a **Catalog enrichment** banner appears at the top of both desks. Click it to open [Catalog setup](/settings/legacy-enrichment/).

## Common questions

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">I can't see New buying order</div>
Creating buying orders needs the store's buying access. If the card is missing and you should have it, ask the store's account owner, then ThreadCloud.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">I can't see Merchandising or Money</div>
Those need Merchandising access. The account owner turns it on for you in <a href="/settings/users-roles/">Team access</a>.
</div>
