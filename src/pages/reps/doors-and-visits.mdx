# Stores and visits

A **store** is a shop you sell to. Each store has a page with your next visit, your buyers there, and your visit diary. **Today** shows your planned visits.

## Add a store

On the **Stores** tab, press **Add a store** and fill in **Store** (required), **City**, **Province or state**, **Country**, and **Website** (like store.com). Press **Add store**.

- **With a website**, ThreadCloud matches the store on ThreadCloud by its website.
- **Without one**, if ThreadCloud already knows a store that might be the same, it asks **Is this a store already on ThreadCloud?** Press **Same store** on the right one, or **A different store**. Nothing is saved until you answer.

## Add several stores

On the **Stores** tab, press **Add several stores**, under your list of stores. Paste one store per line, in this order: **Store, City, Province, Country, Website**. Only the store name is needed, so `Harbour Goods, Ottawa, ON` is a full line. Press **Check stores**. The next screen lists each line and marks the ones that **Will add**. A line that can't be added says why. Press **Add** and the number of stores to save them. Stores you already have are not added twice.

## The store page

Tap a store to open it. From top to bottom:

### Next visit

Your soonest booked visit here: when and where, your prep notes, and the seasons you chose to show. Each season is titled with its brand, like **Emanuel Berg, Spring 2027**, and shows its order deadline and whether it is ready, for example **Orders due Nov 14** and **Ready to sell**, or a count like **3 to check**. Tap one to open the season. With nothing booked it reads **No visit booked.**

### Write an order

Each brand's season is listed under **Write an order**, like **Emanuel Berg, Spring 2027**. Tap one. It opens this store's order for that season, or starts one. Pressing it again opens the same order, never a second one. See [Writing an order](/reps/orders/).

### Buyers

Add each buyer with **Buyer** (their name), **Role**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Private notes**, then **Add buyer**.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Private means private</div>
Only you see these. Others in your agency don't see the buyers and notes you add, and the buyer never does. Keep what you know about a buyer here, not in an order's note: an order's notes go to the buyer.
</div>

### Book a visit

Fill in **When** (date and time), **Where** (like In store or Showroom), tick the **Seasons to show**, add **Prep notes**, and press **Book visit**. The visit appears under Next visit, or in your diary, and on **Today**. Times use your phone's time zone.

### Orders here

Every order you have written for this store, each with its status. Tap one to open it. To see every order across your stores, use the **Orders** tab.

### Visits

Your past and later visits at this store, each with a status: **Planned**, **Done**, **Cancelled** or **No show**. On a planned visit, press **Mark done** after it, or **Cancel**. Either takes it off Today.

### Edit store and Remove store

At the bottom of the store page, press **Edit store** to change **Store**, **City**, **Province or state**, **Country** or **Website**, then press **Save**.

Press **Remove store** to take a store off your lists. The app asks **Remove this store?** first. Its orders, visits and buyers are kept, they just leave your lists.

## Today

**Today** lists **Visits today**, soonest first, and under **Visits this week** the rest of the next seven days. Tap one to open the store. With nothing planned it reads **No visits today**, with **Book a visit** beside it.

Only your own visits show. Your diary is yours: others in your agency keep their own.
