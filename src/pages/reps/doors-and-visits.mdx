# Doors and visits

A **door** is a store you sell to. Each store has a page with your next visit, your buyers there, and your visit diary. **Today** shows the visits you've booked.

## Add a store

On the **Doors** tab, fill in **Add a store**: **Store** (required), **City**, **Province or state**, **Country**, and **Website** (like store.com). Press **Add store**.

- **With a website**, ThreadCloud matches the store on ThreadCloud by its website.
- **Without one**, if ThreadCloud already knows a store that might be the same, it asks **Is this a store already on ThreadCloud?** Press **Same store** on the right one, or **A different store**. Nothing is saved until you answer.

## The store page

Tap a store to open it. From top to bottom:

### Next visit

Your soonest booked visit here: when and where, your prep notes, and the lines you chose to show. Each line links to its season's book, with its deadline and whether the kit is complete, for example **due Nov 14 · kit complete**. With nothing booked it reads **No visit booked.**

### Write an order

Pick the **Line** and press **Open order**. It opens this store's order for that season, or starts one. Pressing it again opens the same order, never a second one. See [Writing an order](/reps/orders/).

### Your buyers here

Add each buyer with **Buyer** (their name), **Role**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Private notes**, then **Add buyer**.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Private means private</div>
Only you see the buyers and notes you add. Others in your agency don't, and the buyer never does. Keep what you know about a buyer here, not in an order's note: an order's notes go to the buyer.
</div>

### Book a visit

Fill in **When** (date and time), **Where** (like In store or Showroom), tick the **Lines to show**, add **Prep notes**, and press **Book visit**. The visit appears under Next visit, or in your diary, and on **Today**. Times use your phone's time zone.

### Your visits here

Your past and later visits at this store, each with a status: **Booked**, **Done**, **Cancelled** or **No show**. On a booked visit, press **Mark done** after it, or **Cancel**. Either takes it off Today.

## Today

**Today** lists your booked visits for today, soonest first, and under **This week** the rest of the next seven days. Tap one to open the store. With nothing booked it reads **No visits today.**

Only your own visits show. Your diary is yours: others in your agency keep their own.
