# Earnings

**Earnings** shows what stores have booked, the commission coming on orders that have shipped, and, for a distributor, what stores owe you. Commission is worked out from the ship notice or invoice you add to the order.

It opens from the **Earnings** row on **Today**, and its back link goes to **Today**. The row shows only once a figure is above zero, so a new agent sees nothing here until an order is booked, a commission is recorded, or a store owes money. There is no Earnings tab.

## What you see

A strip at the top shows only the figures that apply to you, never a row of zeros:

| Figure | For |
|---|---|
| **Booked** | Everyone: the total of orders buyers have confirmed. |
| **Commission coming** | An agent: commission on what shipped, per currency. |
| **Owed to you** | A distributor: what stores owe you for shipped orders. |

Below are the groups that apply: **Commission** (agent), **Shipped orders** (agent), **Stores owe you** (distributor), **Booked** (everyone) and **Revised or duplicate**. An owner or admin sees the whole agency, marked **Whole agency** once there is a second person, and a **Paid** button on commission and store rows, which becomes **Not paid yet** if you undo it. A Member sees only their own orders. A distributor's cost and margin are never shown here to a Member.

## When commission appears

Commission is recorded when you add a **ship notice** or an **invoice** to an order. A confirmation records nothing: you earn on what ships. Until then the page reads **Commission shows once an order has a ship notice or invoice.** A distributor's page reads **Shows once an order ships.** A brand selling in house sees **Shows once a buyer confirms an order.**

## How it's worked out

- **Rate:** the brand's **Commission %** at the moment the document is added.
- **Base:** what the brand shipped. ThreadCloud takes the quantities the ship notice or invoice prints, at its price, or at the order's price when it prints none. When it prints no quantities, the base is the total of the version you sent the buyer.
- **Commission:** base times rate, to the cent.

Each row shows how it was worked out, for example **8% of 12,450.00 CAD shipped, from Emanuel Berg's ship notice (ASN-2291.pdf)**. When a supplier receives your orders, it reads that supplier's name.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">No rate, no amount</div>
A brand with no Commission % shows <strong>Not worked out</strong>. An owner or admin can add the rate on the brand page under <strong>Edit</strong>. Shipments recorded after that are worked out; ones already recorded stay Not worked out.
</div>

## Partial shipments, invoices and revised documents

Each ship notice or invoice is its own row, so partial shipments add up. Invoices take over from ship notices once they cover as many units. A row that doesn't count shows muted, with the reason, like **Covered by the brand's invoices**.

A revised or duplicate document looks like a second shipment. Press **Set aside** on the old one, under **Revised or duplicate**, so it isn't counted twice. **Count it again** brings it back. When a document printed no quantities, the row notes **the brand's document showed no quantities**.

Adding the very same file twice records it once.

## What Earnings isn't

Earnings is what you can expect from the brand's documents, not a record of what you were paid. **Paid** is a mark you set yourself.
