# Territory, claims and store confirmation

A **claim** is your word that you sell a brand in an area. You make one by adding a territory to a line. Stores can then confirm you.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-15</div>
If a button name here doesn't match what you see, the app changed and this page is stale. Tell ThreadCloud.
</div>

## Quick path

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Link the line to its brand on ThreadCloud.</li>
  <li>On the line, open <strong>Edit terms</strong> and fill in <strong>Territory</strong>.</li>
  <li>Open a store under <strong>Doors</strong> and ask it to confirm you.</li>
</ol>

## Say where you sell a brand

Only the agency owner can do this.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open the line from the <strong>Lines</strong> tab.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>Edit terms</strong>.</li>
  <li>Type the provinces or states you cover under <strong>Territory</strong>, like <strong>Ontario and Quebec</strong> or <strong>NY, NJ</strong>. A whole country works too, like <strong>Canada</strong>.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Save terms</strong>.</li>
</ol>

At the top of the line you then see what ThreadCloud read:

- **Claimed in: Ontario, Quebec.** These are your claims.
- **Not read: Eastern Canada. Name provinces or states.** ThreadCloud does not guess. A region or a city is not read, so name the provinces or states.
- **Add the provinces or states you cover so stores can confirm you.** The line is linked to its brand but has no territory yet.

## Who sees a claim

- **Only your agency sees it** until stores confirm it.
- **Two stores confirming** you shows you on the brand's record. At least one of them must be a store you asked. The line then reads **Confirmed by 2 stores** and **Shown on the brand's record**.
- **One store confirming** reads **Confirmed by 1 store**. That is not enough yet.
- **If another agency claims the same brand and area**, both claims go **Under review** with ThreadCloud. Neither agency sees the other. ThreadCloud sorts it out with you.

## Ask a store to confirm you

You can ask once the line is linked to its brand and the door is matched to its store: ThreadCloud matches it by the store's website, or when you picked **Same store** while adding the door. It works the same whether or not the store uses ThreadCloud yet; if it doesn't, your ask waits until it does.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open the store under <strong>Doors</strong>.</li>
  <li>Find <strong>Ask the store to confirm you</strong>. Each of your linked lines is listed.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Ask to confirm</strong> beside the brand.</li>
</ol>

The store answers in its ThreadCloud app.

Beside each brand you then see where it stands:

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Ask to confirm** | You have not asked this store about this brand. |
| **Asked, waiting** | The store has not answered yet. |
| **Confirmed** | The store confirmed you. |
| **Answered** | The store has answered. You cannot ask again. |

If the section is missing, the door is not matched to its store yet, or you have no line linked to its brand. If you see **Link that line to its brand on ThreadCloud first**, open the line and press **Check ThreadCloud**.
