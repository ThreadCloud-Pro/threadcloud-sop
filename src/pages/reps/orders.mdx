# Writing an order

An order is one store's order for one brand's season. You pick in the store, send the buyer a version to confirm, then send the order to the brand (or to its supplier). The brand's confirmation, ship notice and invoice go on the order as they arrive.

## All your orders

The **Orders** tab lists every order. Each row shows the brand and season, like **Emanuel Berg, Spring 2027**, the store and its units, and the status. Tap one to open it. On a wider screen the list stays on the left and the order opens beside it, with **Pick an order** until you choose one. With none it reads **No orders yet**, with **Write an order** under it, which opens **Stores**. To see one store's orders, open that store: they are under **Orders here**.

## Quick path

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On the store, under <strong>Write an order</strong>, tap the brand's season, like <strong>Emanuel Berg, Spring 2027</strong>.</li>
  <li>On the <strong>Styles</strong> tab, <strong>tick</strong> each style the buyer wants, and add sizes, a price or a note. Or press <strong>Scan a tag</strong>.</li>
  <li>On the <strong>Buyer</strong> tab, press <strong>Send to the buyer</strong>, then <strong>Text</strong>, <strong>Email</strong> or <strong>Copy message</strong>, and send it to the buyer yourself.</li>
  <li>When the buyer confirms, on the <strong>Brand</strong> tab (<strong>Supplier</strong> when a supplier receives your orders) press <strong>Download the order</strong>, press <strong>Email</strong> and the name, attach the file, send it, and press <strong>Mark sent to</strong> that name.</li>
  <li>Add the <strong>confirmation</strong>, <strong>ship notice</strong> and <strong>invoice</strong> as they come in.</li>
</ol>

The order page has three tabs across the top: **Styles**, **Buyer** and **Brand**. They stay in view as you scroll. Above them, a strip shows **Picked**, **Units** and a total for each currency, like **EUR total**, and a card tells you what needs you next. The steps that show where the order stands are at the top of the **Buyer** tab. The order's back link goes to **Orders**. The store is the first row on the **Styles** tab, with **Orders due** and the date under it.

## Pick in the store

The **Styles** tab lists the whole season. Each style shows its codes and price.

- **Tap the round tick** to pick a style. Tap it again to unpick it.
- On a picked style, tap **Add sizes, price or a note**. Type the quantity for each size (or one **Qty** when the style has no size run), change **Price each** if the brand agreed a different price, add a **Note**, and press **Save**.
- Once a style has quantities and a price, it shows the units and their total, like **12 units · 2,868.00 EUR**.
- **Scan a tag** finds a style by its tag (see [Scan to order](#scan-to-order)). **Find a style** searches the season by code or name.
- The sizes are a table, not a list. Sizes run across the top with one box under each, and the table scrolls sideways inside the card when the run is long. On a picked style the sizes show as a read-only table with a **Units** total.
- A season with no styles reads **No styles yet**, with **Read the line sheet** beside it.

Picking copies the season's wholesale price onto the order, so a later line sheet doesn't reprice an order you're already writing. Quantities are whole numbers up to 999 per size.

The header counts what's picked, like **Styles, 8 picked**, and adds **2 on this phone** while picks wait offline. With no signal, picks are kept on the phone and sent when you're back online (see [With no signal](/reps/#with-no-signal)).

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">An order's notes go to the buyer</div>
The <strong>Note</strong> on a picked style is printed in the buyer's version. Keep private notes on the store page under <strong>Buyers</strong>.
</div>

## Scan to order

At an appointment you can find a style by its tag instead of scrolling the styles. On the **Styles** tab, press **Scan a tag**. A sheet opens with the camera.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Hold the tag in view and press <strong>Scan the tag</strong>. Or type a style code or name under <strong>Or type a code or name</strong>.</li>
  <li>The phone reads a barcode first, then a printed style code. Both are read on the phone, so this works with no signal (see <a href="/reps/#with-no-signal">With no signal</a>). When there is a signal and the phone could not match the tag, ThreadCloud reads it for you as a last step.</li>
  <li>The style opens with its photo, name, code, colour and wholesale price, and one row for each size in its run. Press the plus and minus buttons, or type the number. A size never goes below 0.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Next</strong> to save and scan the next tag, or <strong>Done</strong> to save and go back to the order.</li>
</ol>

ThreadCloud matches the tag against this season's styles, by style code and then model code, ignoring capitals, spaces and dashes. A colour printed on the tag narrows it.

| You see | What it means |
|---|---|
| The style opens | One match. |
| **Which colour** | The code is on more than one colour. Pick the colour you mean. |
| **Search styles**, with the code filled in | Nothing matched. Search by name or a different part of the code. |
| **Emanuel Berg's barcodes aren't in this season yet.** | The barcode number matched nothing. Scan the printed style code, or search. |
| **No style in Spring 2027 matches.** | The search found nothing. |

A style saved with every size at 0 still counts as picked, so you can send it to the buyer with or without quantities. If a save does not go through, the sheet says **Did not save. Check the signal and scan it again.** and you can try again.

### The sample is one colour, the style comes in others

The sample on the table is usually one colour. After a scan, the sheet shows the sample's photo, the style name and price, and **Colours of this style** with every colour of that style. The scanned colour is ticked and marked as the sample. Tick the other colours the buyer wants.

If a colour is not on the season yet, press **Add a colour** and add it from its swatch: under **Photograph the swatch with its code showing**, take the photo. ThreadCloud reads the code and name and says **Read from the swatch. Check it before you add.** Check **Colour code** and **Colour name**, fix them if they are wrong, and press **Add colour**. It is added to the season with the sample's name, prices and size run. Adding a colour needs a connection, and offline the sheet says so.

Then press **Sizes**, or **Sizes for 3 colours**, and fill the sizes for each ticked colour in turn (**Colour 2 of 3**). **Next colour** saves one and moves to the next.

The swatch photo stays with that colour. It shows on the buyer's confirm page and goes to the store as a swatch (see [Did it reach the store's TC Retail?](#did-it-reach-the-stores-tc-retail)).

## Made-to-order pieces

When the brand makes garments to order, the **Styles** tab has a **Pieces** group with **Add a piece**. Where the brand's [made-to-order form](/reps/lines-and-seasons/#the-made-to-order-form) allows it there is also **Made to measure**. A **piece** is one made-to-order garment, priced from that form.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Press <strong>Add a piece</strong>. Fill in the <strong>Client</strong> where the page asks for one (made to measure), and pick the <strong>Garment</strong>.</li>
  <li>Pick the <strong>Model</strong> and the <strong>Fabric</strong>, then each option the form lists (like <strong>Lining</strong> or <strong>Buttons</strong>, with the code from the made-to-order form). Next to <strong>Fabric</strong>, press <strong>Photograph</strong> and take a photo of the fabric label: ThreadCloud reads its code and selects the fabric. Nothing saves by itself, so check what it picked. If the code is not on the form it says so, for example <strong>Read F-123, not on this form</strong>. It needs a signal; typing and picking always work.</li>
  <li>Fill in the <strong>Sizes</strong> (for made to measure, the <strong>Starting size</strong> and <strong>Changes, in cm</strong>), a <strong>Custom detail</strong> if there is one, and the price under <strong>Detail and price</strong>. For an agent or a brand selling in house it is the brand's price from the form. A distributor types <strong>Your price to the store, each</strong>.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Add to the order</strong>. Open the piece later to change it with <strong>Save</strong>, or press <strong>Remove from the order</strong>.</li>
</ol>

**A store's usual.** Most pieces for one store repeat the same model and options. Tick **Make this the usual for** the store's name before you save, and the next piece for that store, brand and garment starts from them (**Model and options are the store's usual.**). Fabric, sizes and any option the form marks as picked fresh each time are never copied. **Clear the usual** removes it. If the form has no prices yet, the page offers **Open the made-to-order form**.

The model's code and version travel with the piece. The order sent to the brand shows the version first, and the store's draft order and its purchase order carry the model code and the option line.

## Send to the buyer

On the **Buyer** tab, **To** lists the buyers you have on the store, and **What** shows the version, the picks, the units and the total. Add a **Note to the buyer (optional)** and press **Send to the buyer**, the one main button, at the bottom of the page. The line above it reads **Freezes this order as version 2.** (the next number). Every send is a **version**: version 1, then 2, and so on. A check shows **Sent to the buyer** and **Waiting for the buyer to confirm**, and the order reads **Waiting on the buyer**.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">ThreadCloud doesn't send it for you</div>
The message appears under <strong>Version 1</strong>, with <strong>Text</strong> and <strong>Email</strong> for each buyer (their name follows the word, or <strong>the buyer</strong>) and <strong>Copy message</strong>. Send it to the buyer yourself, by text, email or WhatsApp. It ends with the link the buyer confirms from.
</div>

The message lists each style with its colour, codes, price, sizes and quantities, and your note, then the total per currency, your name and your agency, and the link. Units with no price are left out of the total, and the message says so.

**Send to the buyer** waits while picks are still on the phone, so a version never goes out without them.

## The buyer confirms

The link opens a page that opens with who it is from and the version: you or your agency (or the brand), then **For** the store and **version** and its number. The title is the brand and season, like **Emanuel Berg, Spring 2027**, with **Invoiced by** the company that invoices the store beneath it: the brand for an agent, or your agency for a distributor or a brand selling in house (see [Orders go to, and who invoices the store](/reps/lines-and-seasons/#orders-go-to-and-who-invoices-the-store)). Figures show the **Units**, a total for each currency (like **CAD total**) and the deadline (like **Dec 15**, with the weekday in its label). Then **The order**, with a count of styles, lists each style as a card: the colour's photo beside it (its own showroom photo, or its swatch when it has nothing else), its codes, units, price and sizes. It never shows your commission, your cost or your margin.

**Print or save as PDF** is a row under the order, for the buyer's own copy. At the bottom the buyer has two buttons:

- **Review and confirm** opens a sheet that restates the units, the value and who invoices, and says that you are told and that this version cannot change after. The buyer types **Your name** and presses **Confirm** and the number of units.
- **Ask for changes** opens a sheet: they tick **Which style?** (or **The whole order**), type **Your name** and **What should change?**, and press **Send to** your first name.

They can't change a quantity or a price: you own every number. Once answered, the page shows **Confirmed** or **Changes asked** with **Confirmed by** or **Asked by** their name, and keeps **Print or save as PDF**.

Their answer shows in the card at the top of your order: **Confirmed by** and their name, or **Changes asked by** their name with their note. The order reads **Confirmed** or **Changes asked**.

## Change an order after it was sent

Edit the picks as usual. Any change, even to a note, shows **Changed since version 2. Send the buyer version 3.** with **Send** beside it, and the buyer's old link reads **Your rep is updating this order**. Press **Send**, then **Send to the buyer**: the order moves to the next version, and an old link reads **There is a newer version**.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Orders already with the brand keep their status</div>
Changing a pick on an order marked <strong>With the brand</strong> keeps that status, and what the brand sends can still be added. The page reads <strong>Changed since version</strong> and its number, and the buyer's old link reads <strong>Your rep is updating this order</strong> until you send the next version. Change an order the brand already has only when the buyer must confirm the change.
</div>

## Did it reach the store's TC Retail?

Once the buyer confirms, ThreadCloud tries to put the order into the store's own ThreadCloud, which is **TC Retail**. When it works, the **Styles** tab shows **In** the store's name and **TC Retail**, with **Reached**. When it can't, the order says why in plain words, for example **Blazer is not on TC Retail, so the order stays with you.** or **The store has not switched on orders from reps yet.** A distributor's order arrives there naming the distributor as the supplier. Each colour's real photos come with it. A swatch photo comes marked as a swatch, so the store does not use it as a picture of the garment. Photos made by AI never cross.

Most stores do not use ThreadCloud, so this is normal. The order is still yours and still valid: you send it to the brand as usual. Nothing shows here until the buyer has confirmed.

## Send it to the brand or supplier

The third tab is **Brand**. When your brand page says a supplier receives the orders, it reads **Supplier** instead, and every name below is the supplier's. A brand selling in house has no brand steps, so the tab holds only what you add.

Once the order has picks, the tab shows **Send to** and the name (for a distributor, **Your PO to** the brand). It lists the contact person, phone and order email you set under **Orders go to** on the brand page (see [Where orders go](/reps/lines-and-seasons/#where-orders-go)). With no order email it reads **No order email yet** and offers **Add** the name's **order email**.

Once every pick is saved, you can:

- Press **Download the order** for a spreadsheet of the order, or **Download your PO** if you are a distributor. A style picked with no quantities still has its own row, with the size and quantity blank. It lists the agency, rep, store and its address, buyer, **Store's PO number**, brand, season and deadline, then each style, colour, size, quantity, price, **Currency** and **Total**. A distributor's price column reads **Your cost**, and only an owner or admin sees it.
- Press **Email** and the name. Your own mail app opens, addressed to that order email. Attach the downloaded file and send it yourself: ThreadCloud does not send it.

If picks are still saving or waiting on the phone, the page says so and holds these buttons until they are saved.

Then press **Mark sent to** the name. The order reads **With the brand** (**Ordered** for a distributor). The button shows while the order is Waiting on the buyer or Confirmed. It only records that the order went.

## Add what the brand sent

On the **Brand** (or **Supplier**) tab, under **From the brand** (**From the supplier**), pick **What it is** (**Confirmation**, **Ship notice** or **Invoice**), choose the file, and press **Add to the order**. ThreadCloud reads it and keeps the file. A confirmation is listed as that company's own, like **Emanuel Berg's confirmation**.

| You add | The order reads | And |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmation** | The company's name, then **confirmed** | ThreadCloud compares it with the version you sent. |
| **Ship notice** | **Shipped** | Your expected commission is recorded (see [Earnings](/reps/earnings/)). |
| **Invoice** | **Invoiced** | Your expected commission is recorded. |

What the brand sends only ever moves an order forward. It can be added once a version has been sent to the buyer; before that it reads **Send the buyer a version before adding what the brand sent.** Files already added are listed under **On this order**.

### What the brand changed

When the confirmation differs from what you sent, **What** the name **changed** lists each difference, for example **size 50: ordered 4, brand has 2** or **250.00 CAD became 260.00 CAD**. Each is marked:

- **Notice**: less than ordered, a dropped style or a lower price. Tell the buyer.
- **Buyer must confirm**: a style the brand added, more than ordered, or a higher price. The page reads **2 of Emanuel Berg's changes need the buyer's OK.** with **Review** beside it. Update the order to match and send the buyer a new version.

Once the buyer confirms the new version, each change it took up reads **Buyer confirmed** and the warning goes. A change the new version left out, or took up only in part, still reads **Buyer must confirm**, and anything left over is listed under **Still to confirm with the buyer**: for example, the brand added two of a style and the version has one. A style picked with blank quantities or a blank price, or with a price in another currency, has not been agreed to. Fill it in and send another version.

When nothing differs it reads, for example, **Emanuel Berg confirmed the order as sent.** A distributor's own prices are not compared, since the store pays the distributor's prices.

## Order statuses

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Being written. |
| **Waiting on the buyer** | A version was sent; waiting for the buyer's answer. |
| **Confirmed** | The buyer confirmed this version. |
| **Changes asked** | The buyer asked for changes. |
| **Changed since sent** | Edited after it was sent. Send the buyer the next version. |
| **With the brand** | You marked it sent to the brand or supplier. (**Ordered** for a distributor.) |
| **Emanuel Berg confirmed** | The confirmation was added. The status prints the receiver's name. |
| **Shipped** | A ship notice was added. |
| **Invoiced** | An invoice was added. |

A shipped or invoiced order can't be changed here.
