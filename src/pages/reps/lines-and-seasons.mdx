# Lines and seasons

A **line** is a brand you represent. Each line has **seasons** (SS27, FW27), and each season has a **book**: every style and colour you can sell, with its sizes and prices. You fill the book by reading the brand's linesheet into it.

If a button name here doesn't match what you see, the app changed and this page is stale. Tell ThreadCloud.
</div>

## Quick path

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Lines</strong> tab: add the brand under <strong>Add a line</strong>.</li>
  <li>On the line, open a season under <strong>Open a season</strong>.</li>
  <li>On the season, add the linesheet under <strong>Add a linesheet</strong>: pick its currency, then <strong>Read linesheet</strong>.</li>
  <li>Add the brand's photos under <strong>Add photos</strong>, if the linesheet didn't bring them.</li>
  <li>Set the <strong>order deadline</strong> and <strong>delivery windows</strong>.</li>
  <li>Open <strong>To check</strong> and clear each style the reader was unsure of.</li>
</ol>

## Add a line

On the **Lines** tab, fill in **Add a line**:

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | The brand's name. Required. |
| **Brand website** | For example brand.com. |
| **Territory** | Where you sell it, for example Ontario and Quebec. |
| **Commission %** | Your rate, like 8 or 8.5. Earnings uses it, so set it now. |
| **Brand pays commission** | On shipment, On invoice, or When the store pays. |
| **Wholesale currency** | The three-letter code the brand usually prices in, like EUR. It's the default for this line's linesheets. |

Press **Add line**. ThreadCloud checks whether the brand is already on ThreadCloud:

- **This brand is already on ThreadCloud. Is it this one?** Press **Yes** with the brand's name if it's the same brand, or **No, a different brand**.
- **This brand is not on ThreadCloud yet.** Press **Add it to ThreadCloud**, or **Not now**.

Linking the line to its brand on ThreadCloud is how stores will later recognise your orders. A store sees nothing of yours until it confirms it knows you. If you pressed Not now, the line page offers **Check ThreadCloud** to link it later.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Get the commission right the first time</div>
The app can't change a line's commission rate, payout terms or currency after it's added. Earnings uses the rate on the line at the moment a shipment is recorded. If it's wrong, tell ThreadCloud.
</div>

## Open a season

On the line, fill in **Open a season**:

- **Season code**: letters and digits, like SS27. Spaces are dropped, so "FW 2027" becomes FW2027.
- **Name**: optional, like Spring Summer 2027.
- **Order deadline**: optional here; you can set or change it later.

Press **Open season**. Each code can be used once per line: a second SS27 reads **SS27 is already on this line.**

## Read the linesheet

On the season, under **Add a linesheet**:

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Choose the file</strong>: a PDF, a photo (JPEG or PNG), or a spreadsheet (XLSX, XLS or CSV), up to 32 MB.</li>
  <li><strong>Pick the currency</strong> under <strong>Prices on this sheet are in</strong>: CAD, USD, EUR, GBP, or <strong>Other</strong> and type the code (like CHF).</li>
  <li>If retail is in a different currency, open <strong>Retail in another currency</strong> and pick it. Otherwise leave it on <strong>Same</strong>.</li>
  <li>Optional: add a <strong>Note for the reader</strong>, for anything the sheet doesn't say.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Read linesheet</strong>. A PDF or photo can take up to a minute; the button reads <strong>Reading the linesheet…</strong></li>
</ol>

The result shows above the form, for example **Book updated: 24 added. Unmarked prices read as USD. 18 photos from the sheet added to 24 styles.** Read any notes listed under it.

### How the currency is decided

The currency you pick covers every price the sheet doesn't mark. A currency the sheet prints always wins.

| The sheet prints | You picked | The price is stored as |
|---|---|---|
| 478, no symbol | USD | 478 USD |
| $478 | USD or CAD | 478 in the dollar you picked |
| EUR 478 | USD | 478 EUR. The note says the sheet printed EUR. |
| $478 | EUR | **No currency, flagged to check.** A "$" can't be euros: pick the right dollar and read the sheet again. |
| A different currency beside the price, or a column mixing currencies | Anything | **No currency, flagged to check.** Check it against the sheet. |

ThreadCloud never guesses: a price it can't be sure of is left blank and the style is flagged, rather than stored in the wrong currency.

The currency chips start on the one you used for this line's last sheet, or on the line's wholesale currency for a first sheet.

### Reading a sheet again

When the brand sends a revised sheet, read it into the same season. Styles already in the book are updated, new ones are added, and anything the new sheet doesn't mention is kept. A price list without sizes doesn't wipe the sizes.

Your work survives a re-read:

- A style you confirmed stays confirmed if the new sheet says the same thing.
- A field you corrected by hand keeps your value. The result says **Kept your corrections on 3 styles.**

To fix a sheet read in the wrong currency, pick the right one and read the sheet again.

## Photos

A style with a photo shows it on its row in the book, large on the style's page, and beside it on an order, so you see what you're picking in the store. Photos come in three ways.

### From a PDF linesheet

Reading a PDF linesheet also takes the photos it prints. Each photo goes on the style whose code is printed directly beneath it, and on the colour printed there. When the text beneath names no colour, the photo goes on every colour of that style. The result line says how many, like **18 photos from the sheet added to 24 styles.**

A photo with no style code beneath it is skipped, for example when the sheet prints the code beside the photo or above it. Add those from a folder. If the photos can't be taken from the sheet at all, the book is still read, and the note says **The sheet's photos couldn't be taken from it. Add them from a folder instead.**

### From the brand's photo folder

Brands often send a folder of photos named with the style code, like **LD1234_010_front.jpg**. Once the book has styles, the season shows **Add photos**:

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Under <strong>Add photos</strong>, choose the photos: a whole folder on a computer, or several from your phone's library.</li>
  <li>They go up one at a time, with a bar reading <strong>Adding 3 of 12…</strong></li>
  <li>The result says how many landed, like <strong>12 photos added to 10 styles.</strong></li>
</ol>

How a name is matched:

- The style code must be in the name as a whole: **LD1234_010_front.jpg** or **ld-1234 front.jpg** both name LD1234.
- A colour code or colour name right beside the code picks that colour: **LD1234_010** or **LD1234_navy**.
- A name with the style code but no colour beside it goes on every colour of that style.
- A name with no style code, like **IMG_0042.jpg**, is not kept. The result lists it under **matched no style**: rename it with the style code, or add it on the style's page.

JPEG, PNG and WebP photos up to 25 MB each. iPhone HEIC photos are refused with **iPhone HEIC photos can't be shown yet. Export as JPEG and add again.**

### On a style's page

Open the style, tap **Add a photo**, choose the photo and press **Add photo**. It goes on that style whatever its name, and the page reads **Photo added.**

A style's first photo is the one shown on its row and on orders. A photo can't be removed from the app yet; if one is wrong, tell ThreadCloud.

## Setting up the season

A new season shows **Set up the season**, a path of four steps with a count like **1 of 4**:

1. **Set the order deadline**
2. **Read the linesheet**
3. **Add the photos** (optional)
4. **Check the flagged styles**

The next step is lit and has its own button: **Set dates**, **Add it**, **Add photos** or **Start checking**. A done step shows a tick. A step with something left says what, in amber:

- Under **Read the linesheet**: **4 styles without wholesale**, **without retail** or **without a size run**, or **Wholesale in CAD and EUR** when the book holds more than one wholesale currency. The button reads **Read again**: read a corrected sheet, or edit the styles.
- Under **Add the photos**: **No photos yet**, or a count like **18 of 24 styles have a photo**.
- Under **Check the flagged styles**: a count like **2 styles to check against the linesheet**.

When the first, second and fourth steps are done, the path folds into one line, **Ready to sell**. Photos are welcome but never hold a season back. Tap **Setup** on that line to see the steps again.

Below is **The book**: every style with its codes, sizes and prices. Tap a style to open it.

## Season dates

Brands move their dates, so the order deadline and delivery windows can be changed at any time. On the season, tap **Change dates**, or **Set dates** on the setup path if none are set.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Set the <strong>Order deadline</strong>, or clear it.</li>
  <li>Under <strong>Delivery windows</strong>, give each window a <strong>Name</strong> (like D1), the day it <strong>Starts shipping</strong>, and its <strong>Cancel</strong> date.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Save dates</strong>.</li>
</ol>

- To remove a window, clear its row and save.
- The page shows two empty rows. For more, save and two more appear, up to 8 windows.
- Windows are listed in the order they ship.

The season page then shows the deadline with the days left, and each window, like **D1 · Jan 15 to Feb 28**.

## Check the styles the reader was unsure of

When the reader isn't sure about a style, it flags it **check** and says why, for example **The sheet does not clearly show the colour name.** or **The sheet does not show the currency of the wholesale price, so it is left blank.**

A flag never stops you picking or sending the style, and the buyer never sees it. But clear them before you sell from the book.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On the season, tap <strong>To check</strong> above the book.</li>
  <li>Tap a style. The box at the top says why it's flagged.</li>
  <li>Tap <strong>Open the linesheet ↗</strong> to see the brand's original.</li>
  <li>If the book matches the sheet, press <strong>Looks right</strong>.</li>
  <li>If not, open <strong>Edit</strong>, correct it, and press <strong>Save and mark checked</strong>.</li>
</ol>

You go back to the book, where the style shows a green **checked** for a moment. When nothing is left, the filter reads **Nothing left to check.**

### Editing a style

**Edit** has the name, colour, sizes, and each price with its currency:

- **Sizes, with commas between**: S, M, L. Commas keep a size like **One Size** whole. A European half size keeps its comma: **42,5, 43** is two sizes. Leave the box as it is and the sizes don't change.
- **Wholesale** and **Retail**: a price like 478.00, with its **Currency** beside it (like USD). A price needs a currency: this is where you fill in a currency the reader left blank.

If someone reads the sheet again while you have a style open, nothing you press is saved, and the page shows the style as read now: **The sheet was read again while this was open, so nothing was saved. Here it is as read now.** Check it again.
