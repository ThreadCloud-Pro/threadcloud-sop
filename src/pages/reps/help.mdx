# Help

The **Help** tab is where you ask how to do anything in the app. **Winston** answers from this manual. What he can't answer goes to Gregory at ThreadCloud, and his answer comes back to the same thread.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-10</div>
If a button name here doesn't match what you see, the app changed and this page is stale. Tell ThreadCloud.
</div>

## Ask a question

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Tap <strong>Help</strong> at the bottom right.</li>
  <li>Type in <strong>Your question</strong>, or, on a new thread, tap one of the suggested questions.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Ask Winston</strong>. The button reads <strong>Asking Winston…</strong> while he thinks.</li>
</ol>

Winston knows which page you came from, so "how do I add this?" works. He names the screens and buttons as the app shows them, in bold.

## When Winston doesn't know

If the manual doesn't cover your question, Winston sends it to Gregory. The thread then reads **Gregory has your question. His answer shows up here.**

You don't need to wait on the page. When the answer arrives, a dot shows on the **Help** tab until you open it.

## Good to know

- Help needs a connection.
- Your thread is yours alone: other reps in your agency don't see it.
- If the page reads **Help is switched off just now. Tell ThreadCloud.**, tell ThreadCloud directly.
