# Help

**Help** is where you ask how to do anything in the app. **Winston** answers from this manual. What he can't answer goes to the ThreadCloud team, and their answer comes back to the same thread.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-11-24</div>
If a button name here doesn't match what you see, the app changed and this page is stale. Tell ThreadCloud.
</div>

## Ask a question

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Tap your name at the top right to open your account, then tap <strong>Help</strong>. Its back link goes to <strong>Today</strong>.</li>
  <li>Type in <strong>Your question</strong>, or, on a new thread, tap one of the suggested questions under <strong>Try asking</strong>.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Ask Winston</strong>. The button reads <strong>Asking Winston…</strong> while he thinks.</li>
</ol>

The suggestions fit how you work: **How do I read a line sheet?**, **How does the buyer confirm an order?**, **What works with no signal?**, and a fourth by your role: **When does commission show up?** for an agent, **How do I set my prices to stores?** for a distributor, or **Where do I see what is booked?** for a brand selling in house. The box reads **How do I send the buyer a new version?** as an example.

Winston knows which page you came from, so "how do I add this?" works. He names the screens and buttons as the app shows them, in bold.

## When Winston doesn't know

If the manual doesn't cover your question, Winston sends it to the ThreadCloud team. The thread then reads **Sent to the ThreadCloud team. The answer shows here.** With several open, it reads **2 questions are with the ThreadCloud team. Answers show here.**

You don't need to wait on the page. When the answer arrives it appears in the thread under **ThreadCloud**, and a dot shows on the **Account** button until you open Help. The dot reads **New answer from ThreadCloud** to a screen reader.

## Good to know

- Help needs a connection.
- Your thread is yours alone: others in your agency don't see it.
- If the page reads **Help is switched off just now. Tell ThreadCloud.**, tell ThreadCloud directly.
