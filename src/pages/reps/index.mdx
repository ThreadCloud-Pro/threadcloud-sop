# TC Wholesale

TC Wholesale is a phone app for independent sales agents, distributors and brands that sell to stores. You keep each brand's seasons and their styles, see your visits, write orders in the store, and send the buyer the order to confirm. When the brand's confirmation, ship notice and invoice come in, they go on the order, and your commission follows.

The same app fits three ways of working, and the pages say where they differ:

- **An agent** sells a brand for commission. The brand receives the order and invoices the store.
- **A distributor** holds the brand and sells it to stores at your own prices. You are the supplier, and you invoice the store.
- **A brand selling in house** sells its own styles. Nothing goes to a brand, because you are the brand.

An agent can also work for a supplier: a distributor, importer or agency that holds the brand. Then the order goes to that supplier and the supplier invoices the store (see [Orders go to, and who invoices the store](/reps/lines-and-seasons/#orders-go-to-and-who-invoices-the-store)).

## The pages in this section

- **[Brands and seasons](/reps/lines-and-seasons/)**: your brands, each season's styles, reading a line sheet, season dates, and checking styles
- **[Stores and visits](/reps/doors-and-visits/)**: your stores, your buyers there, and your visit diary
- **[Territory, claims and store naming](/reps/territory-and-claims/)**: say where you sell a brand, and ask stores to name you

## Start your agency

If you are setting up on your own, you start from the **Set up TC Wholesale** page instead of an invite link. While this is being tested, you need a **sign-up code** from ThreadCloud. An agent working alone can use their own name as the agency name.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the sign-up page</strong> ThreadCloud gave you on your phone.</li>
  <li><strong>Step 1 of 2.</strong> The page reads <strong>Two steps. Everything else can wait.</strong> Type the <strong>Sign-up code</strong> (the box is not there when your link already carried it) and the <strong>Agency or brand name</strong>. The hint reads <strong>Or your own name, if you sell alone.</strong> A <strong>Website (optional)</strong> is behind <strong>More details</strong>, and later is fine. Press <strong>Next</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Step 2 of 2.</strong> Type <strong>Your name</strong> and <strong>Your email</strong>. The hint reads <strong>You sign in with it. No password.</strong> Press <strong>Set up</strong>, or <strong>Back</strong> to change step 1.</li>
  <li>You are signed in as the owner and land on <strong>Brands</strong>, ready to add your first brand.</li>
</ol>

- A wrong code reads **That sign-up code is not right. Check it and try again.** The page goes back to the step that holds it. Your other answers stay in the form. The code does not, so type it again.
- If the page says Not Found, sign-up is switched off. Ask ThreadCloud.
- If you already have an account, tap **Sign in instead** under the form.
- A colleague joins with an invite link, as below. Add one under **Add a colleague** (see [Your agency](#your-agency)).

## Sign in

There is no password. The sign-in page reads **No password. We email you a link.**

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open TC Wholesale</strong> and type your <strong>Email</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Press Email me a link.</strong> The page reads <strong>Check your email</strong> and <strong>We sent a link to</strong> your address. <strong>Send again</strong> is there if it does not arrive.</li>
  <li><strong>Open the link on your phone.</strong> The page reads <strong>Sign in as</strong> your name, with <strong>Stays signed in 90 days</strong>. Press <strong>Sign in</strong>. You land on <strong>Today</strong>.</li>
</ol>

- The emailed link works **once**, for **an hour**. When it is used or old, ask for another from the sign-in page.
- The phone stays signed in for **90 days**. After that, sign in again.
- **A first sign-in from an invite.** A new colleague, or a phone with no email box on the sign-in page, uses an invite link instead (see [Your agency](#your-agency)).
- An invite link works **once** and expires after **a week**. If it has been used or has expired, the page reads **This link no longer works**: ask ThreadCloud for a new one.
- Each phone signs in with its own link. To use a second phone, ask for a second link.
- **When the sign-in page has no Email box:** it reads **Open your invite link**. Sign in with the link you were sent, or ask your agency owner for a new one (see [Your agency](#your-agency)). A bad or used invite link shows **Sign in**, which goes to the same page.
- **A brand that sends its line sheet and orders to its stores** signs in from the **Brand sign-in** row on the sign-in page (**For brands that work with an agency**). It asks for **Use the email your stores have for you.** and emails a link that works once, for 15 minutes. **Agency sign-in** takes you back. **Set up TC Wholesale** (**New here**) is on the same page when sign-up is open.
- Opening the link doesn't use it up. Only pressing **Sign in** does, so a message app that previews the link won't spend it.

## Put it on your home screen

The app runs in your phone's browser, and it works best from your home screen, where it opens full screen like any other app.

- **iPhone:** open the app in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the app in Chrome, open the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The icon is named **TC Wholesale**.

## Finding your way around

Four tabs run along the bottom:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Your visits today and the rest of this week, what is coming up, and **Earnings** once there is a figure to show. |
| **Orders** | Every order, and where each one stands. |
| **Brands** | The brands you sell, and each brand's seasons. A season's styles count as Brands. |
| **Stores** | The stores you sell to. A store's orders and visits count as Stores. |

The band at the top reads **TC Wholesale**, with your account button at the right (see [Your account](#your-account)). A dot shows on it when a Help answer is waiting for you. **Earnings**, **Your agency** and **Help** all open from Today and light the **Today** tab.

**On a phone** each page is one column. **On a wider screen** the list stays on the left and the open item sits beside it: the orders with an order open, the brands with a brand, the stores with a store. With nothing open the right side reads **Pick an order**, **Pick a brand** or **Pick a store**. Each page keeps **one main button**, and it sits at the bottom of the page.

Every page has a back link at the top left, above the heading. The tab pages (Today, Orders, Brands, Stores) have none.

## Your account

Tap the account button at the top right. The **Account** sheet opens with a group called **You**, which states your own details as rows: **Name**, **Email**, **Phone** and **Title**. Tap a row and **that detail** opens in its own sheet, with just its field and **Save**. A check shows on the row you saved. Your email is how you sign in, so it is stated and can't be changed here.

A detail you have not filled in is not a blank row. It becomes one **Add** row of its own, like **Add phone** or **Add title**, and each opens its own sheet, so one never waits on another. There is no **Edit details** form.

Under **You**:

- **Your agency** opens the agency page (see [Your agency](#your-agency)). Its line names the agency, and for an owner or admin adds how many of its details are missing, like **3 details missing**.
- **Download all your data** is there for an owner or admin (see [Your agency](#your-agency)).
- **Help** opens Winston (see [Help](/reps/help/)). A dot shows when an answer is waiting.
- **Sign out** signs you out.

In your **Phone** sheet, **Show my phone to stores** is a switch (**The stores you work with can see it**). It is off. Turn it on only if you want the stores you work with to see your number (see [What stores see of your agency](#what-stores-see-of-your-agency)).

Phone numbers with ten or eleven digits show as **+1 416 825 2668**. The number you typed is kept as you typed it.

## Today

- **Start here** shows on a new account: **Add your brands**, then **Add your stores**. Each goes once it is done.
- The title reads **Today** with the date under it, like **Tuesday, Nov 24**.
- **Write an order** is the one main button, at the bottom of the page. It opens **Stores**, because an order starts from a store, and shows once you have a brand and a store.
- **Coming up** lists the order deadlines and delivery dates from all your seasons, soonest first, with the date on the right. A row is titled with the brand and season in words, like **Emanuel Berg, Spring 2027**, and reads **Orders due**, **Delivery 1 ships** or **Delivery 1 cancel date**. An order deadline that passed in the last week still shows, marked so you notice it, while its season is open. Tap a row to open that season.
- **Visits today** and **Visits this week** list your planned visits, soonest first. Tap one to open the store.
- **Earnings** opens the Earnings page (see [Earnings](/reps/earnings/)). Its row shows what applies to you, like **$1,944 coming** (agent), **$3,100 owed to you** (distributor) or **$24,300 booked** (brand selling in house). It shows only once a figure is above zero: an order booked, commission recorded, or a store owing you. Having a commission rate alone does not show it.

## Your agency

Press the **Account** button, then **Your agency**. It is your business on TC Wholesale, even when it is only you.

- **Agency details** is a group of rows, stated like your own details: **Name**, **Website**, **Reply email**, **Phone** and **Address**. An owner or admin taps one to change it in its own sheet, and a missing one is an **Add** row, like **Add reply email** or **Add website**. A Member does not see this group.
- **Alone, it is short.** You see **Add a colleague** (**Just you for now**) and **Download all your data** (owner or admin). The **People** list with roles, and **Whole agency** on Earnings, appear once a second person exists.
- **Add a colleague** (owner or admin): fill in **Name**, **Email** and **Role** (**Member** or **Admin**), and press **Add and get link**. The app then shows a sign-in link once, in a box you can't edit. Press **Share** and send it from your own phone, or press **Copy** and paste the link into a message: ThreadCloud does not send it for you. The link works once and lasts 7 days. The page reads **Works once and lasts 7 days. Shown only now.**
- **A Member** sells, and sees the list and nothing else. **An Admin** manages people and terms like the owner.
- **Someone lost their link or phone?** Press **New link for** their name. It shows a fresh link the same way.
- **Download all your data.** An owner or admin gets one spreadsheet with a sheet each for brands, seasons, styles, stores (including removed ones), store contacts, visits, orders, order rows and commissions. Store contacts and visits hold only your own, because each person's buyers, notes and visits are private to them. A Member does not see this row.
- **Reply email.** An owner or admin sets the agency's **Reply email** on the agency page, in **Agency details**. It is where buyers' answers and copies go (**Where buyers' answers and copies go**), and where a buyer's reply goes when ThreadCloud emails them an order from **Send to the buyer** (see [Send to the buyer](/reps/orders/#send-to-the-buyer)). With none set, replies go to your own sign-in email. It is not a brand's order address (see [Orders go to](/reps/lines-and-seasons/#orders-go-to-and-who-invoices-the-store)).

## With no signal

Showrooms and stockrooms often have no signal, so the app keeps working where it can.

- **Pages you've opened before** still open, from the copy saved on your phone.
- **A page you've never opened on this phone** needs a connection. Offline, it reads **You're offline** with a link back to Today.
- **Scan to order still reads tags.** The phone reads a barcode, and a printed style code it can match to the season's styles, with no signal (see [Scan to order](/reps/orders/#scan-to-order)). The first time, it needs a connection to get the readers ready, so open **Scan a tag** once before a day with no signal.
- **Picks on an order are kept on the phone.** Tick styles, type sizes, a price or a note as usual. The style card reads **Kept on this phone, sent when you're back online**, and they go up by themselves when the signal returns.
- **Everything else needs a connection:** sending the order to the buyer, reading a line sheet, adding photos, adding what the brand sent, adding stores, buyers or visits, checking styles, season dates, Earnings, and asking Winston. Offline, these say **You're offline. This needs a connection; try again when you're back online.**

A bar at the top of the page tells you what's happening:

| The bar reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Offline. Pages you have opened still work; picks are kept and sent later.** | You're offline and nothing is waiting. |
| **Offline. 3 changes kept on this phone, sent when you're back online.** | Picks are waiting on the phone. |
| **Sending 3 changes kept on this phone...** | The signal is back and they're going up. |
| **2 changes could not be saved: the order had changed. Open it to check.** | The order moved on while you were offline, for example a version was sent to the buyer from another phone. Open the order and check your picks. Press **OK** to close the bar. |

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Before a day with no signal</div>
Open each store and each order you'll need while you still have a connection. Then they're saved on the phone.
</div>

## Sign out

Press the **Account** button, then **Sign out**. The phone signs out at once and clears the pages and any picks it was keeping, so send waiting picks first. To sign in again, ask for an emailed link from the sign-in page, or use a new invite link.

## What the buyer sees, and what stays yours

The buyer only ever sees the version you send them and the page its link opens: the styles you picked, their prices, sizes and quantities, your note on each style, the totals, and who will invoice them.

The buyer never sees the rest of the season's styles, retail prices, the reader's checks, your commission, your cost or margin, what the brand sent, the order's status, or your private notes on buyers.

A distributor's cost beside the store price shows to an owner or admin only, never to a Member, and never to a store or a brand.

Inside your agency, brands, seasons, stores and orders are shared. Your buyers' details and private notes, and your visit diary, are yours alone. On Earnings you see your own orders; an owner or admin sees the whole agency's.

## What stores see of your agency

A store that has named your agency can see your agency's details in its own ThreadCloud (TC Retail), and can copy them into its own contacts. It sees the agency details you set: its name, website, phone, **Reply email** and address, and the name and email of the people in your agency who are the buyer's contact there. It sees a person's phone only if that person ticked **Show my phone to stores**. It never sees your title, your role or your commission.

The store decides what to copy, and ThreadCloud never changes the store's contacts for it. A store that has not named you sees nothing.

## If something is wrong

- **The app shows Not Found everywhere.** The app is switched off for your agency. Tell ThreadCloud.
- **"This link no longer works."** Ask ThreadCloud for a new invite link.
- **Anything else.** Ask Winston under **Help** in your account, or tell ThreadCloud what you pressed and what the screen said.
