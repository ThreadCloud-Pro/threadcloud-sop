# TC Wholesale

TC Wholesale is a phone app for independent sales agents, distributors and brands that sell to stores. You keep each brand's seasons and their styles, see your visits, write orders in the store, and send the buyer the order to confirm. When the brand's confirmation, ship notice and invoice come in, they go on the order, and your commission follows.

The same app fits three ways of working, and the pages say where they differ:

- **An agent** sells a brand for commission. The brand receives the order and invoices the store.
- **A distributor** holds the brand and sells it to stores at your own prices. You are the supplier, and you invoice the store.
- **A brand selling in house** sells its own styles. Nothing goes to a brand, because you are the brand.

An agent can also work for a supplier: a distributor, importer or agency that holds the brand. Then the order goes to that supplier and the supplier invoices the store (see [Orders go to, and who invoices the store](/reps/lines-and-seasons/#orders-go-to-and-who-invoices-the-store)).

## Start your agency

If you are setting up on your own, you start from the **Set up TC Wholesale** page instead of an invite link. While this is being tested, you need a **sign-up code** from ThreadCloud. An agent working alone can use their own name as the agency name.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the sign-up page</strong> ThreadCloud gave you on your phone.</li>
  <li><strong>Fill in the form:</strong> <strong>Sign-up code</strong>, <strong>Agency or brand name</strong> (the hint reads <strong>Or your own name</strong>), <strong>Website</strong> (optional, like youragency.com), <strong>Your name</strong> and <strong>Email</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Press Set up.</strong> You are signed in as the owner and land on <strong>Brands</strong>, ready to add your first brand.</li>
</ol>

- A wrong code reads **That sign-up code is not right. Check it and try again.** Your other answers stay in the form. The code does not, so type it again.
- If the page says Not Found, sign-up is switched off. Ask ThreadCloud.
- If you already have an account, press **Sign in instead**.
- A colleague joins with an invite link, as below. Add one under **Add a colleague** (see [Your agency](#your-agency)).

## Sign in

You don't make an account or a password. ThreadCloud sends you an invite link by text or email.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the invite link on your phone.</strong> The page reads <strong>Sign in as</strong> your name.</li>
  <li><strong>Press Sign in.</strong> You land on <strong>Today</strong>.</li>
</ol>

- The phone stays signed in for **90 days**. After that, sign in again.
- An invite link works **once** and expires after **a week**. If it has been used or has expired, the page reads **This link no longer works**: ask ThreadCloud for a new one.
- Each phone signs in with its own link. To use a second phone, ask for a second link.
- **On a new phone, or when your link is used up:** if the sign-in page shows an **Email** box, type your email and press **Email me a link**. The link works once, for an hour. If the page only says **Open your invite link**, ask your agency owner for a new link (see [Your agency](#your-agency)). A bad or used invite link shows **Sign in**, which goes to the same page.
- **A brand that sends its line sheet and orders to its stores** signs in from **Brand sign-in** on the sign-in page. **Agency sign-in** takes you back.
- Opening the link doesn't use it up. Only pressing **Sign in** does, so a message app that previews the link won't spend it.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Treat your link and your phone like a key</div>
Anyone who presses Sign in on your link, or picks up your signed-in phone, sees your agency's brands, seasons and orders. Don't forward the link. If a phone is lost, tell ThreadCloud and they will switch its access off.

## Put it on your home screen

The app runs in your phone's browser, and it works best from your home screen, where it opens full screen like any other app.

- **iPhone:** open the app in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the app in Chrome, open the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The icon is named **TC Wholesale**.

## Finding your way around

Four tabs run along the bottom:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Your visits today and the rest of this week, what is coming up, and **Earnings** once there is a figure to show. |
| **Orders** | Every order, and where each one stands. |
| **Brands** | The brands you sell, and each brand's seasons. A season's styles count as Brands. |
| **Stores** | The stores you sell to. A store's orders and visits count as Stores. |

The **Account** button at the top opens a sheet with your details, **Your agency**, **Download all your data** and **Help**. A dot shows on it when a Help answer is waiting for you. **Earnings**, **Your agency** and **Help** all open from Today and light the **Today** tab.

Every page has a back link at the top left, above the heading. The tab pages (Today, Orders, Brands, Stores) have none.

## Today

- **Start here** shows on a new account: **Add your brands**, then **Add your stores**. Each goes once it is done.
- **Write an order** at the top opens **Stores**, because choosing the store is the first step. It shows once you have a brand and a store.
- **Coming up** lists the order deadlines and delivery dates from all your seasons, soonest first, with the date on the right. A row is titled with the brand and season in words, like **Emanuel Berg, Spring 2027**, and reads **Orders due**, **Delivery 1 ships** or **Delivery 1 cancel date**. An order deadline that passed in the last week still shows, marked so you notice it, while its season is open. Tap a row to open that season.
- **Visits today** and **Visits this week** list your planned visits, soonest first. Tap one to open the store.
- **Earnings** opens the Earnings page (see [Earnings](/reps/earnings/)). Its row shows what applies to you, like **$1,944 coming** (agent), **$3,100 owed to you** (distributor) or **$24,300 booked** (brand selling in house). It shows only once a figure is above zero: an order booked, commission recorded, or a store owing you. Having a commission rate alone does not show it.

## Your agency

Press the **Account** button, then **Your agency**. It is your business on TC Wholesale, even when it is only you.

- **Alone, it is short.** You see your agency's details, **Download all your data** (owner or admin) and one plain row, **Add a colleague**. The people list with roles, and **Whole agency** on Earnings, appear once a second person exists.
- **Add a colleague** (owner or admin): fill in **Name**, **Email** and **Role** (**Member** or **Admin**), and press **Add and get link**. The app then shows a sign-in link once. Press **Share** and send it from your own phone: ThreadCloud does not send it for you. The link works once and lasts 7 days. The page reads **Works once and lasts 7 days. Shown only now.**
- **A Member** sells, and sees the list and nothing else. **An Admin** manages people and terms like the owner.
- **Someone lost their link or phone?** Press **New link for** their name. It shows a fresh link the same way.
- **Download all your data.** An owner or admin gets one spreadsheet with a sheet each for brands, seasons, styles, stores (including removed ones), store contacts, visits, orders, order rows and commissions. Store contacts and visits hold only your own, because each person's buyers, notes and visits are private to them. A Member does not see this row.
- **Reply email.** In **Edit details**, an owner or admin sets the agency's **Reply email**, where buyers' answers and copies go. It is not a brand's order address (see [Orders go to](/reps/lines-and-seasons/#orders-go-to-and-who-invoices-the-store)).

## With no signal

Showrooms and stockrooms often have no signal, so the app keeps working where it can.

- **Pages you've opened before** still open, from the copy saved on your phone.
- **A page you've never opened on this phone** needs a connection. Offline, it reads **You're offline** with a link back to Today.
- **Picks on an order are kept on the phone.** Tick styles, type sizes, a price or a note as usual. The style card reads **Kept on this phone, sent when you're back online**, and they go up by themselves when the signal returns.
- **Everything else needs a connection:** sending the order to the buyer, reading a line sheet, adding photos, adding what the brand sent, adding stores, buyers or visits, checking styles, season dates, Earnings, and asking Winston. Offline, these say **You're offline. This needs a connection; try again when you're back online.**

A bar at the top of the page tells you what's happening:

| The bar reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Offline. Pages you have opened still work; picks are kept and sent later.** | You're offline and nothing is waiting. |
| **Offline. 3 changes kept on this phone, sent when you're back online.** | Picks are waiting on the phone. |
| **Sending 3 changes kept on this phone...** | The signal is back and they're going up. |
| **2 changes could not be saved: the order had changed. Open it to check.** | The order moved on while you were offline, for example a version was sent to the buyer from another phone. Open the order and check your picks. Press **OK** to close the bar. |

<div class="callout-title">Before a day with no signal</div>
Open each store and each order you'll need while you still have a connection. Then they're saved on the phone.
</div>

## Sign out

Press the **Account** button, then **Sign out**. The phone signs out at once and clears the pages and any picks it was keeping, so send waiting picks first. To sign in again, you need a new invite link.

## What the buyer sees, and what stays yours

The buyer only ever sees the version you send them and the page its link opens: the styles you picked, their prices, sizes and quantities, your note on each style, the totals, and who will invoice them.

The buyer never sees the rest of the season's styles, retail prices, the reader's checks, your commission, your cost or margin, what the brand sent, the order's status, or your private notes on buyers.

A distributor's cost beside the store price shows to an owner or admin only, never to a Member, and never to a store or a brand.

Inside your agency, brands, seasons, stores and orders are shared. Your buyers' details and private notes, and your visit diary, are yours alone. On Earnings you see your own orders; an owner or admin sees the whole agency's.

## If something is wrong

- **The app shows Not Found everywhere.** The app is switched off for your agency. Tell ThreadCloud.
- **"This link no longer works."** Ask ThreadCloud for a new invite link.
- **Anything else.** Ask Winston on the **Help** tab, or tell ThreadCloud what you pressed and what the screen said.
