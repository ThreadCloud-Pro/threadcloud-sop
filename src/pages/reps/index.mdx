# TC Wholesale

TC Wholesale is a phone app for independent sales reps and agencies. You keep each brand's book for the season, see your visits, write orders in the store, and send the buyer a recap to confirm. When the brand's confirmation, ship notice and invoice come in, they go on the order, and your commission follows.

Everything on these pages is also what Winston answers from: in TC Wholesale, open your account, tap **Help** and ask.

## The pages in this section

- **[Lines and seasons](/reps/lines-and-seasons/)**: your brands, each season's book, reading a linesheet, season dates, and checking styles
- **[Doors and visits](/reps/doors-and-visits/)**: your stores, your buyers there, and your visit diary
- **[Territory, claims and store confirmation](/reps/territory-and-claims/)**: say where you sell a brand, and ask stores to confirm you
- **[Writing an order](/reps/orders/)**: picks in the store, the recap the buyer confirms, and the brand's paper
- **[Earnings](/reps/earnings/)**: the commission you can expect on what shipped
- **[Help](/reps/help/)**: ask Winston how to do anything in the app

## Start your agency

If you are setting up a new agency, you start from the **Create your agency** page instead of an invite link. While this is being tested, you need a **sign-up code** from ThreadCloud.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the sign-up page</strong> ThreadCloud gave you on your phone.</li>
  <li><strong>Fill in the form:</strong> <strong>Sign-up code</strong>, <strong>Agency name</strong>, <strong>Agency website</strong> (optional, like youragency.com), <strong>Your name</strong> and <strong>Your email</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Press Create agency.</strong> You are signed in as the agency owner and land on <strong>Lines</strong>, ready to add your first brand.</li>
</ol>

- A wrong code reads **That sign-up code is not right. Check it and try again.** Your other answers stay in the form. The code does not, so type it again.
- If the page says Not Found, sign-up is switched off. Ask ThreadCloud.
- Others on your team join with an invite link, as below.

## Sign in

You don't make an account or a password. ThreadCloud sends you an invite link by text or email.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the invite link on your phone.</strong> The page reads <strong>Sign in as</strong> your name.</li>
  <li><strong>Press Sign in.</strong> You land on <strong>Today</strong>.</li>
</ol>

- The phone stays signed in for **90 days**. After that, sign in again.
- An invite link works **once** and expires after **a week**. If it has been used or has expired, the page reads **This link no longer works**: ask ThreadCloud for a new one.
- Each phone signs in with its own link. To use a second phone, ask for a second link.
- **On a new phone, or when your link is used up:** if the sign-in page shows an **Email** box, type your email and press **Email me a link**. The link works once, for an hour. If the page only says **Open your invite link**, ask your agency owner for a new link (see [Your agency](#your-agency)).
- Opening the link doesn't use it up. Only pressing **Sign in** does, so a message app that previews the link won't spend it.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Treat your link and your phone like a key</div>
Anyone who presses Sign in on your link, or picks up your signed-in phone, sees your agency's books and orders. Don't forward the link. If a phone is lost, tell ThreadCloud and they will switch its access off.
</div>

## Put it on your home screen

The app runs in your phone's browser, and it works best from your home screen, where it opens full screen like any other app.

- **iPhone:** open the app in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the app in Chrome, open the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The icon is named **TC Wholesale**.

## Finding your way around

The band at the top reads **TC Wholesale**, with your account button at the right. Five tabs run along the bottom:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Today** | What is due, your visits today and the rest of this week, and a way to start an order. Tap a visit to open that store. |
| **Orders** | All your orders, with their status. |
| **Lines** | The brands you represent, and each brand's seasons. A season's book counts as Lines. |
| **Stores** | The stores you sell to. |
| **Earnings** | Commission expected on orders that shipped. |

Every page has a back link at the top left, above the heading. Help is not a tab: it is in your account (see below).

## Your account

Tap the account button at the top right. The **Account** sheet shows two groups, **You** and **Agency**, with your email, phone and title, and the agency's website, phone, order email and address. A detail you have not filled in is left out, and one row says how many are missing, like **3 details missing · Add**. Tap it to fill them in.

Under the details:

- **Edit details** opens a form. Anyone can change their own name, phone and title. Your email is how you sign in, so it can't be changed here. An owner or admin can also change the agency's name, website, phone, order email and address. A rep sees the agency's details but can't change them.
- **Show my phone to stores I work with** is a tick box under your phone. It is off. Tick it only if you want the stores you work with to see your number (see [What stores see of your agency](#what-stores-see-of-your-agency)).
- **Your agency** opens the people in your agency.
- **Export** is there for an owner or admin. It downloads the same spreadsheet as **Download all your data** (see [Your agency](#your-agency)).
- **Help** opens Winston (see [Help](/reps/help/)). A dot shows when an answer is waiting.
- **Sign out** signs you out.

Phone numbers with ten or eleven digits show as **+1 416 825 2668**. The number you typed is kept as you typed it.

## Today

- **Write an order** is the one main button. It takes you to **Stores**, because an order starts from a store. **Book a visit** sits beside it as a lesser button.
- **Coming up** comes first. It lists the order deadlines and delivery dates from all your seasons, soonest first, with the date on the right. A row reads **Orders due**, or the delivery window's code followed by **starts shipping** or **cancel**. An order deadline that passed in the last week still shows, marked so you notice it, while its season is open. Tap a row to open that season.
- **Start here** shows on a new agency: **Add the brands you carry**, then **Add the stores you sell to**. Each goes once it is done.
- **Today** and **This week** list your booked visits, soonest first.
- **Orders** opens all your orders (see [Writing an order](/reps/orders/#all-your-orders)).
- Your agency's people are under **Your agency** in your account.

## Your agency

Open your account and press **Your agency** to see the people in your agency and each one's role.

- **A rep** sees the list and nothing else.
- **An owner or admin** can press **Add someone**, fill in **Name**, **Email** and **Role** (**Rep** or **Admin**), and press **Add and get link**. The app then shows a sign-in link once, in a box you can't edit. Press **Share** and send it from your own phone, or press **Copy** to copy the link and paste it into a message: ThreadCloud does not send it for you. The link works once and lasts 7 days.
- **Someone lost their link or phone?** Press **New link for** their name. It shows a fresh link the same way, to send from your own phone.
- **Download all your data.** An owner or admin also sees **Download all your data** under **Your data**. It gives you one spreadsheet with a sheet each for brands, seasons, styles, stores (including removed ones), store contacts, visits, orders, order lines and commissions. Store contacts and visits hold only your own, because each person's buyers, notes and visits are private to them. A rep does not see this row.

## With no signal

Showrooms and stockrooms often have no signal, so the app keeps working where it can.

- **Pages you've opened before** still open, from the copy saved on your phone.
- **A page you've never opened on this phone** needs a connection. Offline, it reads **You're offline** with a link back to Today.
- **Scan to order still reads tags.** The phone reads a barcode, and a printed style code it can match to the book, with no signal (see [Scan to order](/reps/orders/#scan-to-order)). The first time, it needs a connection to get the readers ready, so open **Scan a tag** once before a day with no signal.
- **Picks on an order are kept on the phone.** Tick styles, type sizes, a price or a note as usual. The style card reads **Kept on this phone, sent when you're back online**, and they go up by themselves when the signal returns.
- **Everything else needs a connection:** sending the recap, reading a linesheet, adding photos, adding the brand's paper, adding stores, buyers or visits, checking styles, season dates, Earnings, and asking Winston. Offline, these say **You're offline. This needs a connection; try again when you're back online.**

A bar at the top of the page tells you what's happening:

| The bar reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Offline. Pages you have opened still work; picks are kept and sent later.** | You're offline and nothing is waiting. |
| **Offline. 3 changes kept on this phone, sent when you're back online.** | Picks are waiting on the phone. |
| **Sending 3 changes kept on this phone...** | The signal is back and they're going up. |
| **2 changes could not be saved: the order had changed. Open it to check.** | The order moved on while you were offline, for example a recap was sent from another phone. Open the order and check your picks. Press **OK** to close the bar. |

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Before a day with no signal</div>
Open each store and each order you'll need while you still have a connection. Then they're saved on the phone.
</div>

## Sign out

Open your account and press **Sign out**. The phone signs out at once and clears the pages and any picks it was keeping, so send waiting picks first. To sign in again, you need a new invite link.

## What the buyer sees, and what stays yours

The buyer only ever sees the recap you send them and the page its link opens: the styles you picked, their prices, sizes and quantities, your note on each line, and the totals.

The buyer never sees the rest of the book, retail prices, the reader's checks, your commission, the brand's paper, the order's status, or your private notes on buyers.

Inside your agency, lines, seasons, books, stores and orders are shared. Your buyers' details and private notes, and your visit diary, are yours alone. On Earnings you see your own orders; an agency owner sees the whole agency's.

## What stores see of your agency

A store that has confirmed it works with your agency can see your agency's details in its own ThreadCloud, and can copy them into its own contacts. It sees your agency's name, website, phone, order email and address, and the name and email of the people in your agency who are the buyer's contact there. It sees a person's phone only if that person ticked **Show my phone to stores I work with**. It never sees your title, your role or your commission.

The store decides what to copy, and ThreadCloud never changes the store's contacts for it. A store that has not confirmed you sees nothing.

## If something is wrong

- **The app shows Not Found everywhere.** The app is switched off for your agency. Tell ThreadCloud.
- **"This link no longer works."** Ask ThreadCloud for a new invite link.
- **Anything else.** Ask Winston under **Help** in your account, or tell ThreadCloud what you pressed and what the screen said.
