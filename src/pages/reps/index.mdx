# ThreadCloud for reps

ThreadCloud for reps is a phone app for independent sales reps and agencies. You keep each brand's book for the season, see your visits, write orders in the store, and send the buyer a recap to confirm. When the brand's confirmation, ship notice and invoice come in, they go on the order, and your commission follows.

## The pages in this section

- **[Lines and seasons](/reps/lines-and-seasons/)**: your brands, each season's book, reading a linesheet, season dates, and checking styles
- **[Doors and visits](/reps/doors-and-visits/)**: your stores, your buyers there, and your visit diary
- **[Writing an order](/reps/orders/)**: picks in the store, the recap the buyer confirms, and the brand's paper
- **[Earnings](/reps/earnings/)**: the commission you can expect on what shipped

## Sign in

You don't make an account or a password. ThreadCloud sends you an invite link by text or email.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the invite link on your phone.</strong> The page reads <strong>Sign in as</strong> your name.</li>
  <li><strong>Press Sign in.</strong> You land on <strong>Today</strong>.</li>
</ol>

- The phone stays signed in for **90 days**. After that, ask for a new link.
- An invite link works **once** and expires after **a week**. If it has been used or has expired, the page reads **This link no longer works**: ask ThreadCloud for a new one.
- Each phone signs in with its own link. To use a second phone, ask for a second link.
- Opening the link doesn't use it up. Only pressing **Sign in** does, so a message app that previews the link won't spend it.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Treat your link and your phone like a key</div>
Anyone who presses Sign in on your link, or picks up your signed-in phone, sees your agency's books and orders. Don't forward the link. If a phone is lost, tell ThreadCloud and they will switch its access off.
</div>

## Put it on your home screen

The app runs in your phone's browser, and it works best from your home screen, where it opens full screen like any other app.

- **iPhone:** open the app in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the app in Chrome, open the menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The icon is named **ThreadCloud**.

## Finding your way around

Four tabs run along the bottom:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Your visits today and the rest of this week. Tap a visit to open that store. |
| **Lines** | The brands you represent, and each brand's seasons. A season's book and its orders count as Lines. |
| **Doors** | The stores you sell to. A store's orders count as Doors. |
| **Earnings** | Commission expected on orders that shipped. |

Every page has a back link at the top left, above the heading.

## With no signal

Showrooms and stockrooms often have no signal, so the app keeps working where it can.

- **Pages you've opened before** still open, from the copy saved on your phone.
- **A page you've never opened on this phone** needs a connection. Offline, it reads **You're offline** with a link back to Today.
- **Picks on an order are kept on the phone.** Tick styles, type sizes, a price or a note as usual. The style card reads **Kept on this phone, sent when you're back online**, and they go up by themselves when the signal returns.
- **Everything else needs a connection:** sending the recap, reading a linesheet, adding the brand's paper, adding stores, buyers or visits, checking styles, season dates, and Earnings. Offline, these say **You're offline. This needs a connection; try again when you're back online.**

A bar at the top of the page tells you what's happening:

| The bar reads | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Offline. Pages you have opened still work; picks are kept and sent later.** | You're offline and nothing is waiting. |
| **Offline. 3 changes kept on this phone, sent when you're back online.** | Picks are waiting on the phone. |
| **Sending 3 changes kept on this phone...** | The signal is back and they're going up. |
| **2 changes could not be saved: the order had changed. Open it to check.** | The order moved on while you were offline, for example a recap was sent from another phone. Open the order and check your picks. Press **OK** to close the bar. |

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Before a day with no signal</div>
Open each store and each order you'll need while you still have a connection. Then they're saved on the phone.
</div>

## Sign out

At the bottom of **Today**, press **Sign out** and your name. The phone signs out at once and clears the pages and any picks it was keeping, so send waiting picks first. To sign in again, you need a new invite link.

## What the buyer sees, and what stays yours

The buyer only ever sees the recap you send them and the page its link opens: the styles you picked, their prices, sizes and quantities, your note on each line, and the totals.

The buyer never sees the rest of the book, retail prices, the reader's checks, your commission, the brand's paper, the order's status, or your private notes on buyers.

Inside your agency, lines, seasons, books, stores and orders are shared. Your buyers' details and private notes, and your visit diary, are yours alone. On Earnings you see your own orders; an agency owner sees the whole agency's.

## If something is wrong

- **The app shows Not Found everywhere.** The app is switched off for your agency. Tell ThreadCloud.
- **"This link no longer works."** Ask ThreadCloud for a new invite link.
- **Anything else.** Tell ThreadCloud what you pressed and what the screen said.
