# Reorder

Reorder is how you restock something you already carry. You find the product, set how many you want in each size, issue the purchase order, settle what the supplier says they can send, and receive it when it lands. It is a separate order from the original buy, so your numbers stay clean.

## Quick path

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open Reorders</strong> from the <strong>Reorder</strong> card on the home screen's Sales floor desk, and click <strong>New reorder</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Find the product</strong>: search for one you carry, or click <strong>Run</strong> on a saved list. ThreadCloud reads the cost from Shopify as you pick.</li>
  <li><strong>Set how many, by size</strong> on the <strong>Pick</strong> tab. Quantities save on their own.</li>
  <li><strong>Issue the PO</strong>: click <strong>Review PO</strong>, then <strong>Issue PO</strong>. Or click <strong>Receive without sending</strong> to skip the supplier step.</li>
  <li><strong>Settle the supplier's answer</strong> on the <strong>Supplier answer</strong> tab.</li>
  <li><strong>Receive</strong> the box on the <strong>Receive</strong> tab when it arrives.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Where "what should I reorder?" lives</div>
The reorder screens are for placing the restock. To decide <em>what</em> to restock (what's selling, what's running low) use <a href="/planning/trading/">Merchandising</a>. It surfaces reorder candidates from sell-through and weeks-of-supply, and links straight into a reorder.
</div>

## The Reorders list

On the home screen, switch to the **Sales floor** desk and click the <strong>Reorder</strong> card ("One style or a full store restock"). You can also type "reorder" in the home search. Both open **Reorders**, the list of every reorder the store has.

Across the top: <strong>New reorder</strong> to start one, <strong>Restock lists</strong> for your saved lists, and a search box.

The tabs sort the list by where each reorder stands. Each tab except Received shows a count.

| Tab | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Everything not fully received |
| **Waiting on supplier** | Sent, and the supplier hasn't answered |
| **Short** | The supplier confirmed fewer than you ordered, or a box arrived short, and nobody has decided what to do yet |
| **Shipped** | The supplier says it shipped, or part of it has arrived |
| **Received** | Every unit you expect has arrived |

Each row shows the **PO number**, **Brand**, **Styles**, **Units**, **For customer** (anyone a piece is earmarked for), **Sent**, **Status** and **Supplier no.** (the supplier's own order number, or **Add** when there isn't one yet). Click a row to open it. A draft opens on **Pick**; a sent reorder opens on its **Supplier answer**.

Reorder PO numbers carry the brand's code, the year and a running number, for example `ABC-RO-26-014`.

### Status

Every reorder carries one status chip, the most useful fact first:

| Chip | Means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Not sent yet |
| **Waiting on supplier** | Sent, no answer yet |
| **Confirmed** | The supplier answered and nothing is short |
| **Short** | Fewer confirmed than ordered, or a box arrived short. It needs your decision. |
| **Substitute offered** | The supplier offered something else for a style |
| **Backordered** | The supplier gave a later date for a style |
| **Shipped** | The supplier says it shipped |
| **Part received** | Some of it has arrived |
| **Received** | Everything you expect has arrived |

### Search

Type in the search box ("PO number, supplier order number, brand, style or customer"). It finds a reorder by our PO number, the supplier's order number (including any other number they quoted on an answer), the brand, a style code or name, or the customer a piece is earmarked for. Dashes and spaces don't matter: `ABC-RO-26-014` and `abcro26014` find the same reorder. The line above the results says how many matched and by what, for example "2 reorders match by PO number and style". **Clear** empties the box.

### Can't find it

When a box or an email arrives with no number you recognise, click <strong>Find by what's in the box</strong> under the list (it also opens below any search). Fill in what you can read off the box:

- **Brand** (or **Any brand**)
- **Style code**, from the label
- **Quantity**: how many pieces are in the box

Click <strong>Find</strong>. ThreadCloud ranks the reorders that are sent and not fully received, marking each **Most likely**, **Possible** or **Unlikely** with a reason, for example "Has JK11 Navy jacket. 4 still to come." Click <strong>This is it</strong> on the right one. "No open reorder fits." means nothing open matches.

## Start a new reorder

Click <strong>New reorder</strong>. The page is a search, split into two panels:

- <strong>Products</strong> (left): search your Shopify catalog. Type at least 2 letters. Each result shows a thumbnail, brand, colour, stock count, and dots for how complete the product data is. Click a product to start a reorder.
- <strong>Saved lists</strong> (right): your saved restock plans. <strong>Recent</strong> shows the last few; <strong>Browse by brand</strong> groups the rest. Click <strong>Run</strong> on any list to start a reorder pre-filled with those products and sizes.

A reorder is locked to one brand and one currency. If you try to add a product from a different brand, the app says so and blocks it. Run a second reorder for the second brand.

## Where the cost comes from

A new reorder takes its cost from Shopify, not from what you paid last time. When you pick a product, ThreadCloud reads the product's cost from Shopify right then. Three things can stop it:

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "Could not verify Shopify cost. Try again." | Shopify did not answer, or answered with an incomplete cost read. Nothing was created. | Wait a moment and pick the product again. |
| "Variant costs differ in Shopify. Review them before reordering." | The sizes of this product carry different costs in Shopify. ThreadCloud will not guess which one is right. | Fix the cost on the variants in Shopify so they agree, then pick again. |
| "This product is no longer available in Shopify." | Shopify returned nothing for it (deleted, draft, or not visible to the app). | Make the product active in Shopify, or pick a different one. |

ThreadCloud refuses to open a reorder on an incomplete cost read. It will not fall back to a stale number.

### A product with no cost on file

If Shopify has no cost for the product and ThreadCloud has no record of buying it, the picked card reads <strong>Source cost needed</strong> and asks for the wholesale cost and its currency. Type the <strong>Unit cost</strong>, check the currency box (three letters, e.g. EUR), then click <strong>Open reorder</strong>. <strong>Back to search</strong> cancels. A currency code the app does not recognize is refused with "Not a recognized currency code".

If you try to add such a product from inside the workspace instead, the message reads: "has no supplier cost on file, and ThreadCloud has no record of buying it. Set its cost on the product in Shopify, or receive it through ThreadCloud once, then add it here."

### Costs on a saved list

When you <strong>Run</strong> a saved list, ThreadCloud checks every product's Shopify cost before creating anything. Products it cannot include are listed on the card as "N products left out" with the reason:

- no supplier cost: reorder each on its own from the search, where you confirm the cost as you go;
- not returned by Shopify: make the product active again, or take it off the list;
- bought in another currency: reorder them on their own, they get their own PO;
- variant costs differ in Shopify: fix them in Shopify first.

If the whole list is excluded, nothing is created and the card says so.

### "Could not fill every missing Shopify cost"

Where a product has a cost in ThreadCloud but none in Shopify, ThreadCloud tries to write that cost back to Shopify when the reorder opens. If it could not, the workspace shows: "ThreadCloud could not fill every missing Shopify cost. The reorder was still created." The reorder is fine. Set the cost on the product in Shopify when you get a chance so the next reorder reads cleanly.

## The reorder's header and tabs

Every reorder page shares one header. It shows the brand and the PO number, a status chip, and a short line such as "Sent Sep 24 · 2 of 8 received" (or "PO not sent"). Under it, four tabs walk the reorder from start to finish. A tick marks a finished step.

| Tab | What it's for | Beside the name |
|---|---|---|
| <strong>Pick</strong> | Choose products and quantities | How many styles |
| <strong>PO</strong> | Review and issue the purchase order | **Sent** or **Not sent** |
| <strong>Supplier answer</strong> | What the supplier can send, and what to do about it | **Waiting**, or the status |
| <strong>Receive</strong> | Count the box in | Units received of units expected, for example "2 of 8" |

## Pick: set quantities

Each product shows a row of size cells. Type how many you want in each size.

- <strong>Quantities save on their own</strong> about a second after you stop typing. There is no Save button. Watch for the small <em>Saving…</em> then <em>Saved</em> note.
- Each size cell shows a <strong>stock badge</strong> pulled live from Shopify every time you open the page, not a cached count. A size that is not in Shopify yet reads "Stock unknown".
- If you started from a saved list, the cell also shows the plan snapshot next to the live count, e.g. <em>plan 20 · have 15</em>. <em>plan</em> is what you had in mind when you saved the list; <em>have</em> is what's on hand right now.
- Trousers and other two-axis sizes (waist × inseam) lay out as a grid, not a single row.

### A size the product doesn't carry yet

Click <strong>+ Size</strong> on the product to add a size Shopify has never had, for example a 44R on a jacket that runs 46 and up. Type it the way the product's other sizes are spelled (on a two-axis product, fill both boxes) and click <strong>Add</strong>. ThreadCloud creates the size in Shopify, copying the price and cost from the nearest size, and the new cell appears at 0 for you to fill in. If someone already added that size in Shopify admin, it links the existing one instead of making a duplicate.

Add sizes before the PO goes out. A size added after the PO is issued needs the PO re-issued for the supplier to see it.

### The product line

Under each product name is one line of references, in this order where each exists: brand, category, <strong>ARTICLE</strong>, <strong>REF</strong> (the supplier's style reference), <strong>MODEL</strong> (model name and code), colour, and <strong>COLOUR CODE</strong>. Hover the line for a reminder: "Supplier style ref. Verify this is the same product". Where no supplier reference is on file, the line falls back to the store SKU.

These references are carried through creation and receiving. They come from the Shopify product's full metafield set, so a product whose references live past the first page of metafields still shows them, and they print on the PO and the receiving document.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Fixing an old product's references</div>
If the line shows the wrong article, model, or colour code (common on products enriched before ThreadCloud), click <strong>Edit product details</strong> on that product. It opens the product in Clean Catalog with its <strong>Vendor Ref</strong>, <strong>Article</strong>, <strong>Model code</strong>, <strong>Model name</strong>, and <strong>Colour code</strong> fields. Edit, click <strong>Save product details</strong>, then <strong>Back to reorder</strong>. The saved values are written to the Shopify product, so they hold on every later reorder. Viewers do not see this button. If it reads "Product details could not be opened" or "could not be saved", try once more; if it persists, the runbook has the next step.
</div>

### Cost per line

Each product line has a cost box. It is the base cost per unit for this reorder, feeds the PO and your margins, and does not change the cost on file in Shopify. Once a line has been received its cost is frozen at the receipt; changing it after that records a cost correction, and the box asks for a reason and an optional supporting document reference.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Earmark for a waiting customer</div>
If a customer is waiting on a size, click <strong>+ customer</strong> on that cell (<strong>+ another</strong> once one is there), then type their name or pick a Shopify customer. The earmark guarantees that size is on the order; the quantity bumps up to cover it. When the box arrives, the Receive page shows "For [name] · [size]" under that product with a <strong>Tell</strong> link, so you know who to call.
</div>

Other things on the Pick tab:

- **The settings row** above the products: **Discount** (a percent off the whole order), **Cancel after** (an optional date the supplier shouldn't ship past) and **Supplier order no.** (the supplier's own number, once they give you one). Each line also has its own **% off**; per-line discounts grey out when an order discount is set, and the two never stack.
- **Add more products**: the search at the bottom ("Add another [brand] product") adds more products from the same brand. The same Shopify cost check runs on each add. "Already in this reorder." means it is a duplicate. A product bought in another currency is refused with both currencies named: one reorder holds one currency, so start a separate reorder for it.
- **The More menu** (the **…** button in the header):
  - <strong>Copy PO number</strong>, once the reorder has one. A note reads "PO number copied"; if the clipboard is blocked it reads "Copy unavailable" with the number beside it to select.
  - <strong>Save as plan</strong>: save these products and sizes as a reusable restock list. Next season, click <strong>Run</strong> and it's all there. <strong>View plans</strong> opens your lists.
  - <strong>Edit plan</strong>, on a reorder that came from a plan: changes the plan itself.
  - <strong>Delete reorder</strong>: removes the reorder and any earmarks on it. ThreadCloud asks you to confirm.

The dark bar at the bottom shows the units and the total. Before the PO is issued it has two buttons, both greyed out until at least one size has a quantity ("Set at least one size quantity"):

- <strong>Review PO</strong>: the normal path. Opens the PO tab.
- <strong>Receive without sending</strong>: issues the PO without contacting the supplier and takes you straight to Receive. Use this when you already placed the order by phone.

Once the PO is issued, the bar offers <strong>Purchase order</strong> and <strong>Receive</strong> instead.

## PO: issue the purchase order

The PO tab shows the purchase order for the supplier: styles, sizes, quantities, and the supplier's contact. Issuing freezes the document. After that, edits on Pick do not rewrite what the supplier was sent.

- <strong>Issue PO</strong>: the button in the dark bar at the bottom. It reads <strong>Issuing</strong> while it works, then takes you to the Receive tab.
- <strong>Share</strong> (in the header) holds the document's actions:
  - <strong>Email [brand]</strong>: copies a ready-made email and opens Gmail; paste it with Ctrl+V. If no brand contact is on file, it opens Gmail with the link and you add the address.
  - <strong>Copy PO</strong>: copies the purchase order document itself.
  - <strong>Copy supplier answer link</strong>: copies the link the supplier uses to say what they can send. It reads <strong>Link copied</strong>.
  - <strong>Print or PDF</strong>.

Once issued, the bar offers <strong>Send to supplier</strong> (the same email as <strong>Email [brand]</strong>) and <strong>Receive</strong>.

If the PO cannot be issued, a box titled <strong>Purchase order not issued</strong> names the reason. The ones you will meet:

- "There is nothing to send. Complete at least one product before issuing this purchase order."
- "This purchase order has already been sent. Open an amendment to revise and re-issue it."
- "This purchase order cannot be revised because receiving has started."
- "This purchase order was issued by another request. Reload the page to see the current version." (someone else issued it at the same time; reload).

If the PO shows "N ordered lines cannot be committed", those products are missing a fact the supplier needs. Click <strong>Edit reorder</strong> to fill it in.

If you open an amendment on an issued reorder, the bar offers <strong>Re-issue PO</strong>. Re-issuing supersedes the link already in the supplier's inbox; the old link tells them to ask you for the current one.

## Supplier answer: what they can send

When the supplier answers on their link, or you log their confirmation (see <a href="/workflows/supplier-confirmations/">Supplier confirmations</a>), the **Supplier answer** tab shows it line by line.

The headline says where it stands, for example:

- "Waiting on [brand]." Nothing back yet.
- "[brand] confirmed 6 of 8. 2 short." Fewer than you ordered, and it needs your decision.
- "Accepted 6 of 8. 2 dropped." You accepted what they can send.
- "[brand] confirmed all 8."

Under it, a bar and a key count what is **confirmed**, **short**, **dropped** and **received**, and a ship date shows when the supplier gave one.

The table lists each style with **Ordered**, **Confirmed** ("Not yet" until they answer), **Received**, and what the supplier said about it ("No note" when nothing). A style shows "For [name]" when a customer is earmarked on it.

**Supplier order number**: type the supplier's own number for this order and click <strong>Save</strong>. It shows on the Reorders list and search finds it. If the supplier quoted a different number on an answer, the line under the box says so.

### Accept what they can send

When the supplier has answered every line and some sizes are short, the dark bar at the bottom reads, for example, "6 of 8 confirmed. The 2 short come off this PO." Click <strong>Accept 6, drop 2</strong> (the numbers are yours).

- The drop is recorded on the reorder. Nothing is resent to the supplier, and the PO they hold doesn't change.
- Receiving then expects the accepted quantity, not the original one.
- A substitute the supplier offered is never accepted this way. That stays your call.
- If the supplier hasn't answered every line yet, you can't accept. The bar offers <strong>Ask [brand] again</strong>, which opens a Gmail message with their link.

If a dropped size had a customer earmarked on it, that earmark is released and a list titled **Tell them it isn't coming** appears, one customer per row with a <strong>Tell [name]</strong> link. The style also reads "Not coming for [name]".

The row of links under the table holds the rest:

- <strong>Email [brand]</strong>: opens a Gmail message to the supplier with the PO number.
- <strong>Tell [name]</strong> for each customer still waiting on a piece.
- <strong>Undo the drop</strong>: puts the dropped units back on the reorder and restores the earmarks. Only while nothing has been received.
- <strong>Send revised PO</strong>: optional. Sends the supplier a new PO with the accepted quantities. ThreadCloud asks you to confirm, because the link they already hold stops working. Only before anything is received.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">How Tell and Email open</div>
Tell, Email and Ask again open a new Gmail tab with the message started. ThreadCloud sends nothing itself. A customer with only a phone number opens your phone app instead. A linked Shopify customer opens their card with their current phone and email.
</div>

The header's <strong>Purchase order</strong> button opens the PO. The **…** menu has <strong>Edit quantities</strong> (back to Pick) and <strong>Review supplier answer</strong> (the full review screen, see <a href="/workflows/supplier-confirmations/">Supplier confirmations</a>).

## Receive: check it in

A reorder receives on the same Receive page as a buying order, with the reorder's own header and tabs on top. Reversing a shipment, correcting a receipt, the receipt history and printing tags all work there exactly as described on <a href="/workflows/receiving/">Receiving</a>. Open it from the **Receive** tab, the <strong>Receive</strong> button in the dark bar, or Home's **Receive reorders**.

Each product shows the same references you saw on Pick, so you can check the carton against the screen. Under a product with an earmark, a row reads "For [name] · [size]" with a <strong>Tell [name]</strong> link. Call them when their size lands.

A reorder in another currency needs its source currency before it can be received. If the page asks "Source currency EUR?", click <strong>Confirm EUR</strong> when that is right.

### When a reorder reads Received

A reorder reads **Received** once every unit you expect has arrived against it. Expected means what you ordered, less anything you accepted as dropped. A substitute the supplier sent instead does not count toward it: the ordered piece is still owed.

## If it goes wrong

- <strong>"belongs to a different brand"</strong>: a reorder is one brand only. Finish this one, then start another for the other brand.
- <strong>"Could not verify Shopify cost. Try again."</strong>: Shopify did not answer cleanly. Pick again in a moment. Nothing was created.
- <strong>"Variant costs differ in Shopify."</strong>: make the variant costs agree in Shopify, then pick again.
- <strong>Quantities didn't save</strong>: they save a second after you stop typing. If you see <em>Save failed</em>, check your connection and re-type the cell.
- <strong>"Nothing to accept until the supplier has answered every line."</strong>: wait for the rest of the answer, or ask them again.
- <strong>"Receiving has started. Accept and drop instead."</strong>: a revised PO can't go out once a box is in. Use <strong>Accept</strong> to drop what isn't coming.
- <strong>Stuck status, or anything else</strong>: see <a href="/runbook/">If something breaks</a>.
