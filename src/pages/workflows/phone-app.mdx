# Phone app

The phone app is the store's staff app for the phone: **Buy** by shooting photos into an order or a pre-owned lot, **Receive** a box, and **Sell** from the floor. It opens in the phone's browser and signs in as the person who approves it. It shows the design the person chose in **Settings → My preferences** (see [Settings and store health](/settings/store-health/#my-preferences)), so the same screen can look different from one person's phone to the next.

## Sign a phone in

There is no password. The phone shows a code, and you type it on the desk.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On the desk, open <strong>Settings → Phones</strong>. Under <strong>Add a phone</strong> the page shows the phone app's address as a link, with a QR code and a <strong>Copy link</strong> button.</li>
  <li>On the phone, open that address (scan the QR code, or send yourself the copied link). The phone shows <strong>Sign in</strong> and a code.</li>
  <li>On the desk, type the code in the <strong>Code</strong> box and click <strong>Sign it in</strong>. The page notes that it signs the phone in as you.</li>
  <li>The phone signs itself in and opens your first tab.</li>
</ol>

The code works once, for 10 minutes. If it runs out, tap **Get a new code** on the phone. **Try again in a minute.** means ThreadCloud is busy.

Under **Signed in**, the Phones page lists the phones already in, each with a **Sign out** button. With none it reads **No phones are signed in.** Every tab's front page also has a Sign out on the phone. Taking a person out of [Team access](/settings/users-roles/) signs their phones out.

## Which tabs you see

The tab bar follows the person's switches in [Team access](/settings/users-roles/):

| Tab | Shown with | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Buy** | Buy | Photos into an order. |
| **Receive** | Receive | Counting a box in. |
| **Sell** | Sell | Tags, clients and what has arrived. |

The phone opens on the person's own first tab. A screen the person's switches don't cover turns back to their first tab with a line naming the switch. Someone let in with none of Sell, Receive or Buy sees **Nothing here yet**, and **Ask the store owner to turn on Selling or Receiving for you in Settings › Team access.**

## Buy

**Buy** is the buyer's tab. It lists:

- **Shooting now**, with the order this phone shot into within the last two hours and a round shutter button that opens its camera.
- **New appointment**: pick a **Brand** (or **Assorted brands**, with **Find a brand** to search), check the currency (**Change** to edit it), pick a **Season** (or type one such as FW-26, or choose **No season**), then tap **Start shooting**. It makes a blank order to shoot into. A brand that isn't on the desk can't be found here: add it on the desk first.
- **Find an order**, a search box that reads "Brand, season or PO". Type and tap **Find** to search every open order, past the first few the list shows. It reads **No open order matches that.** when nothing fits.
- The store's open buying orders, most recently photographed first, each with a camera button.

In the classic design, **New appointment** and **New lot** are rows at the top of the list. In the new design a **+** button at the top (named **New**) opens a sheet with **Appointment** and **Lot**.

Open an order for its **Shoot** button and its photos, filtered with **Needs you**, with **Go through N** to place the photos that need a decision. The order's notes can be added from here too. If a photo can't send, it stays on the phone and the page offers **Retry** or **Discard**.

### What to shoot next

The camera names the next photo over the viewfinder. On a product (opened from the order), it asks for **Front**, **Back**, **Side** and **Detail**, shown as, for example, "Back · 2 of 4", starting after the photos the product already has. Once the four are done, tap **Add details** to be offered **Inside**, **Lining**, **Label** and **Buttons**. On a whole order, each piece starts with its hangtag, then the same four, and **Next piece** starts the next piece. You can tap any named shot to jump to it. Label mode (reading care labels) has no guide.

## Pre-owned lots

A lot is a batch of pre-owned pieces with no supplier document: something you bought, took in trade, hold on consignment, or keep as a sample. Like a new appointment, only a person with the **Buy** switch can start one; anyone else sees the same message as for an appointment.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On **Buy**, tap <strong>New lot</strong> (in the new design, the <strong>+</strong> button, then <strong>Lot</strong>).</li>
  <li>Under <strong>From</strong>, pick <strong>Bought</strong>, <strong>Trade-in</strong>, <strong>Consignment</strong> or <strong>Sample</strong>.</li>
  <li>For a consignment you must pick the client; for a trade-in the client is optional. Type a name or phone and tap <strong>Find</strong>, then pick the person. Only a reference to the Shopify customer is kept.</li>
  <li>For a consignment, check the <strong>Terms</strong>, which start from the store's defaults: <strong>Client's share in store credit, %</strong>, <strong>Client's share in cash, %</strong>, <strong>Days on the floor</strong>, <strong>Markdown on day</strong> and <strong>At the end</strong>. The markdown day can't come after the last day on the floor.</li>
  <li>Tap <strong>Start shooting</strong> (new design: <strong>Start lot</strong>). It opens the lot's camera.</li>
</ol>

The lot appears in the Buy list as "Lot, consignment" (or bought, trade-in, sample).

**Shoot each piece.** The lot's camera asks, for each piece, for **The item**, **Another angle**, **Label** and **Detail** (as many details as you like). The camera line reads, for example, "Piece 2 · Label". Tap a name to jump to it, **Skip label** if there is none, and **Next piece** once the item is shot. Every photo is tied to its piece and role on ThreadCloud's side, so two phones can shoot the same lot: a second phone carries on after the highest piece any phone has shot, instead of starting again at piece 1.

**Make the products.** Open the lot to see its pieces in the order shot, each with its photos. Photos that don't belong to a piece are listed under **Not in a piece** with the note "Shot on another phone or outside the guide. Shoot the piece again here to make it." Tap <strong>Make N products</strong> (new design: <strong>Make</strong>) and each piece is read in turn and made into a draft product. It shows what was read (brand, size, type, colour and era), or says it could not be read and offers <strong>Try again</strong>.

For each made piece:

- Pick its **Condition**: **New with tags**, **Excellent**, **Very good**, **Good** or **Fair**.
- Type the **Flaws** (up to 300 characters). It starts as "None seen".
- Type the **Price**. For a consignment the page shows the client's share of it. For anything else, type the **Cost** ("0 if nothing paid"). Save with **Save condition** and **Save price** (new design: **Save**).
- **Suggest price** searches comparable listings and shows a range. It is a suggestion, never a price: nothing is set until you type and save one.

A sample piece may carry a cost of zero and still be approved; every other piece needs a cost above zero.

**What reaches Shopify.** A made piece is a draft product that is approved and pushed like any other (see [Push to Shopify](/workflows/push-to-shopify/#pre-owned-lot-pieces)).

## Receive

**Receive** opens with a search box, **Find an order**, that reads "Brand, season or PO". Type and tap **Find**. It finds orders you can receive, any order that was sent, and any order that already has units received on it even if it was never marked sent, with the ones due first. A draft or an imported old season with nothing in stays out. It reads **No order matches that.** when nothing fits.

Without a search, the tab lists every order to receive, in two groups:

- **To receive**: orders due now, including ones partly received and ones past their window, and orders you receive at the desk without ever marking them sent.
- **Coming up**: orders whose window has not opened.

Each row shows the brand, the season, the delivery window and where the order stands, and the percentage already in; an order past its window is marked. With nothing due it reads **Nothing is arriving right now.**

Open an order (its title line carries the season, PO, the date it was sent, the window and the status). In the new design the count comes first: a compact header, then the list of products to count, with labels and photos as follow-ups that never block receiving. Each product row shows a thumbnail and what is still owed, and every product has a camera: from it you take a **Photo** or a **Label** shot. An order with nothing to count shows no empty count card, and a product is never listed twice. Count the box under **Count the box**, then tap the receive button at the bottom. The sheet shows the location the stock goes into (**Into** the store's location) and asks for a **Delivery note number** (leave it blank and ThreadCloud numbers it). On an order in another currency it also shows the exchange rate (only someone with Buy types it; for anyone else it shows today's rate and locks with the receipt). Tap **Receive** in the sheet to book it. The sheet then reads **Received N units** and **Tags print at the desk.**

- A box with more than the order expects asks **More than the order expects**, with **Receive anyway**.
- Products the phone can't count stay on the order, greyed out under the reason, so nothing drops off it:
  - **Not on Shopify yet** lists products with no Shopify product. Receiving never runs before the first push. The note reads "Push them in Enrich, then count them here." for a buyer, with <strong>Open Enrich</strong>, or "A buyer pushes them in Enrich, then you can count them here." for anyone else.
  - **Receive at the desk** lists products that are on Shopify but owed only on sizes the phone can't count; the desk's receive sets those up. <strong>Open in ThreadCloud</strong> opens the order on the desk.
  The links show only where the person's switches open that desk page.
- Extras beyond the order are received at the desk.

With the garment in hand, three more jobs can be done on the same order without leaving the phone:

- **Colour group.** A product whose colour group is still waiting for a look is listed with the supplier's colour words as printed on the tag (for example "COFFEE"). Tap it, check the **Colour group** that is preselected (what Enrich would pick) against the garment, and confirm it or choose another. Only the order's own groups are offered. A confirmed row leaves the list.
- **Measure one size.** Each product in the box that has no measurement yet is listed under **To measure**, with the size to pick out of the box. Tap it, enter the measurements and save. It is the same save as on the desk, and the row stays on screen as **Measured**, saying whether Shopify has it. When all are done it reads **Everything in the box is measured.**
- **Damaged or wrong item.** Tap **Damaged or wrong item** ("Tell the rep, then book it at the desk"). Pick the product and size, how many, **Damaged** or **Wrong item**, take a photo, and add a note (where the damage is, or what came instead). **Send** hands the photo and one line naming the unit to the phone's share sheet, so you can pick Mail or Messages. A phone that can't share a photo shows **Email** and **Text** buttons for the rep instead, and you add the photo from **Photos**. Nothing is saved in ThreadCloud and nothing goes to Shopify. A return needs the rep's OK first: the unit is counted in and set aside, and the desk books the return when the rep answers.

A care-label shot you took keeps its label mark while it waits to send.
- The same receipt is never booked twice. After a dropped connection, **Your last Receive needs an answer** checks it.

**Open in ThreadCloud** at the top of the order opens the same order on the desk, in Shopify admin. It shows only where the person's switches open that desk page.

**Nothing more is coming.** Once something has arrived on a buying order, a person with the **Buy** switch also sees a **Nothing more is coming** button on the order's page. It opens a sheet that says what it will do. **Mark N units not coming** closes off what the order still owes (it leaves **To receive** and **Coming up**, and what arrived stays received), and **Keep waiting** leaves it. If a customer had a hold on a unit that will not arrive, the phone names them so you can tell them. Everyone sees the standing close ("N units marked not coming on Nov 9."), and a buyer gets **Undo**. It is the same close as on the desk (see [Receiving](/workflows/receiving/#nothing-more-is-coming)), and it works on a reorder too.

Tags for phone receipts are printed at the desk: use **Print received tags** on the Receiving log. See [Print Labels](/workflows/labels/#print-received-tags).

## Going back

The phone's back links return to where you started, not to the front page:

- A photo or label shot started from **Receive** goes back to the order on Receive ("Back to the order"). **Retake the tag**, from **Needs you**, goes back to **Needs you**. A shot started on **Buy** goes back to the product or the order.
- A lot's photos page goes back to the lot ("Back to the lot") and shoots with the lot camera.
- **New appointment** and **New lot** have a back arrow to **Buy** ("Back to Buy").
- On the care-label page, the way out reads **Back to the order**.
- On **Sell**, a client's held piece opens its product, and its back link returns to that client ("Back to the client"). A product opened from a scan goes back to the scan.
- **Order notes** are a buyer's: only a person with the **Buy** switch is prompted to add notes on an order. Anyone else sees the notes once they are there.

## Sell

**Sell** has:

- **Scan a tag** (sizes on hand, coming and when). Its typed box reads **Or type a name or code**: a product name, style code or barcode, then **Find**. A name that fits several products lists them to pick from.
- **Find a product or client**: type a name, phone or style code and tap **Find**. Products you find open the product page; clients open the client. It reads **No product or client by that name, number or code.** when nothing fits.
- **Arrived for clients**: what has arrived for client orders, with a **Text** button to tell the client.

A product you look up shows its **Details** and its **Measurements**, with **Add measurements** (or **Update measurements** when it has some) to change them from the phone. It lists **On order**: each order bringing it in, with the units coming, when they arrive, the PO and the day it was sent. A client's held pieces and custom orders show their dates too. Each carries an **Open** or **Open in ThreadCloud** link to the order on the desk, only where the person's switches open that desk page.
