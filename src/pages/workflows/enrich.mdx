# Enrich

The second pass. After Parse & Build reads the order, Enrich fills in everything else: descriptions, fit notes, fabric details, country of origin, occasion tags, and the Shopify metafields behind smart filters. It is also where you approve each product and where you see, per product, whether Shopify really has it.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
On every order after parse, before push. The background pass usually runs on its own. You step in to review, approve, and clear anything the page says needs attention.
</div>

<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-01</div>

## When Enrich runs

Three triggers:

1. **Automatically** after Parse & Build. A background pass starts once you click <strong>Enrich for web</strong> on the parsed order.
2. **Whole order.** Click <strong>Update All</strong> at the top of the Enrich tab. It reads your documents and notes into every product that can take them. The button is greyed out when no AI provider is set up or nothing on the order can be filled.
3. **One product.** Open the product and click <strong>Generate</strong> (no copy yet) or <strong>Regenerate</strong> (copy exists). This redoes the photo read, fields, title, and description together for that product.

You shouldn't need to fire it by hand for normal orders.

## What Enrich actually does

For every style on the order, AI fills in:

### Customer-facing
- **Web description** (the long product description on the storefront)
- **Fit notes**: how the garment runs (slim / regular / relaxed) and sizing advice
- **Occasion**: when to wear it (Business / Casual / Resort / Formal)

### Structured metafields (smart filters read these)
- **Category + Subcategory**: the canonical TC taxonomy
- **Colour Group**: the canonical colour bucket (Black, Navy, Brown, etc.) for filter facets
- **Pattern**: Solid / Stripe / Check / etc.
- **Fabric content**: primary fibre + composition
- **Country of origin**: Italy, Japan, Portugal, etc.
- **Fit type**: Slim, Regular, Classic, Relaxed
- **Construction attributes** (for tailoring): lapel, button stance, lining, vents, canvas type, etc.

### Identity
- **Vendor Ref**: supplier's internal style code (separate from the Shopify SKU)
- **Brand Code**: short brand prefix, three letters
- **Model**: the brand's model or fit the product is cut on (see [Brand model and measurements](#brand-model-and-measurements))

## The Enrich screen

Open the order and click the <strong>Enrich</strong> tab. Top to bottom:

1. **The order header**, shared with every tab of the order (see <a href="/workflows/build-order/#the-order-header">Parse & Build</a>). On Enrich it also carries the **Enrich settings** control: **Product title guidance**, **Brand voice**, **Description order** and **Never use** (words the copy must never use). These govern every generate and regenerate on the store.
2. **Product details** heading with three buttons: <strong>Documents &amp; notes</strong> (opens the context panel), a Shopify button, and <strong>Update All</strong>.
3. **A one-line status** that only appears when something needs you. It names the most urgent item and gives one button for it. It reads, in priority order: a product detail error, then "N products need Shopify verification", then a review item, then "Sending changes to Shopify" while a push runs, then "Shopify sync needs attention. Changes saved here have not updated Shopify." if a push did not land.
4. **The filter bar**: <strong>All products</strong> / <strong>To review</strong> switch on the left, a search box ("Search name, style or colour"), a <strong>Filters</strong> button, and the <strong>Tiles</strong> / <strong>List</strong> view switch.
5. **Select all** checkbox and the bulk action bar (appears once something is selected).
6. **The worklist**: every pending and approved product in one list, pending first.
7. **Skipped**: a collapsed section at the bottom for products you skipped. Click <strong>Show</strong> to open it.

If the order was re-submitted from Build and some products fell off it, a line under the list says "N products no longer included in this order" with a <strong>Review in Parse &amp; Build</strong> button.

### The Shopify button

The second button at the top changes with the order's real state:

| Reads | Means |
|---|---|
| <strong>Send to Shopify (N)</strong> | N products have something Shopify has not received: never created, edited since the last push, or a size not yet linked. Click to open the push confirmation (see <a href="/workflows/push-to-shopify/">Push to Shopify</a>). |
| <strong>Shopify needs attention</strong> | Nothing to send, but at least one product is "Pushed but unverified" (see below). |
| <strong>Shopify up to date</strong> | Every product on the order is "Pushed and verified". |
| <strong>No Shopify changes</strong> | Nothing to send and nothing verified yet. |

The button is greyed out while a push is running.

## All products vs To review

- <strong>All products</strong> shows every pending and approved product.
- <strong>To review</strong> narrows to the products that still need a decision: every pending product, plus any product the engine flagged after its last run. The count on the switch is that number.

An approved product with a fresh engine flag shows an <strong>Accept review</strong> button in its footer. Click it to accept the current result and clear the flag.

## Filters

Click <strong>Filters</strong>. The button shows a count, e.g. "Filters (2)", when any are active. Three dropdowns:

| Filter | Choices |
|---|---|
| Approval | All products, Needs attention, Approved, Skipped in review |
| Missing detail | Any detail, Title, Description, then every enrich field the order's pack uses |
| Shopify | All products, Needs attention, Needs verification, Not created, Changes to send, Up to date |

"Skipped in review" is a product you skipped on this screen. It is separate from a product left out of a re-submitted Build. They used to share one bucket; they no longer do.

Under the dropdowns:

- <strong>Clear filters</strong>
- <strong>See all missing details</strong>: lists each missing field with a count, e.g. "Fabric content · 12". Click one to filter the list to just those products.
- <strong>Import barcodes</strong>: appears once at least one product has been pushed.

Drag-to-reorder is off while any filter or search is active.

## Tiles vs List

The <strong>Tiles</strong> / <strong>List</strong> switch is remembered per user. Both views open the same editor when you click a product; only the collapsed presentation changes.

- **Tiles**: a grid. Each tile carries a status chip: <strong>Ready to review</strong>, <strong>Incomplete</strong>, <strong>Approved</strong>, or <strong>Skipped</strong>. "Ready to review" means the product would pass the Approve check (title, cost, MSRP, at least one ordered size). "Incomplete" means Approve would refuse it.
- **List**: a compact row per product with a drag grip, the supplier reference, and a field completeness bar.

If two products on the order read identically (same codes), both are marked as twins so you can tell them apart before approving.

### Reading the colours

Each product shows the details it needs as pills, in reading order: what it is, colour, material, origin, fit, construction, care, when to wear it, then the operational ones last. A detail its category doesn't need isn't shown. The colours mean the same thing on the tile, in the editor and in the count:

| Colour | Means |
|---|---|
| **Red** | A required detail that is empty, or filled with a value that isn't on its list |
| **Amber** | An optional detail filled with a value that isn't on its list |
| **Green** | A value that's accepted, whoever filled it in |
| **No colour** | An optional detail left blank |

The tile's count reads, for example, **3 details missing** (click it for the list) or **Details complete**. The count is advice only. It never stops a push: you decide.

Once a product is in Shopify and nothing is waiting to go, its Shopify line shows a check mark before **In Shopify · Up to date**.

### Adding photos

In a product's Photos, you can pick up to 5 photos at a time. They upload one after another, and the line then reads, for example, "Added 5." If you picked more, it adds the first 5 and says "Pick the rest in another batch." A photo that couldn't be added is named.

## Reviewing a product

Click a product to open its editor in place. Walk down the fields top to bottom:

| Field | What to check |
|---|---|
| Web Description | Reads well? No "elevate your wardrobe" cliches? Correct fabric mentioned? |
| Fit Type | Matches what you know about the brand's fit |
| Occasion | At least one selected, more if applicable |
| Fabric content | Matches the linesheet (this is the most common AI miss) |
| Country of Origin | Only "prestige" origins get mentioned in description (Italy, Japan, etc.) |
| Category/Subcategory | Should be correct from parse. Verify. |

Fields save as you edit them. If a save fails, the editor shows "Your change could not be saved." with a <strong>Retry saves</strong> button. While a product is saving, or has a failed save, the page will not let you open another product or leave the tab: you get <strong>Stay and finish</strong> or <strong>Leave without saving</strong>.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Speed tip</div>
Products with no flags and a "Ready to review" chip can usually be approved in one click. Spend your attention on the ones in <strong>To review</strong>.
</div>

## Approve

Each product's footer has the actions for its state:

| State | Buttons |
|---|---|
| Pending | <strong>Skip</strong>, <strong>Approve</strong> |
| Approved | <strong>Accept review</strong> (only when flagged), <strong>Unapprove</strong>, then <strong>Push to Shopify</strong> (not created yet: creates just this product, as a draft) or <strong>Push update</strong> (already in Shopify: sends your edits and keeps its status) |
| Skipped | <strong>Restore</strong> |

Every state also has <strong>Flag</strong>, which marks the product for attention. It then reads <strong>Flagged</strong>.

### What Approve checks

Approve refuses only on the four facts a product cannot ship without: a product title, a unit cost, a retail price, and at least one size with a quantity. If one is missing, the app names it.

Everything else is advisory. If the engine could not settle the product behind your approval, the product is still approved. A line appears under the button starting with "Approved." followed by the actual reason and what clears it. The product is approved by you, not settled by the engine, and the line says so. It does not ask you to reload.

The same applies to <strong>Approve all ready</strong>: the products are approved, and any engine advisory shows once above the list.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Approval and push are separate gates</div>
Approving a product does not push it. A product approved over an advisory still has to clear the push gate before it reaches Shopify. See <a href="/workflows/push-to-shopify/">Push to Shopify</a>.
</div>

## Bulk actions

Tick the checkbox on any products (or <strong>Select all</strong>). The bar shows:

- <strong>Apply edit</strong>: set one field across the selection.
- <strong>Approve N</strong>
- <strong>Push selected</strong>: creates the selected products that aren't in Shopify yet and re-syncs the ones that are. See <a href="/workflows/push-to-shopify/#push-selected">Push to Shopify</a>.
- <strong>More</strong> menu: <strong>Approve all ready (N)</strong> (only when some pending products pass the Approve check), <strong>Generate showroom flatlays</strong>, <strong>Skip</strong>, <strong>Clear selection</strong>.

Every bulk action is greyed out while another bulk action is running, and while a product editor is mid-save or holding a failed save. Finish or retry the save first.

## Shopify state per product

Under each product's footer there is one line for what Shopify actually has. There are exactly three states:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <strong>Pushed and verified</strong> | Shopify itself confirmed a linked inventory item for every ordered size. This is the only state that counts as pushed. |
| <strong>Pushed but unverified</strong> | Shopify has the product, but at least one ordered size is not linked to a variant. The line names the sizes and says to push the style again to link them. A product with no ordered sizes also reads this way: "This product exists in Shopify. Add sizes before receiving." |
| <strong>Not pushed</strong> | "This product has not been created in Shopify yet." |

### Verify with Shopify

If a previous push did not record a product identity, the line reads "Not pushed" with the reason "A previous push did not record a product identity, so pushing again is fenced." and offers a <strong>Verify with Shopify</strong> button.

Verify asks Shopify what it has for this style and links what it finds. It never creates a product, so it is safe on a row whose identity is in doubt. When it finds one, the line reads "Found this style's existing Shopify product by SKU and linked it. No product was created." (or "by handle"). If it finds nothing, the error shows under the button.

The top-of-page status line also routes you here: "N products need Shopify verification" with a <strong>Verify product</strong> button (one product) or <strong>Review products (N)</strong> (several).

### What "Pushed" means for receiving

Receiving books against the variant links. A product that is "Pushed but unverified" cannot be received against its unlinked sizes. Clear it in Enrich first.

## Collection context

Click <strong>Documents &amp; notes</strong> to open the context panel. The <strong>Collection context</strong> box is free text that gets woven into every product description on the next generate (put title and copy directions here too). Type your notes, then click <strong>Save context</strong> (or press <strong>Cmd/Ctrl+Enter</strong>). The button stays disabled until you've changed something, the label shows saving / unsaved / saved, and a <strong>Discard</strong> button restores the last saved text. Saving is an explicit click, not a background timer, so you decide when it's committed; anything you type while a save is in flight is never lost.

### Model context documents

The context panel accepts <strong>PDFs</strong> as well as photos, so you can drop a supplier's per-model fit guide (for example a trouser maker's sheet listing each of its fits) straight in. There is no attach-to-model step; just drop it. When a document names a <strong>model</strong> and a <strong>fit</strong> but carries no article or colour code, the Fit Type auto-fills on <strong>every colourway of that model</strong> (fit is colour-independent). It is gap-fill only, so it won't overwrite a fit you set by hand, and a bare model name with no facts attached is ignored.

## Editing the description specifically

The AI description follows the brand-voice rules in [Settings → AI & Models](/settings/ai-models/). To change one:

1. Open the product and edit the description text directly.
2. Or click <strong>Generate</strong> (no copy yet) or <strong>Regenerate</strong> (copy exists) to redo the photo read, fields, title, and description together.

If a regenerate could not write copy, the product says so in one line: what happened and what to do next, for example "No copy came back for this product, so its description is still empty. Add a line to Notable details or buyer notes and regenerate this product." If the copy saved here but Shopify did not confirm it, the line tells you to push the product again.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Heads up</div>
If you edit a description manually and then change the fabric or COO later, the description does <strong>not</strong> auto-update. It's locked to your manual edit. To re-link it, click Regenerate.
</div>

## Fields that depend on the product

Some fields only appear when the product's category makes them relevant. Tailored jackets and suits show construction details such as lapel, button stance, vents, construction and lining. Dress shirts show collar and cuff. Trousers show rise. Knitwear and polos show **Knit weight** (Lightweight or Heavyweight: does it fit under a blazer?). Footwear shows **Formality** (Dress, Smart Casual or Casual).

If a field doesn't appear, the app decided it isn't relevant for this product's category. You can't force it visible from the screen. If the category itself is wrong, fix the category first and the right fields appear.

## Brand model and measurements

Open a product and use the **Model** field to pick the brand model it is cut on (<strong>Pick a model</strong>). Models come from the brand's record in the Brand Registry. A model that isn't confirmed yet shows **Unconfirmed**.

Once a product has a model, its measurements show the brand's own spec beside them, at the same size, so you can see at a glance if a measurement is off. Measurements saved here go to Shopify on the next push, and feed the size chart on the product page.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Approving cards with empty fabric</div>
If fabric is empty when you approve, the product pushes to Shopify with no fabric metafield. Smart filter "shop by fabric" misses these products. <strong>Always fill fabric before approving.</strong> Use Filters → Missing detail → Fabric content to find them.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Wrong COO going public</div>
Country of origin shows on the storefront for prestige origins (Italy, Japan, etc.). If parse extracted "Tunisia" but the linesheet actually says "Italy", fix it before approving. Hard to undo after products are live.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Editing the description after push</div>
If you push to Shopify and then edit the description in Enrich, you need to push again to see the change on the storefront. The Shopify button will read <strong>Send to Shopify (N)</strong> until you do.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Treating "Pushed but unverified" as done</div>
The product exists in Shopify but a size is not linked. Receiving cannot book that size. Push the product again (or Verify with Shopify when offered) until it reads "Pushed and verified".
</div>
