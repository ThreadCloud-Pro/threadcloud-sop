# Print Labels

ThreadCloud prints a **hang tag** for every unit you receive. You print from the Receive page right after you receive a box, and you can reprint any time: for a whole shipment, for one product on an order, or for any product in the store from **Logistics**.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-11-09</div>
Which fields print is set by the Owner under <strong>Settings</strong>, so your tag may differ from the description below.
</div>

## The printer

The default is a **Dymo** label printer with **30336** labels, **54 × 25 mm**. A store on Zebra thermal stock can choose the **51 × 25 mm** size instead (see below). Every print opens in a new browser tab and sends itself to the print dialog.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Start a print from ThreadCloud (see below). A new tab opens with the tags, one per unit.</li>
  <li>In the print dialog, pick the Dymo and check the label size is 30336 (54 × 25 mm).</li>
  <li>Set the scale to <strong>100%</strong> or <strong>Actual size</strong>, never "Fit to page".</li>
  <li>Print.</li>
</ol>

## What's on the hang tag

- A **stock-type badge** (**NOS**, **STOCK**, **MTO** or **CUSTOM**) and the **season code**
- **Brand**, **style name** and **price**
- **Size**, with the scale it's in, and **colour**
- The **barcode**, with the **SKU** and the supplier's reference printed under it, side by side. Handy for checking a garment against the paper order without scanning.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Change what's on the tag</div>
Someone with the <strong>Store settings</strong> switch (and the Owner) chooses which fields print in <strong>Settings → Receiving</strong>, on the <strong>Tag printing</strong> tab, under <strong>Receiving label design</strong>: brand name, style name, colour, SKU, price (MSRP) and the barcode. A preview shows the tag as you change it. Click <strong>Save</strong>. Anyone else sees who can change it.
</div>

### Tag size

On the same <strong>Tag printing</strong> tab, <strong>Tag size</strong> ("Match the label stock in the printer") offers <strong>54 x 25 mm (Dymo 30336)</strong>, the default, and <strong>51 x 25 mm (Zebra)</strong>. Pick the one that matches the label stock, then <strong>Save</strong>. The preview and every tag you print afterwards use it. In the print dialog, match the paper size to the one you picked.

## Print when you receive

When you receive a box, a window titled "N units received" opens. Choose <strong>Print both</strong> (tags, then the receiving document), <strong>Tags only</strong>, <strong>Doc only</strong>, or <strong>Skip for now</strong>. Receive 5 of size L and you get 5 tags for size L. The panel on the page also keeps a <strong>Print N tags</strong> button, and a finished order's close-out has a <strong>Tags</strong> tile for the last box. See <a href="/workflows/receiving/">Receiving</a>.

## Print received tags

Boxes received on the phone are counted there, but their tags print at the desk. On <strong>Logistics → Receiving Log</strong>, the <strong>Print received tags</strong> button (top right) shows a count of receipts from the last two weeks whose tags have not printed. Phone receipts are included.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click <strong>Print received tags</strong>. The window lists each receipt with its brand, delivery note and date, and its tag count. Tick the ones to print.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Print N tags</strong> (up to 50 receipts per print). The tags open in a new tab.</li>
  <li>Back on the page, <strong>Did the tags print?</strong> asks. Click <strong>Yes, mark printed</strong> and those receipts leave the list. Click <strong>Not yet</strong> and they stay, so a cancelled print is not lost.</li>
</ol>

<strong>Mark printed</strong> in the first window clears the ticked receipts without printing, for tags you printed another way. A receipt's own full print of tags (<strong>Tags</strong> on the Received tab, or <strong>Reprint Labels</strong> on the receipt) also takes it off the list. The button is greyed out when nothing is waiting.

## Reprint later

| You need | Where |
|---|---|
| All the tags from one shipment | **Logistics**, <strong>Reprint Tags</strong>. It opens the Receiving log on its <strong>Received</strong> tab. Find the shipment by brand, order or doc ref and click <strong>Tags</strong> (or <strong>Doc</strong> for the receiving document). Or open the receipt and click <strong>Reprint Labels</strong>. |
| The tags for one product on an order | The order's **Receive** page: click <strong>Reprint tags</strong> on that product's card. |
| One tag for any product in the store | **Logistics**, <strong>Reprint a product label</strong>. See below. |

### Reprint a product label

Use this when a tag falls off, gets damaged, or a product was never tagged, including products ThreadCloud didn't buy.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Logistics</strong> from the menu.</li>
  <li>In <strong>Reprint a product label</strong>, scan the barcode or type a name, SKU, brand or supplier reference ("Scan the barcode or search products").</li>
  <li>Use the arrow keys to pick the right result, or click it. A scanner's Enter searches without printing.</li>
  <li>Set <strong>Qty</strong> (1 to 100) and click <strong>PRINT</strong>.</li>
</ol>

A product ThreadCloud bought prints exactly like its original tag, with season, stock badge and supplier reference. A product from before ThreadCloud prints what Shopify knows about it. If the browser blocks the print window, click <strong>OPEN LABEL</strong>.

## If the Dymo isn't available

1. **Another printer.** The tag prints on any printer from the same print tab. It's sized for the Dymo, so on sheet labels the layout shifts. Test one before printing a stack.
2. **Shopify's barcode labels.** ThreadCloud writes each size's SKU into its Shopify barcode, so Shopify's own barcode labels scan to the same product. In Shopify admin, search Products for `tag:tc-order-` followed by the order number, select the products and print barcode labels from there. You lose brand and colour but keep the size, SKU, barcode and price.
3. **Hand-write the SKU** on a tag. Last resort.

Custom orders have their own label buttons, including <strong>PRINT ON SHEET</strong> for a regular office printer. See <a href="/workflows/mtm/">Custom orders</a>.

## If it goes wrong

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Barcode won't scan</div>
The print scale was set to "Fit to page", which shrinks the barcode. Print again at 100% or Actual size.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">The print tab says the link is invalid or expired</div>
Print links last 24 hours. Go back to the shipment or Logistics and start the print again to get a fresh link.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">The tag shows the wrong size</div>
Fix the size on the receipt first (see <a href="/workflows/receiving/#fix-a-wrong-size">Fix a wrong size</a>), then reprint. The new tag prints the corrected size.
</div>
