# Logistics

**Logistics** ("shipments in motion") is the desk for everything that's already been bought: what's on its way, what's landed, and the paperwork that goes with it. Open it from the menu.

If a button name here doesn't match what you see, the app changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.

## What's on the page

| Part | What it's for |
|---|---|
| <strong>Reprint a product label</strong> | Print a hang tag for any product in the store. |
| <strong>Reprint Tags</strong> | Find a received shipment by brand and reprint its tags or its receiving document. |
| <strong>Receiving Log</strong> | What's due now, what's coming up, and every receiving document. |
| <strong>Transfer Out</strong> | Send stock back to a supplier. See [Transfer Out](/workflows/transfer-out/). |
| <strong>MTM Orders</strong> | The custom orders list. |
| <strong>Purchase orders</strong> | Every PO, sent and unsent, with its supplier link. |

## Reprint a product label

For a tag that fell off, got damaged, or was never printed. It works for any product in the store, including ones ThreadCloud didn't buy.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Scan the product's barcode, or type a name, SKU, brand or supplier reference.</li>
  <li>Pick the right size from the results (arrow keys, or click). A scanner's Enter only searches; it never prints by itself.</li>
  <li>Set <strong>Qty</strong> (1 to 100) and click <strong>PRINT</strong>.</li>
</ol>

If the browser blocks the print window, click <strong>OPEN LABEL</strong>. More on printing: <a href="/workflows/labels/">Print Labels</a>.

## Receiving log

<strong>Receiving Log</strong> opens the **Receiving log** ("what's landed"). It has three tabs, and each has its own search box. On a phone, each row stacks as a card.

On the Receiving log, <strong>Print received tags</strong> (top right) prints the tags of recent receipts that have not been printed yet, including boxes received on the phone. See <a href="/workflows/labels/#print-received-tags">Print Labels</a>.

The log covers buying orders only. Reorders are not on it: open the reorder from the **Reorders** list (or Home's **Receive reorders**) and use its **Receive** tab. See <a href="/workflows/reorder/">Reorder</a>.

**Due now** lists every order you can receive today, with its **Order**, **Delivery window** and **Received** (for example "25% received, 8 days left" or "60% received, 2 days late"). Orders in their window come first, then orders past their window, then orders already arriving before their window opens (both marked, for example "Past window, Sep 15 to Oct 10"), then orders with no window. Search by brand or order. Click a row to open its Receive page.

**Coming up** lists orders whose window hasn't opened yet and that have nothing received, with the window and when it **Opens** (for example "Opens Oct 15"). Search by brand or order.

**Received** lists every receiving document from the last 90 days, newest first, by the receipt date picked on the Receive page (so a box booked late sits under the day it arrived). Each row shows when it was received, **Brand**, **Order**, **Doc Ref**, **Units Received** and a status (**Pending**, **Partial** or **Complete**). Search by brand, order or doc ref. <strong>Show older</strong> loads earlier receipts. On each row:

- <strong>Doc</strong> reprints the receiving document.
- <strong>Tags</strong> reprints the shipment's hang tags.
- Click the row to open the receipt, where you can also correct or reverse it (see <a href="/workflows/receiving/">Receiving</a>).

<strong>Reprint Tags</strong> opens the same log on the Received tab.

### The Shopify card

Someone who can open Enrich also sees a card headed **Shopify** at the top of the log when something needs a look. Receiving never creates or re-pushes a Shopify product by itself; the card asks you to.

- **Not on Shopify yet.** One line per order that has started arriving and still has products with no Shopify product, for example "Gabo · FW-26: 3 products not on Shopify yet". <strong>Review in Enrich</strong> opens that order's Enrich tab, where you review and push them. Reorders, custom orders, imported history and archived POs are not listed.
- **Re-sync.** A line such as "4 received products have details Shopify doesn't have yet" appears for received products whose care label was read after their last push. <strong>Re-sync</strong> opens **Re-sync received products**, which lists them and explains that Shopify gets each product's details as ThreadCloud holds them now. Click **Re-sync N products** (or **Not now**). It runs Enrich's own update push, one order at a time, and never creates a product. At most 100 products go in one click; more come up after it. Photos shot at receiving stay in ThreadCloud.

When it finishes the card says, for example, "Re-synced 2 products to Shopify. 1 product needs you before it can re-sync." ("Nothing was re-synced. 3 products need you before they can re-sync." when none went, "Nothing needed re-syncing." when all were current). Each product that did not go is listed with why, what to do, and a button to the place that fixes it, for example <strong>Review in Enrich</strong> when Enrich has a question about it, <strong>Approve in Enrich</strong> when it is not approved, <strong>Add price in Enrich</strong> when it has no price or cost, or <strong>Open the order</strong> when the order changed. The same re-sync on a Receive page reports the same way, and shows in the strip at the bottom while it runs.

## Purchase orders

At the bottom of the page, **Purchase orders** lists every PO, split into **Unsent**, **Sent** and **Answer waiting** (each with a count). **Answer waiting** lists the buying orders and reorders whose supplier answer needs you; it reads "No supplier answers waiting on you." when there are none. A row whose answer is waiting has a <strong>SUPPLIER ANSWER</strong> button in place of <strong>VIEW PO</strong>; it opens the order's Supplier answer. See <a href="/workflows/supplier-confirmations/">Supplier confirmations</a>. Each row shows the PO number, brand and season, delivery, styles and units, when it was created or sent, and "Supplier acknowledged" once the supplier has confirmed.

- <strong>COPY ACK LINK</strong> copies the supplier's confirmation link, to paste into an email. It reads **ACK LINK COPIED**.
- <strong>VIEW PO</strong> opens the purchase order.

Use this when a supplier asks for the PO again, or says the link they have doesn't work. The copied link is always the current one.

### Archive an unsent PO

In the new design, the register has a status switch: **Unsent**, **Sent**, **Answer waiting** and **Archived**, each with a count. An owner or buyer sees an archive button at the end of an unsent PO's row (its tooltip reads **Archive: hide from Unsent**). Use it for a test PO or a dead draft: it leaves **Unsent** and Home's count of POs not sent. The PO is not deleted. Open **Archived** to find it, marked **Archived** with the date, and click the button on its row (**Unarchive**) to bring it back. A PO that is being amended can't be archived. Archiving does not remove receipts: stock, cost and merchandising figures keep counting what was received on it.
