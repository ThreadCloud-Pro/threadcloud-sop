# Logistics

**Logistics** ("shipments in motion") is the desk for everything that's already been bought: what's on its way, what's landed, and the paperwork that goes with it. Open it from the menu.

## What's on the page

| Part | What it's for |
|---|---|
| <strong>Reprint a product label</strong> | Print a hang tag for any product in the store. |
| <strong>Reprint Tags</strong> | Find a received shipment by brand and reprint its tags or its receiving document. |
| <strong>Receiving Log</strong> | What's due now, what's coming up, and every receiving document. |
| <strong>Transfer Out</strong> | Send stock back to a supplier. See [Transfer Out](/workflows/transfer-out/). |
| <strong>MTM Orders</strong> | The custom orders list. |
| <strong>Purchase orders</strong> | Every PO, sent and unsent, with its supplier link. |

## Reprint a product label

For a tag that fell off, got damaged, or was never printed. It works for any product in the store, including ones ThreadCloud didn't buy.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Scan the product's barcode, or type a name, SKU, brand or supplier reference.</li>
  <li>Pick the right size from the results (arrow keys, or click). A scanner's Enter only searches; it never prints by itself.</li>
  <li>Set <strong>Qty</strong> (1 to 100) and click <strong>PRINT</strong>.</li>
</ol>

If the browser blocks the print window, click <strong>OPEN LABEL</strong>. More on printing: <a href="/workflows/labels/">Print Labels</a>.

## Receiving log

<strong>Receiving Log</strong> opens the **Receiving log** ("what's landed"). It has four tabs (**Due now**, **Coming up**, **Received** and **Open POs**), and each has its own search box. On a phone, each row stacks as a card.

On the Receiving log, <strong>Print received tags</strong> (top right) prints the tags of recent receipts that have not been printed yet, including boxes received on the phone. See <a href="/workflows/labels/#print-received-tags">Print Labels</a>.

The log covers buying orders and reorders. A reorder with the supplier's own PO number (including an open order brought in from another system) is listed and found by that number, and opens the same Receive page it always had. A reorder you are still drafting, with no supplier number, stays off **Due now** and **Coming up**; open it from the **Reorders** list or Home's **Receive reorders**. See <a href="/workflows/receiving/#reorders-on-the-receiving-page-and-the-phone">Receiving</a> and <a href="/workflows/reorder/">Reorder</a>. Imported history never appears here.

When products are on the floor but were never received in ThreadCloud, a card at the top of the log lists them by order with a **Receive** link. See <a href="/workflows/receiving/#arrived-but-never-received">Receiving</a>.

**Due now** lists every order you can receive today, with its **Order**, **Delivery window** and **Received** (for example "25% received, 8 days left" or "60% received, 2 days late"). Orders in their window come first, then orders past their window, then orders already arriving before their window opens (both marked, for example "Past window, Sep 15 to Oct 10"), then orders with no window. Search by brand or order. Click a row to open its Receive page.

**Coming up** lists orders whose window hasn't opened yet and that have nothing received, with the window and when it **Opens** (for example "Opens Oct 15"). Search by brand or order.

**Received** lists every receiving document from the last 90 days, newest first, by the receipt date picked on the Receive page (so a box booked late sits under the day it arrived). Each row shows when it was received, **Brand**, **Order**, **Doc Ref**, **Units Received** and a status (**Pending**, **Partial** or **Complete**). Search by brand, order or doc ref. <strong>Show older</strong> loads earlier receipts. On each row:

- <strong>Doc</strong> reprints the receiving document.
- <strong>Tags</strong> reprints the shipment's hang tags.
- Click the row to open the receipt, where you can also correct or reverse it (see <a href="/workflows/receiving/">Receiving</a>).

<strong>Reprint Tags</strong> opens the same log on the Received tab.

### Open POs

**Open POs** lists every order still arriving, read against the delivery window you want. It reads only. Fully received POs leave the tab. A card at the top counts what needs you, for example "3 late" or "2 past cancel date", with a **Show** button that narrows the list to that group (**Show all** brings it back). The groups are **Past cancel**, **Late**, **Early**, **At risk** and **No window**.

The chip beside the tabs groups the list: **By window** (the default), **By brand** or **By order**. Each row names the brand and PO. A pill and a one-line reason appear only for a PO with a problem, for example "Window ended Nov 2, 9 days ago, 12 units still out". A PO that is on track shows no pill. Search finds an order by brand, season, PO number or the supplier's own number.

Click a problem row to open it. People who can contact suppliers (the Buy area) see **Message supplier** there:

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click the row, then <strong>Message supplier</strong>. A sheet opens with a ready message for where the order stands: confirm the ship dates, please hold until our window opens, past our window, or a cancellation notice for an order past its cancel date.</li>
  <li>Under <strong>Send to</strong> the PO's usual contact is first. Pick someone else if they should get it. It never offers a former rep.</li>
  <li>Edit the message if you want to. Then click <strong>Copy message</strong> (a cancellation reads <strong>Copy cancellation notice</strong>), or <strong>Open in Gmail</strong> to write it to the address on file.</li>
</ol>

ThreadCloud never sends it for you. Each copy or Gmail press is recorded on the order ("Message copied", "Opened in Gmail"). **Copy message** works with no email on file; the sheet says "No email on file" with an **Add one** link. A cancellation notice does not cancel the order in ThreadCloud.

### The Shopify card

Someone who can open Enrich also sees a card headed **Shopify** at the top of the log when something needs a look. Receiving never creates or re-pushes a Shopify product by itself; the card asks you to.

- **No longer on Shopify, or different SKUs.** Before a delivery lands, ThreadCloud checks the open orders that still owe units against Shopify (it only reads). A line such as "Gabo · FW-26: 2 products are no longer on Shopify" means someone deleted the product in Shopify. <strong>Review in Enrich</strong> opens that order's Enrich tab to push it again. A line such as "Gabo · FW-26: 1 product has different SKUs on Shopify" means a SKU was changed in Shopify after the push. <strong>Open receiving</strong> opens the order's Receive page, where the relink runs. See <a href="/workflows/receiving/#deleted-products-and-changed-skus">Receiving</a>.
- **Not on Shopify yet.** One line per order that has started arriving and still has products with no Shopify product, for example "Gabo · FW-26: 3 products not on Shopify yet". <strong>Review in Enrich</strong> opens that order's Enrich tab, where you review and push them. Reorders, custom orders, imported history and archived POs are not listed, and neither are products that are closed.
- **Re-sync.** A line such as "4 received products have details Shopify doesn't have yet" appears for received products whose care label was read after their last push. <strong>Re-sync</strong> opens **Re-sync received products**, which lists them and explains that Shopify gets each product's details as ThreadCloud holds them now. Click **Re-sync N products** (or **Not now**). It runs Enrich's own update push, one order at a time, and never creates a product. At most 100 products go in one click; more come up after it. Photos shot at receiving stay in ThreadCloud.

When it finishes the card says, for example, "Re-synced 2 products to Shopify. 1 product needs you before it can re-sync." ("Nothing was re-synced. 3 products need you before they can re-sync." when none went, "Nothing needed re-syncing." when all were current). Each product that did not go is listed with why, what to do, and a button to the place that fixes it, for example <strong>Review in Enrich</strong> when Enrich has a question about it, <strong>Approve in Enrich</strong> when it is not approved, <strong>Add price in Enrich</strong> when it has no price or cost, or <strong>Open the order</strong> when the order changed. The same re-sync on a Receive page reports the same way, and shows in the strip at the bottom while it runs.

## Purchase orders

At the bottom of the page, **Purchase orders** lists every PO. In the classic design it is split into **Unsent**, **Sent** and **Answer waiting** (each with a count); the new design's tabs are described below. **Answer waiting** lists the buying orders and reorders whose supplier answer needs you; it reads "No supplier answers waiting on you." when there are none. A row whose answer is waiting has a <strong>SUPPLIER ANSWER</strong> button in place of <strong>VIEW PO</strong>; it opens the order's Supplier answer. See <a href="/workflows/supplier-confirmations/">Supplier confirmations</a>. Each row shows the PO number, brand and season, delivery, styles and units, when it was created or sent, and "Supplier acknowledged" once the supplier has confirmed.

- <strong>COPY ACK LINK</strong> copies the supplier's confirmation link, to paste into an email. It reads **ACK LINK COPIED**.
- <strong>VIEW PO</strong> opens the purchase order.

Use this when a supplier asks for the PO again, or says the link they have doesn't work. The copied link is always the current one.

### The register in the new design

In the new design the register has four tabs, each with a count and its own list, so a long backlog on one tab never pushes another tab's rows off the page:

| Tab | What it lists |
|---|---|
| **To send** | POs not issued yet. This is the same count as Home's POs not sent. |
| **Incoming** | POs that were issued, or where receiving has started, and are not fully received. |
| **Received** | POs fully received. "Nothing more is coming" counts as received. |
| **Archived** | POs set aside (below). |

Imported history is never a PO and is not on the register, so nothing is hidden by a cap on legacy orders. An order that was never issued but has already been received against shows on **Incoming**, marked **Not issued**.

**Answer waiting** is not a tab here. Supplier answers waiting on you are listed once, on **Supplier answers** (Seasonal orders and Reorders tabs), and Logistics has a **Supplier answers** row under **Do**. An old link to the register's Answer waiting filter lands there. See [Supplier confirmations](/workflows/supplier-confirmations/).

### Archive an unsent PO

An owner or buyer sees an archive button at the end of a **To send** row (its tooltip reads **Archive: hide from To send**). Use it for a test PO or a dead draft: it leaves **To send** and Home's count of POs not sent. The PO is not deleted. Open **Archived** to find it, marked **Archived** with the date, and click the button on its row (**Unarchive**) to bring it back. A PO that is being amended can't be archived. Archiving does not remove receipts: stock, cost and merchandising figures keep counting what was received on it.
