# Logistics

**Logistics** ("shipments in motion") is the desk for everything that's already been bought: what's on its way, what's landed, and the paperwork that goes with it. Open it from the menu.

If a button name here doesn't match what you see, the app changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

## What's on the page

| Part | What it's for |
|---|---|
| <strong>Reprint a product label</strong> | Print a hang tag for any product in the store. |
| <strong>Reprint Tags</strong> | Find a received shipment by brand and reprint its tags or its receiving document. |
| <strong>Receiving Log</strong> | What's due now, what's coming up, and every receiving document. |
| <strong>Transfer Out</strong> | Send stock back to a supplier. See [Transfer Out](/workflows/transfer-out/). |
| <strong>Supplier Responses</strong> | Every supplier answer to a PO, pending and reviewed. |
| <strong>MTM Orders</strong> | The custom orders list. |
| <strong>Purchase orders</strong> | Every PO, sent and unsent, with its supplier link. |

## Reprint a product label

For a tag that fell off, got damaged, or was never printed. It works for any product in the store, including ones ThreadCloud didn't buy.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Scan the product's barcode, or type a name, SKU, brand or supplier reference.</li>
  <li>Pick the right size from the results (arrow keys, or click). A scanner's Enter only searches; it never prints by itself.</li>
  <li>Set <strong>Qty</strong> (1 to 100) and click <strong>PRINT</strong>.</li>
</ol>

If the browser blocks the print window, click <strong>OPEN LABEL</strong>. More on printing: <a href="/workflows/labels/">Print Labels</a>.

## Receiving log

<strong>Receiving Log</strong> opens the **Receiving log** ("what's landed"). It has three tabs, and each has its own search box. On a phone, each row stacks as a card.

**Due now** lists every order you can receive today, with its **Order**, **Delivery window** and **Received** (for example "25% received, 8 days left" or "60% received, 2 days late"). Orders in their window come first, then orders past their window, then orders already arriving before their window opens (both marked, for example "Past window, Sep 15 to Oct 10"), then orders with no window. Reorders are here too. Search by brand or order. Click a row to open its Receive page.

**Coming up** lists orders whose window hasn't opened yet and that have nothing received, with the window and when it **Opens** (for example "Opens Oct 15"). Search by brand or order.

**Received** lists every receiving document from the last 90 days, newest first, by the receipt date picked on the Receive page (so a box booked late sits under the day it arrived). Each row shows when it was received, **Brand**, **Order**, **Doc Ref**, **Units Received** and a status (**Pending**, **Partial** or **Complete**). Search by brand, order or doc ref. <strong>Show older</strong> loads earlier receipts. On each row:

- <strong>Doc</strong> reprints the receiving document.
- <strong>Tags</strong> reprints the shipment's hang tags.
- Click the row to open the receipt, where you can also correct or reverse it (see <a href="/workflows/receiving/">Receiving</a>).

<strong>Reprint Tags</strong> opens the same log on the Received tab.

## Supplier Responses

Every time a supplier answers a PO, it lands here. Filter by **All**, **Pending** or **Reviewed**, search by brand, order or PO number, and click a row to review it. See <a href="/workflows/supplier-confirmations/">Supplier confirmations</a>.

## Purchase orders

At the bottom of the page, **Purchase orders** lists every PO, split into **Unsent** and **Sent** (each with a count). Each row shows the PO number, brand and season, delivery, styles and units, when it was created or sent, and "Supplier acknowledged" once the supplier has confirmed.

- <strong>COPY ACK LINK</strong> copies the supplier's confirmation link, to paste into an email. It reads **ACK LINK COPIED**.
- <strong>VIEW PO</strong> opens the purchase order.

Use this when a supplier asks for the PO again, or says the link they have doesn't work. The copied link is always the current one.
