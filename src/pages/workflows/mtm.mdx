# Custom orders

Custom orders are one-off special orders: a made-to-measure suit, a special-order jacket, a monogrammed tie, anything you buy for one customer (or a few customers on one maker's order). You start from the maker's confirmation, review the details, send the products to Shopify so they can be scanned at the register, and receive and tag them when they land.

## Quick path

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>New custom order</strong>: drop in the maker's confirmation, or enter it by hand.</li>
  <li><strong>Review</strong>: the customer, the garments, the measurements, the price and the delivery date.</li>
  <li><strong>SEND TO SHOPIFY</strong>: creates the products, ready to scan at the register.</li>
  <li><strong>MARK RECEIVED</strong> when the garments arrive, and enter the final cost.</li>
  <li><strong>Print the labels</strong>, tag the garments, and ring them up on POS when the customer collects.</li>
</ol>

The top of every custom order shows where it is: **1 Review**, **2 Send to Shopify**, **3 Receive & tag**.

## Start a custom order

On the home screen, switch to the **Sales floor** desk and click the <strong>Custom order</strong> tile ("bespoke · one customer"). The **Custom orders** list under the tiles shows open orders, the ones that need you first. You can also reach the list from **Logistics**, <strong>MTM Orders</strong>.

Click <strong>New custom order</strong>. Two ways in:

- **Drop the supplier confirmation**: a PDF, an image or a text file. ThreadCloud reads it and pulls out the customer, the maker and reference, the garments and options, the price, and any measurements it can see. This is the fast path when the maker sent you a confirmation.
- <strong>Enter the order manually</strong> ("No confirmation to upload?"): for a phoned-in order. It skips the upload and opens a blank order.

Either way you land on the review screen with whatever was found filled in.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">One order, several confirmations</div>
If an order came as more than one confirmation (say a jacket from one maker and trousers from another), drop all the files in at once. ThreadCloud reads each one and builds a single order from them. If one file can't be read, the rest still go through; fill that garment in by hand.
</div>

## Review

Everything on the review screen **saves on its own** as you go. Watch for **Saving…** then **Saved**. Work top to bottom:

### A · Customer

Type a name, or search your Shopify customers and tap the right one ("Is this them? Tap to confirm."). If they're new, use <strong>+ CREATE</strong> to make them in Shopify. A customer isn't required to send the order: an unnamed order can be attached to the customer at the register.

For a trunk show or a split order, click <strong>DIFFERENT CLIENTS PER ITEM</strong> to give each garment its own client. <strong>BACK TO ONE CLIENT</strong> undoes it.

### B · Garments

One card per garment or product. For each one:

- **Choose product type**. Every garment needs one before you can send.
- **Size ordered**. It prints on the tag ("Add the size · prints on the tag"). **Size scale** sets the scale the size is read on and printed under; change it if the size reads wrong.
- **Fabric**, with an optional fabric photo, and details such as lapel, lining and buttons. <strong>+ ADD FIELD</strong> adds another detail.
- **Adjustments**, **Qty**, notes for the item, detail photos and a spec sheet.
- **COST** (what you pay the maker) and **RETAIL** (what the customer pays).

Click <strong>ADD GARMENT</strong> for more. Custom orders aren't limited to tailoring: ties, scarves and accessories work too.

#### Bill several pieces as one

Pieces sold together don't have to be priced one by one. Tick two or more garment cards and click <strong>Combine N · bill as one</strong>, or click <strong>All one item</strong> to group the whole order. A jacket with trousers becomes a **Suit**; any other mix (a shirt and tie, two shirts) becomes a **Set**. A group has one cost and one retail price, and goes to Shopify as one product. <strong>Ungroup</strong> gives each piece its own price back. An order can hold several groups alongside single pieces.

### C · Fit profile

The customer's measurements. Toggle **IN** or **CM**. Only the measurements that matter for the garment type show. When the customer is linked in Shopify, their measurements are saved to their profile; only the fields you fill in update it, blanks leave what's there alone.

### D · Pricing & delivery

- **Customer total**: the sum of the garment prices. It fills in once every garment has a price.
- **Maker currency**: what you pay the maker in.
- **Deposit** (optional): what the customer paid up front, in your store currency.
- **Estimated delivery**: required.

The meter beside the heading counts down what's left ("2 of 4 to send") and reads **Ready to send** when the product type, the garment prices, the currency and the delivery date are all in.

### E · Supplier confirmation

The **Brand / maker** and the maker's **Reference number**.

## Send to Shopify

When the meter reads **Ready to send**, click <strong>SEND TO SHOPIFY</strong>. It reads **CREATING PRODUCTS…** while it works. ThreadCloud creates a product in Shopify for each garment (or each group), with a barcode, ready to scan at the register. The order moves to **Send to Shopify** on the progress line and waits for delivery.

If the button is greyed out, hover over it to see what's missing. If Shopify refuses, the screen says "Couldn't send to Shopify." with the reason.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Walk the customer through the spec first</div>
Custom orders are non-returnable. Go over the spec with the customer, and take a deposit, before the order goes to the maker. Once it's with the maker, changes are expensive or impossible.
</div>

## Receive and tag

When the garments land, open the order. The screen reads "Before you receive, check every product against this list." For each garment, find its row by the **tag code**, then check the **size** and the **price** match the tag. If anything is off, fix it on the review step first.

Then enter the final **Cost** for each garment, the **Customer total** if it changed, and any **Notes** (for example "fabric upgrade billed extra"), and click <strong>MARK RECEIVED</strong>. ThreadCloud books the stock into Shopify and tells you if it couldn't.

Once received, the order shows the final cost, the retail and the margin, and three print buttons:

- <strong>RECEIVING DOC</strong>: the receiving document, with garments, units and cost.
- <strong>PRINT LABELS</strong>: one label per tag on the Dymo.
- <strong>PRINT ON SHEET</strong>: a sheet of labels for a regular office printer.

<strong>EDIT RECEIPT</strong> lets you correct the cost, total or notes later (<strong>SAVE CHANGES</strong>). <strong>REVERSE RECEIPT</strong> sends the order back to waiting for delivery and clears the confirmed costs, total and notes, so you can receive it again.

## On the sales floor (POS)

The <strong>Custom Orders</strong> tile in Shopify POS ("Load a made-to-measure order") lists every custom order that's been sent to Shopify and not yet paid at the register. Each row shows the customer, the garments, the deposit on file, the ETA and the total. Tap a row to load its items, and the customer if one is linked, into the cart. You'll see "Added to the cart." when it's done.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">No customer on the order?</div>
The tile says "Customer not attached" and loads the items anyway. Add the customer in the cart before you take payment.
</div>

## Owner tools on the Custom orders list

- <strong>Make all POS-ready</strong>: puts every custom order product back on Point of Sale and sets its scannable barcode. Use it if a custom order is missing from the register.
- <strong>Barcode check</strong>: lists custom-order products that share a barcode or have no order behind them. It changes nothing until you choose to archive the stray copies.

## If it goes wrong

- **The upload didn't read the confirmation well**: fix the fields on review, or start over with <strong>Enter the order manually</strong>.
- **SEND TO SHOPIFY is greyed out**: every garment needs a product type and a price, and the order needs a currency and a delivery date. Hover over the button to see which is missing.
- **Anything else**: see <a href="/runbook/">If something breaks</a>.
