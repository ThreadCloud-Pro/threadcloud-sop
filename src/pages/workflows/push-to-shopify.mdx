# Push to Shopify

Pushing is how a product gets into your Shopify store. You push from the **Enrich** tab. The first push creates the product in Shopify as a **draft**, with its sizes, SKUs, cost, price, photos and product details. Later pushes send your edits to the product that is already there.

## Before you push

- Every product you want to push is **Approved** in Enrich.
- Cost and MSRP look right.
- The sizes match the order.
- The order currency in the header matches the supplier's invoice.
- Photos are on the product, hero first (a push works without them, but the storefront will look bare).

A product with no ordered sizes is not pushed. Add its sizes in Parse & Build first.

## Three ways to push

| Where | Button | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| The **Product details** heading on Enrich | <strong>Send to Shopify (N)</strong> | Opens the push confirmation for every product that has something Shopify hasn't received yet. |
| The bulk action bar, after ticking products | <strong>Push selected</strong> | Pushes only the ticked products: creates the new ones and re-syncs the ones already in Shopify. |
| A single approved product's footer | <strong>Push to Shopify</strong> or <strong>Push update</strong> | Creates just this product as a draft, or sends your edits to the product that already exists. |

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open the order and click the <strong>Enrich</strong> tab.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Send to Shopify (N)</strong>.</li>
  <li>Read the confirmation, titled "Sync N products to Shopify?". It lists what gets pushed and the products in this sync.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Sync N to Shopify</strong>.</li>
  <li>Wait for the result line. You can leave the page; the push keeps going.</li>
</ol>

### Push selected

Tick the products you want and click <strong>Push selected</strong>. The confirmation asks "Push N selected styles to Shopify?" and names the mix, for example "3 new, 2 updates". New products are created as drafts. Products already in Shopify are re-synced with your latest edits, the same as <strong>Push update</strong> on one product. Click <strong>Push N now</strong>.

- A new product needs a price and a cost first; one without is left out as "missing price or cost".
- A product in Shopify that isn't approved yet is re-synced only while it is still a draft in Shopify; otherwise it reports "Approve it first."
- Skipped and rejected products are never sent, and show as "left out".
- If nothing selected can go, the confirmation reads **Nothing to push** with the reason.

## What gets pushed

The confirmation spells it out:

- Title, description, SKU, cost and MSRP
- The size and colour variants, with stock set to **0**. Stock fills in when you receive.
- The product's photos, hero first
- Product details Shopify filters and the product page read: category, subcategory, fabric, fit, country of origin, care, pattern, occasion, colour group, weight, HS code, and the fields that depend on the product (lapel, collar, and so on)
- Measurements, and the size chart on the product page when the product has a brand model

Every product is also tagged with `tc-imported` and `tc-order-` plus the order number, so you can pull up a whole order in Shopify admin. When a size has no barcode, its SKU is written into the barcode field, so the hang tag and Shopify's own barcode labels scan to the same product.

## Drafts, and when a product goes live

A first push creates the product as a **draft**. Shoppers can't see it yet. A later push keeps whatever status the product has: a draft stays a draft, a live product stays live, and its web address doesn't change.

A product goes onto your **Point of Sale** when you <a href="/workflows/receiving/">receive</a> it. You decide when it goes live on the online store.

If a product was received before its first push, that first push makes it active and puts it on Point of Sale, because the stock is already in the store. This happens only when Shopify confirmed every size. If it couldn't, the product stays a draft and the result says "Received before push but kept as a draft: Shopify could not confirm every size. Once a push confirms it, use Make POS-ready on the Receiving tab." Later pushes never change a product's status, and a store with no Point of Sale channel is left as it is.

Missing product details don't stop a draft being created. A product that is already **active** in Shopify still needs approved copy before its changes go out.

## Reading the result

Each product's footer shows one line for what happened:

- **Pushed to Shopify**: the push created the product.
- **Updated on Shopify**: the push updated a product that already existed.
- **Not everything landed:** followed by what didn't go through (a cost, a product detail). Fix it and push again.
- **Fields changed during that push, so push again.** Something was edited while the push was running.
- A failure names its reason. A failure doesn't always mean nothing landed: the product may already exist in Shopify with the write unfinished. Push again. Never create the product a second time by hand.

The line clears as soon as you edit the product again.

A whole push reports in one sentence, for example "14 of 14 pushed to Shopify" or "12 of 14 pushed, 2 need attention". When you push a selection, each product that didn't land is named with its reason. A product missing its cost or MSRP is never attempted; the confirmation names it before the push runs.

## Is it really in Shopify?

Under each product's footer, Enrich shows what Shopify actually has:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pushed and verified** | Shopify confirmed every ordered size. This is the only state that counts as done. |
| **Pushed but unverified** | Shopify has the product but at least one size isn't linked. Push the product again to link it. |
| **Not pushed** | The product has not been created in Shopify yet. |

If a product reads "Not pushed" with "A previous push did not record a product identity, so pushing again is fenced.", click <strong>Verify with Shopify</strong>. It asks Shopify what it has for this product and links it. It never creates a product. See <a href="/workflows/enrich/#verify-with-shopify">Enrich</a>.

Receiving can only book sizes that are linked. Clear "Pushed but unverified" before the boxes arrive.

## Sending later edits

Edit a product in Enrich after it's been pushed and the heading button reads <strong>Send to Shopify (N)</strong> again, and the product's footer offers <strong>Push update</strong>. Nothing changes on the storefront until you push.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">What a push overwrites</div>
ThreadCloud owns the product's description, its ThreadCloud product details, its ThreadCloud tags, and a price ThreadCloud has set. A push never changes stock (receiving does that), and it leaves fields that aren't ThreadCloud's alone. A price you set in Enrich is written on every push, even if someone changed it in Shopify admin since.
</div>

## Pulling products back

If you pushed with wrong data:

- **Fix it**: edit in Enrich and push again. Don't delete; just overwrite.
- **Remove a whole order**: in Shopify admin, search for `tag:tc-order-` followed by the order number, select the products and delete them. Only do this for products that were never received.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Treating "Pushed but unverified" as done</div>
The product exists in Shopify but a size isn't linked, and receiving can't book that size. Push again, or use Verify with Shopify when it's offered, until it reads "Pushed and verified".
</div>

<div class="callout callout-danger">
<div class="callout-title">Editing ThreadCloud fields in Shopify admin</div>
If you change a product's description, tags or price directly in Shopify admin, the next push from ThreadCloud overwrites it. Make those changes in Enrich.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Creating the product by hand after a failed push</div>
A failed push may already have created the product. Push again from Enrich. Creating it by hand gives you two products with the same SKU.
</div>
