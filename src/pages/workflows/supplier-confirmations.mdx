# Supplier confirmations

After you send a purchase order, the supplier answers. Sometimes on the acknowledgement link you emailed them, sometimes as a confirmation PDF or an email. ThreadCloud compares that answer to the order and shows you every difference, line by line. You decide what to accept.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
Every time a confirmation comes back on a sent PO. Do it before the goods ship, so receiving is counting against numbers the supplier actually confirmed.
</div>

<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-01</div>

## Two ways a confirmation arrives

1. **The acknowledgement link.** The purchase order's <strong>Email brand contact</strong> button emails the supplier a link (or copy it with <strong>Supplier ack link</strong>). They confirm each product on it. Their answer lands on the order on its own.
2. **A document or email.** The supplier sends a PDF, an image, or an email. You attach it on the purchase order and ThreadCloud reads it.

Both end up on the same review screen.

## Where to find the purchase order

- **A buying order:** in Parse & Build, the footer button <strong>PO sent</strong> (or <strong>VIEW PO &amp; ACK LINK</strong> on the locked band) opens it.
- **A reorder:** the reorder's **PO** tab.
- **Any order:** **Logistics** lists every purchase order under **Purchase orders**, with <strong>VIEW PO</strong> and <strong>COPY ACK LINK</strong>. See <a href="/workflows/logistics/">Logistics</a>.

## Knowing an answer came back

- On the home screen's **Merchandising** desk, **Needs you** lists "Supplier response" with a <strong>Review</strong> button for every answer waiting on you, and "no supplier confirmation logged" with <strong>Log confirmation</strong> for a sent PO that hasn't been answered yet.
- **Logistics**, <strong>Supplier Responses</strong> lists every answer, with **All**, **Pending** and **Reviewed** filters. Search by brand, order or PO number and click a row to open it.

## Uploading a confirmation

Open the order's purchase order (see above). Once the PO has been sent, a card titled <strong>Supplier confirmation</strong> sits below the tabs ("Attach the confirmation when it comes back from the supplier."). Click it to open.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Attach the PDF, JPG, or PNG. Or paste the email body into the box ("…or paste the confirmation email body here"). You need one or the other; with neither, the card says "Attach the confirmation PDF or image, or paste the email body."</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Compare to order</strong>. The button reads <strong>Reading…</strong> while it works.</li>
  <li>When it finishes, you land on the review screen. If it could not read the file, the card says "Could not read the confirmation. Try a PDF or paste the text."</li>
</ol>

After that, the purchase order shows a <strong>Supplier response</strong> card: the document name (or "Answered on the supplier acknowledgement link", or "Pasted from the confirmation email"), a "Received" date, a match line ("N of N lines matched", "N lines need a decision", or "Not yet reviewed line by line"), confirmed delivery and payment terms if stated, and two buttons: <strong>Open document</strong> and <strong>Review the response</strong>.

## The review screen

The header reads the brand and "Supplier response", with a <strong>DISMISS</strong> button. A line at the top says which answer you are looking at: "Showing the supplier's answer on the confirmation document, received [date]." The card below is titled <strong>Order confirmation</strong> with the file name, an <strong>Open document</strong> button, and the supplier's own reference if the document carried one.

### What gets compared

| Section | What it holds |
|---|---|
| <strong>Confirmed date</strong> or <strong>Delivery window</strong> | Your ordered dates with the supplier's beside them. A single confirmed ship date shows as "Confirmed date"; a range shows as "Delivery window". "Not stated on the confirmation" when the document has none. |
| <strong>Cancel date</strong> | The cancellation date the supplier stated, when there is one. Shown for your information; it is not applied to the order from this screen. |
| <strong>Payment terms</strong> | The brand's terms on file beside the confirmed terms. |
| <strong>Confirmation contact</strong> | The supplier's office contact from the document. Display only. |
| <strong>Confirmation lines</strong> | Every line on the document, sorted into the groups below. |
| <strong>Product Details from Supplier</strong> | Facts the supplier stated per product: Country of Origin, Fabric Composition, Colour Group, Item Weight, Supplier Reference, Model Code, Colour Code. |
| <strong>Availability Changes</strong> | Sizes the supplier confirmed short. |
| <strong>Unavailable</strong> | Products the supplier cannot supply at all. |

### Dates

If the supplier's dates differ from the order, the old dates are struck through and the new ones shown in green. Click <strong>Use confirmed dates</strong> to put them on the order. Once done, the section shows a <strong>Reviewed</strong> badge. If it fails, the screen says "Could not apply the confirmed dates. Reload the review and try again."

### Payment terms

If the confirmed terms differ from what is on file, click <strong>Update supplier terms</strong>. This writes to the brand's supplier record so the new terms carry onto future POs.

## Accepting or rejecting lines

The <strong>Confirmation lines</strong> block sorts every line on the supplier's document:

- <strong>Needs your choice (N)</strong>: lines ThreadCloud could not place on one product with confidence. Each shows the line's title and codes. If there is an AI suggestion, it shows "AI · high/medium/low" with the product it thinks it is, and an <strong>Accept [code]</strong> button. Otherwise use the dropdown ("Choose a product…") to pick the product yourself. Choosing is the save.
- To reject a line, pick <strong>Not on this order</strong> from the same dropdown (or click <strong>Accept</strong> when the suggestion is "Looks like it is not on this order"). It moves to <strong>Not on this order (N)</strong>. <strong>Undo</strong> puts it back.
- <strong>Matched lines (N)</strong>: lines placed on a product. Each shows the ordered and confirmed quantity. A line you placed yourself has an <strong>Undo</strong> button ("Release this product from the confirmation line and put the line back for review").
- <strong>Ordered, not confirmed (N)</strong>: products on your order the supplier did not list. They get a <strong>Not confirmed</strong> badge. "The supplier didn't list these. Confirm with them before assuming they're unavailable." If lines still need a choice, place those first; what is left is what the supplier did not list.

Two shortcuts at the top of the block:

- <strong>Accept N suggestions</strong>: binds every high and medium confidence suggestion in one go. Low confidence and "not on this order" proposals still need a click each.
- <strong>Suggest matches</strong> (later <strong>Suggest again</strong>): asks for a recommendation per unplaced line. Nothing binds until you accept.

On an older document that was read before line-by-line review existed, there is a <strong>Re-check matches</strong> button to re-run the match.

If a choice does not save: "Could not save that choice. Try again." If someone else changed the review meanwhile: "This review moved on. Reloading."

## Accepting a confirmed cost

When a matched line's confirmed cost differs from the order's live cost, the line shows both, and a small <strong>accept cost</strong> tick beside it ("Tick to write the supplier's confirmed cost onto this order line. The MSRP follows unless it is yours."). <strong>Tick all costs (N)</strong> beside the Matched lines heading ticks every changed cost at once. Untick any line to keep its cost.

Then click <strong>Apply confirmed costs</strong> (the button reads <strong>Apply product details</strong> when product details are ticked too; it saves both). Each line reports what happened underneath it, in green or red:

- "Order cost 519.00 EUR to 549.00 EUR. MSRP 1095.00 CAD to 1150.00 CAD." The cost is on the order in Parse & Build now, and the retail price was re-derived from it through the order's margin or multiplier.
- "MSRP unchanged at ..." or a named reason the MSRP was left alone: a price you set by hand, a locked price, or a supplier-printed retail is never moved.
- "The confirmation stated no currency, so it was read in the order's EUR." when the document did not label the figure.
- "Order cost updated; received units keep their receipt cost, and the change is recorded as a correction." when the line has already been received. A locked FX rate is never touched.
- A red line means it was not applied, and says why. A cost stated in a different currency from the order ("currency differs" on the line) is refused, not converted. A cost accepted against an order cost that changed since you opened the review is refused; reload and tick again.

Reorders show the confirmed costs but do not offer the tick.

## Product details from the supplier

On the acknowledgement link, the supplier is asked to verify specific facts per product: fabric, country of origin, colour group, and item weight. Facts the buyer had not settled are marked "Please verify". The supplier clicks <strong>Confirm</strong> or <strong>Unknown</strong> on each, or corrects the value.

On your side these arrive under <strong>Product Details from Supplier</strong>: a row per fact with "Current → Supplier" and an <strong>Apply</strong> tick. Every change is ticked by default; untick a row to keep your value. <strong>Tick all</strong> re-ticks everything. A fact the supplier marked <strong>Unknown</strong> shows for your information only; its tick is disabled. Click <strong>Apply product details</strong> to save the ticked values onto the styles. If it fails: "Could not apply the supplier's product details. Try again." If a supplier reference would make two products identical, the apply refuses: "This supplier identity would duplicate another product. Review the model and colour codes before applying."

## Quantity changes

When the supplier confirmed fewer units on some sizes, a <strong>Quantity changes</strong> notice appears and an <strong>Availability Changes</strong> card lists each product with a <strong>Partial</strong> badge and the confirmed grid.

- On a **buying order**: review the size adjustments, then update quantities in Parse & Build (unlock the order to amend it if the PO was sent). Nothing here changes the order.
- On a **reorder**: two outcomes, spelled out on the screen. <strong>Keep my quantities</strong> means do nothing; receiving defaults to the confirmed numbers and the shortfall stays on backorder. <strong>Drop to confirmed (clear backorder)</strong> reduces the order to the confirmed numbers. Only do this if you won't chase the rest.

Products the supplier marked unavailable appear under <strong>Unavailable</strong> with their note.

If the supplier confirmed everything with no changes, the screen says "Supplier confirmed all styles with no changes or additional details." with a <strong>Dismiss</strong> button.

## Superseded confirmations

A supplier can answer more than once: a PDF, then a corrected PDF; or the link, then an email. ThreadCloud keeps all of them and shows the newest.

When the one you are viewing is not the newest, the notice at the top reads "Showing the supplier's answer on [the newest source], received [date]. It supersedes [the older source] of [date], below." with a <strong>Read the superseded confirmation</strong> link. The older card carries a <strong>Superseded</strong> badge, and its apply buttons (<strong>Use confirmed dates</strong>, <strong>Update supplier terms</strong>) are hidden. Only the newest answer can be applied.

Re-issuing a PO also supersedes the supplier's link. If they open the old one they see: "This purchase order was revised after this link was sent. Please ask the buyer for the current link." Send them the new one from the purchase order.

## Errors the supplier can hit on the link

These show on the supplier's screen, so they will come back to you by email:

| They see | What happened | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| "This order needs a corrected PO number. Please ask the buyer to issue a corrected purchase order and acknowledgement link." | The order has no real PO number, so nothing can be acknowledged against it. | Assign a PO number, re-issue the PO, and send the new link. |
| "This purchase order was revised after this link was sent. Please ask the buyer for the current link." | You re-issued the PO after emailing the link. | Send the current link. |
| "This order was revised while you were reviewing it. Refresh the page to continue with the current sizes and quantities." | The order changed while they had the page open. | Nothing on your side. They refresh and answer again. |
| "Invalid or expired link. Please request a new one from the buyer." | The link was revoked or is no longer valid. | Send a fresh link from the purchase order. |
