# Receiving

When a box arrives, you open the order's **Receive** page, count what's in this box, and press **Receive**. ThreadCloud books the stock into Shopify, locks the exchange rate, puts the products on your Point of Sale, and prints the hang tags. It's usually done at the receiving table with a tablet beside the open box.

<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-03</div>

</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
The day a box arrives for a buying order or a reorder. Both receive on this same page; a reorder shows its own header and tabs on top (see <a href="/workflows/reorder/">Reorder</a>). For a custom order, see <a href="/workflows/mtm/">Custom orders</a>.
</div>

## What you'll need

- The box and the packing list
- The Dymo label printer loaded with 30336 labels (see <a href="/workflows/labels/">Print Labels</a>)
- A tape measure, if you're capturing measurements

## Quick path

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the order's Receive page.</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Check the box details</strong> in the dark bar at the bottom: date, location, delivery note and exchange rate.</li>
  <li><strong>Count what's in this box</strong>: click <strong>All N arrived</strong> on a product that matches, or type each size.</li>
  <li><strong>Open Checks</strong> if the bar shows any, and clear them.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Receive N units</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Print</strong> the hang tags.</li>
  <li><strong>Tag and shelve</strong> the goods.</li>
</ol>

## Find the order

Any of these gets you to the Receive page:

- **Home.** The **Receive** card ("Pick the order, count what came in") is on both desks, with a count of orders in their delivery window. It opens the Receiving log. On the Sales floor desk, **Delivery windows open now** and **Past their window** list orders with units still to come; click one. **Customer orders** has <strong>Receive reorders</strong> and <strong>Receive custom orders</strong>.
- **Logistics, Receiving log.** The **Due now** tab lists every order you can receive today. Click a row. See <a href="/workflows/logistics/#receiving-log">Logistics</a>.
- **The order itself.** Click the <strong>Receive</strong> tab in the order header, or a reorder's <strong>Receive</strong> tab.

## The page at a glance

The top reads **Receive** with one line, for example "112 of 186 received so far. Count what came in this box." While you count, a large number on the right shows how many units are **counted in this box**, and the bar under it fills: what was already in, then this box.

Below that is **In this box**: one card per product still owed. Products that are already in full sit further down under **Already received**, folded to one line each.

## Check the box details

The dark bar at the bottom carries one sentence about the box, for example "Arriving today into Main Store · Add delivery note · Rate 1.4823". Click any part of it to open **Box details**:

| Field | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Today, unless you're booking a delivery late. The Receiving log files the receipt under this date. |
| **Receive into** | The Shopify location the stock goes into. |
| **Delivery note number** | The number on the supplier's delivery note. Leave it blank and ThreadCloud makes one. Used on the receiving document and the tags. |
| **Note** | Anything worth remembering: a partial box, a damaged unit, a late ship date. |
| **Exchange rate** | Only on an order in another currency, for example "1 EUR in CAD". Check it is a normal rate for the currency (around 1.5 for EUR to CAD), not 1.0. Once the first box is received it reads "locked on the first receipt". |

Click <strong>Done</strong>. A part the receive can't go without (a date, a location, a rate) shows in amber until it's filled.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Why the exchange rate locks</div>
The rate on the day the stock lands is your cost truth. Later boxes on the same order reuse it, so your margins stay anchored to it however rates move afterwards.
</div>

## Count what's in this box

Each product card has a switch on the left, the photo and name, the supplier code and colour, and its sizes in a row. Under each size is what it still owes, for example "of 3".

- Every size starts at **0**. Type the count from the box.
- A size can't go past what it still owes: the box stops at that number.
- A size already received in full shows a tick and no box. Nothing more can be booked on it.
- **All N arrived**, on the right of a card, fills every size of that product to what it still owes. Use it when the product matches the order, then spot-check.
- Once you've typed something, the card shows your count against what's owed, for example "5 of 6", and <strong>Fill the rest</strong> tops up every size that is still short.

Under each size you'll see where it stands: "1 short", "+1 over", or "not ordered" for a size the order never asked for.

Tools above the list:

- **Find a style**: search by name, code or colour.
- <strong>All arrived</strong> fills every product in the box at once. Use it when the whole box matches the order.
- <strong>Clear counts</strong> empties what you typed. It shows once something is typed.

On a long order there's also <strong>All in this box</strong> (switch every product on or off, with a count such as "12 of 14") and three filters: **All**, **Needs you** (a delivery started and not finished) and **Left out**.

Switch a product off to leave it out of this box. It moves under **Left out** ("Switch on to receive") with what it still owes.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Two products that look the same</div>
If two products on the order share a name and colour, both carry a <strong>twin, check code</strong> marker. Match the supplier code on the garment before you switch one on.
</div>

### When the count doesn't match the order

- **Fewer than ordered:** enter what arrived. The rest stays owed and the order stays open.
- **More than the current order, but within the PO the supplier was sent:** ThreadCloud asks you to confirm ("Some quantities exceed the current draft but fit the issued PO."). Check the carton and the PO first.
- **A size you didn't order:** if the product already has that size in Shopify, it shows in the size run marked **not ordered**. Enter the count. It's kept separate, so it doesn't count against what the supplier still owes.

### Checks

When something in the box needs a second look, the dark bar shows <strong>Checks · N</strong>. Click it for the list, each with a button that jumps to the row:

- a size counted over what it owes;
- units on a size the order never asked for;
- a product not on Shopify, so its tags print without barcodes;
- two rows that read the same ("check the code");
- products left out that still owe units;
- the exchange rate this receipt will lock.

### Products not on Shopify yet

A line under the list says how many styles "aren't on Shopify yet". You can still book their stock: click <strong>Show them</strong> and switch a product on. Its card reads "Not on Shopify, tags print without barcodes". ThreadCloud keeps the count and sends it to Shopify when the product is pushed from Enrich (<strong>Open Enrich</strong>). A product marked **Needs Shopify** can't be received until it is pushed.

When nothing can be counted at all, a card says why ("Nothing can be received yet") with the one button that fixes it, for example <strong>Open Enrich</strong> or <strong>Open purchase order</strong>.

### Measurements and fit

Each card has a <strong>Fit</strong> button. It opens the product's fit and measurements; a tick beside it means measurements are on file, and **Unsaved** means you typed some and haven't saved. Measure the garment at the **Measured Size** shown (your store's sample size for that scale) and fill in what you can. When the product has a brand model, the brand's spec shows beside each field so you can spot a garment that's off. Measurements you save here appear on the product page's size chart as measured on the garment.

The fit selector records how the product runs: **-2 Very large**, **-1 Runs large**, **True to size**, **+1 Runs small**, **+2 Very small**. The card then shows it, for example "Fit: Runs small".

## Receive

Click <strong>Receive N units</strong> in the dark bar. The number is what you counted. While it works the bar reads "Threading shipment in". <strong>More</strong> beside it has <strong>Export CSV</strong> (a Shopify-format file of what you're about to receive) and <strong>Cancel</strong>.

When it lands, a panel reads "N units received" with one line under it, for example "6 lines · now in Main Store · 4 products live on POS · rate locked at 1.4823, costs updated in Shopify". Its buttons: <strong>Print N tags</strong>, <strong>Print receipt</strong> and <strong>Undo</strong>.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">If the panel warns you</div>
<strong>Shopify stock not confirmed on N units</strong>: the receipt is recorded, but Shopify may still show those sizes as sold out. Check them in Shopify before selling them (see <a href="/workflows/shopify-stock-sync/">Shopify stock sync</a>).<br />
<strong>Shopify kept the old cost on N items</strong>: use <strong>Re-push in Enrich to write cost again</strong>.<br />
<strong>N products not live on POS</strong>: use <strong>Make POS-ready</strong> (below).<br />
<strong>N units skipped on N lines</strong>: those sizes couldn't be linked safely. Push them in Enrich, reload, then receive only those.
</div>

Don't receive twice. If a receive matches one that is still running, the panel reads **Still going through**: wait a few seconds and reload, and don't count the box again. If it matches one already recorded, it reads **Already recorded** and nothing is booked a second time.

<strong>Undo</strong> pulls this box straight back out ("Pull this shipment back?"). Stock comes back out of Shopify and the receipt is removed for good. Click <strong>Pull it back</strong>, or <strong>Keep it</strong>.

## Print tags

Right after you receive, a window titled "N units received" opens. Tags print at 54 × 25 mm on the Dymo 30336, one per unit, in a new tab.

- <strong>Print both</strong>: tags, then the receiving document.
- <strong>Tags only</strong>
- <strong>Doc only</strong>
- <strong>Skip for now</strong>: you can print later from the receipt, from the order's close-out, or from the Receiving log.

To reprint the tags for one product, use <strong>Reprint tags</strong> on its card. Everything about printing, and what to do if the printer fails, is on <a href="/workflows/labels/">Print Labels</a>.

## Tag and shelve

Put each tag on its garment and move the stock to the floor or the stockroom.

## A shipment in more than one box

Receive what arrived, then come back to the same page when the rest lands. Count only what's in the new box; ThreadCloud adds it to what was already received. Each receive makes its own receipt.

## Paperwork

Under the list, **Paperwork** is one row of links: <strong>Purchase order</strong>, one <strong>Receipt 01</strong>, <strong>Receipt 02</strong> and so on for each box received, <strong>Return to vendor</strong> once anything is in, and <strong>Make POS-ready</strong> (owners and buyers).

## When the order is complete

Once every unit on the order is in, the top reads **Woven complete** with the units received, how much came in the first box, and whether it **Arrived** on time or how many days late. Under it, the dates of each box link to their receipts.

The close-out below shows only what is left to do:

| Tile | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Tags** | "16 tags from today's box". <strong>Print 16 tags</strong> prints them. |
| **Point of sale** | "N styles not POS-ready". <strong>Make POS-ready</strong> fixes them. |
| **Overs** | Units you received past what was ordered, by product and size. <strong>Start a return</strong> sends them back. |

A **record** panel for the brand shows how its recent orders arrived: how much came in the first box, and on time or days late. <strong>Show products</strong> opens the list again if you need to correct something.

## Make POS-ready

A received product should be sellable at the register. If a cashier can't find one, click <strong>Make POS-ready</strong> in **Paperwork** or on the close-out's **Point of sale** tile. It makes every received product on the order active and puts it on the Point of Sale channel again, then says how many are on POS, for example "6 of 6 products on POS".

It only touches products with received stock ("Receive a shipment first; products reach POS on receiving"). It can't be used to skip receiving.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Stock received before the first push</div>
When a product was received before it was pushed, its first push from Enrich makes it active and puts it on Point of Sale, but only if Shopify confirmed every size. If Shopify couldn't, the product stays a draft and Enrich says so: "Received before push but kept as a draft: Shopify could not confirm every size. Once a push confirms it, use Make POS-ready on the Receiving tab." Push again, then click <strong>Make POS-ready</strong> here. A store with no Point of Sale channel is left as it is.
</div>

## The receipt (receiving document)

A receipt is the permanent record of what was booked in that day. Open it from **Paperwork** (<strong>Receipt 01</strong> and so on), or from the **Received** tab of the Receiving log. The top shows the supplier, order, PO number and **FX Rate (locked)**, and each product lists **Ordered**, **Prev. Received**, **This Shipment** and **Total Received**, with a note when it is short or still outstanding.

Buttons: <strong>Export CSV</strong>, <strong>Print</strong> (the document), <strong>Reprint Labels</strong>, <strong>Reverse</strong> and <strong>Back</strong>.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">The receipt doesn't rewrite itself</div>
After you correct something, the receipt still shows what was booked that day. That's deliberate. To see where stock stands now, look at the order's <strong>Receive</strong> page, not the receipt.
</div>

## Fix a wrong size

Sometimes the size that lands doesn't match what's sewn into the garment, usually because the parse misread a size on a dense sheet. Trust the garment's label.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the receipt</strong> that booked it.</li>
  <li>On that product, click <strong>Correct</strong>. A dialog asks what is wrong.</li>
  <li><strong>Choose the fix</strong> from the options below.</li>
  <li><strong>Confirm</strong>, then reprint the tag for that unit. It now prints the right size.</li>
</ol>

| Option | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Received quantity** | You booked too many or too few. <strong>Receive more</strong> or <strong>Unreceive</strong> a quantity of one size. This makes a separate adjustment document and leaves the original intact. |
| **One mis-parsed size** | The size itself is wrong (a "45" that should be a "44"). Pick the **Wrong size**, pick the correct one, and click <strong>Fix size</strong>. The units move to the correct size, which is added to the product, and the wrong size comes off. |
| **Units on the wrong size** | Units were booked on the wrong one of two sizes the product already has. Click <strong>Move units</strong>. Only for products not yet in Shopify; for a product already in Shopify, use Received quantity instead. |
| **Whole size run** | The whole run is shifted or on the wrong scale. Shift every size, or change to another scale. Stock and product details stay attached. |
| **Unreceive entire style** | Take every unit of this product off this receipt. Other products stay received. |

You always pick sizes from a list that matches the product's scale, so there's no format to get wrong. If the correct size is already on the product, ThreadCloud stops before changing anything ("already exists on this style").

A fix on a product that isn't in Shopify yet is saved in ThreadCloud and goes to Shopify with the first push. The line says so ("Corrected here; syncs to Shopify at first push").

## Reverse a whole shipment

If a whole receipt was wrong, open it and click <strong>Reverse</strong>. The dialog tells you how many units across how many products it will take back out of stock, and that the receipt will be deleted. Click <strong>Reverse shipment</strong>. Shopify stock comes back down, and you can receive again with the right counts.

The order's locked exchange rate and its POS publishing stay as they are. Reversing is a stock correction, not a rewind of the order.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Reverse before anything sells</div>
If some of that stock has already sold, talk to the Owner before reversing. To fix one size, use <strong>Correct</strong> instead of reversing everything.
</div>

## Sending stock back

To return part of a delivery to the supplier, click <strong>Return to vendor</strong> in **Paperwork**, or <strong>Start a return</strong> on the close-out's **Overs** tile. See <a href="/workflows/transfer-out/">Transfer Out</a>.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Exchange rate at 1.0 on a foreign order</div>
If the order is in EUR and the rate reads 1.0, every cost is wrong. Fix the rate in Box details before you receive the first box. It locks after that.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Receiving a reorder against the original order</div>
A reorder is its own order, even if the supplier shipped it with the original. Receive each against its own order so your numbers stay right.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Unreceiving by hand to fix a size</div>
Don't unreceive a unit and receive it again on another size. Use <strong>Correct</strong> on the receipt so the record keeps where the units are.
</div>
