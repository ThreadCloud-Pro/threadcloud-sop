# Receiving

When the boxes arrive, you open the order's **Receive** tab, count what's in them against the order, and confirm the shipment. ThreadCloud books the stock into Shopify, locks the exchange rate, puts the products on your Point of Sale, and prints the hang tags. It's usually done at the receiving table with a tablet beside the open boxes.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
The day a buying order's shipment arrives. For a reorder, use the Receive tab inside the reorder (see <a href="/workflows/reorder/">Reorder</a>). For a custom order, see <a href="/workflows/mtm/">Custom orders</a>.

## What you'll need

- The boxes and the packing list
- The Dymo label printer loaded with 30336 labels (see <a href="/workflows/labels/">Print Labels</a>)
- A tape measure, if you're capturing measurements

## Quick path

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the order's Receive tab.</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Check the shipment details</strong>: date, reference, location and exchange rate.</li>
  <li><strong>Enter what's in the box</strong>, size by size, in <strong>Receiving Now</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Receive shipment</strong> (or <strong>Receive partial shipment</strong>).</li>
  <li><strong>Print</strong> the hang tags and the receiving document from <strong>Ready to print</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>Tag and shelve</strong> the goods.</li>
</ol>

## Stage 1: Find the order

Any of these gets you to the Receive tab:

- **Home, Sales floor desk.** Orders whose receiving is due show under **Needs you** with a <strong>Receive</strong> button. **Coming to the floor** shows incoming orders, soonest first, each with <strong>Open the haul</strong> and <strong>Receive</strong>.
- **Logistics, Receiving Log.** The **Expected** tab lists every order with a delivery window, its **Receipt %** and a status (for example **In Window** or **Past Window**). Click a row.
- **The order itself.** Click the <strong>Receive</strong> tab in the order header.

## Stage 2: Check the shipment details

The top of the page shows **Receiving into**, the shipment reference, and a running count, for example "112 of 186" with a percentage.

The **Tag the box** card ("Date, reference, where it lands.") holds:

| Field | What to check |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Today, unless you're booking a delivery late. |
| **Reference** | Filled in for you. Used on the receiving document and the tags. |
| **Location** | The Shopify location the stock goes into. |
| **Exchange rate** | Only on an order in another currency. Check it is a normal rate for the currency (for example around 1.5 for EUR to CAD), not 1.0. It reads "Locks on confirm" the first time and "Locked at ..." after that. |
| **Notes** | Anything worth remembering: a partial box, a damaged unit, a late ship date. |

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Why the exchange rate locks</div>
The rate on the day the stock lands is your cost truth. Later shipments on the same order reuse it, so your margins stay anchored to it however rates move afterwards.
</div>

If some products aren't in Shopify yet, a note says so. You can still book the stock now: ThreadCloud keeps the count and sends it to Shopify when each product is pushed from Enrich (<strong>OPEN PUSH QUEUE</strong> takes you there).

## Stage 3: Enter what's in the box

Each product shows its sizes in rows: **Size**, **Ordered**, **Prev. Rcvd** (received in earlier shipments), **Receiving Now** and **Outstanding**. Type the count from the box into **Receiving Now**.

Tools above the list:

- **Search** ("Search style, color, size") to find a product fast.
- <strong>Fill outstanding</strong> fills every outstanding quantity for the products showing. Use it when the box matches the order, then spot-check.
- <strong>Clear quantities</strong> empties what you typed.
- <strong>Include visible</strong> and <strong>Exclude visible</strong> tick or untick the products showing. Untick a product (its checkbox) to leave it out of this shipment.

The line under the tools tells you how many products are showing, receivable and included.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Two products that look the same</div>
If two products on the order share a name and colour, ThreadCloud warns you on the row. Match the supplier code on the garment before you tick it.
</div>

### When the count doesn't match the order

- **Fewer than ordered:** enter what arrived. The rest stays outstanding and the order stays open.
- **More than the current order, but within the PO the supplier was sent:** ThreadCloud asks you to confirm ("Some quantities exceed the current draft but fit the issued PO."). Check the carton and the PO first.
- **More than the PO:** refused. You can't receive more than was ordered.
- **A size you didn't order:** if the product already has that size in Shopify, it shows as its own row marked **not ordered**. Enter the count. It's kept separate, so it doesn't count against what the supplier still owes.

### Measurements and fit

Each product has a <strong>Measurements</strong> panel. Measure the garment at the **Measured Size** shown (your store's sample size for that scale) and fill in what you can. When the product has a brand model, the brand's spec shows beside each field so you can spot a garment that's off. Measurements you save here appear on the product page's size chart as measured on the garment.

The **Fit** selector records how the product runs: **-2 Very large**, **-1 Runs large**, **True to size**, **+1 Runs small**, **+2 Very small**.

## Stage 4: Confirm

Click <strong>Receive shipment</strong>. If not everything on the order has arrived, the button reads <strong>Receive partial shipment</strong>. <strong>Export CSV</strong> beside it downloads a Shopify-format file of what you're about to receive, if you need it.

When it lands, a **Receiving confirmed** banner shows the units and lines received, and:

- **FX locked at ...** the first time, with "Shopify costs updated".
- **N products now live on POS**. Your cashiers can sell them.
- Lines booked in ThreadCloud and queued for Shopify, if some products weren't pushed yet.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">If the banner warns you</div>
<strong>Shopify stock not confirmed on N units</strong>: the receipt is recorded, but Shopify didn't confirm the stock for the sizes named. Check those sizes in Shopify before selling them (see <a href="/workflows/shopify-stock-sync/">Shopify stock sync</a>).<br />
<strong>Shopify kept the old cost</strong>: push the order again from Enrich to write the cost.<br />
<strong>N products did not reach Point of Sale</strong>: use <strong>Make POS-ready</strong> (below).
</div>

Don't confirm twice. If a confirm matches one that is already running or already recorded, ThreadCloud says so and records nothing a second time. Wait a few seconds and reload.

## Stage 5: Print tags and the receiving document

Right after you confirm, **Ready to print** opens: "N hang tags for this shipment, plus the receiving doc for your records." Tags print at 54 × 25 mm on the Dymo 30336, one per unit, in a new tab.

- <strong>Print both</strong>: tags, then the receiving document.
- <strong>Tags only</strong>
- <strong>Doc only</strong>
- <strong>Skip for now</strong>: you can print later from the receipt or from Logistics.

To reprint the tags for one product, use the tag button on that product's row. Everything about printing, and what to do if the printer fails, is on <a href="/workflows/labels/">Print Labels</a>.

## Stage 6: Tag and shelve

Put each tag on its garment and move the stock to the floor or the stockroom. The Receive tab in the header then reads, for example, "186 of 186".

## A shipment in more than one box

Receive what arrived, then come back to the same Receive tab when the rest lands. Enter only what's in the new boxes; ThreadCloud adds it to what was already received. Each confirm makes its own receipt.

Past receipts are listed under **Shipment records** ("Received to date"), each with its reference, unit count, date and exchange rate. Click <strong>Open receipt</strong> to see one.

## The receipt (receiving document)

A receipt is the permanent record of what was booked in that day. Open it from **Shipment records**, or from **Logistics, Receiving Log, Shipments**. The top shows the supplier, order, PO number and **FX Rate (locked)**, and each product lists **Ordered**, **Prev. Received**, **This Shipment** and **Total Received**, with a note when it is short or still outstanding.

Buttons: <strong>Export CSV</strong>, <strong>Print</strong> (the document), <strong>Reprint Labels</strong>, <strong>Reverse</strong> and <strong>Back</strong>.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">The receipt doesn't rewrite itself</div>
After you correct something, the receipt still shows what was booked that day. That's deliberate. To see where stock stands now, look at the order's <strong>Receive</strong> tab, not the receipt.
</div>

## Fix a wrong size

Sometimes the size that lands doesn't match what's sewn into the garment, usually because the parse misread a size on a dense sheet. Trust the garment's label.

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Open the receipt</strong> that booked it.</li>
  <li>On that product, click <strong>Correct</strong>. A dialog asks what is wrong.</li>
  <li><strong>Choose the fix</strong> from the options below.</li>
  <li><strong>Confirm</strong>, then reprint the tag for that unit. It now prints the right size.</li>
</ol>

| Option | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Received quantity** | You booked too many or too few. <strong>Receive more</strong> or <strong>Unreceive</strong> a quantity of one size. This makes a separate adjustment document and leaves the original intact. |
| **One mis-parsed size** | The size itself is wrong (a "45" that should be a "44"). Pick the **Wrong size**, pick the correct one, and click <strong>Fix size</strong>. The units move to the correct size, which is added to the product, and the wrong size comes off. |
| **Units on the wrong size** | Units were booked on the wrong one of two sizes the product already has. Click <strong>Move units</strong>. Only for products not yet in Shopify; for a product already in Shopify, use Received quantity instead. |
| **Whole size run** | The whole run is shifted or on the wrong scale. Shift every size, or change to another scale. Stock and product details stay attached. |
| **Unreceive entire style** | Take every unit of this product off this receipt. Other products stay received. |

You always pick sizes from a list that matches the product's scale, so there's no format to get wrong. If the correct size is already on the product, ThreadCloud stops before changing anything ("already exists on this style").

A fix on a product that isn't in Shopify yet is saved in ThreadCloud and goes to Shopify with the first push. The line says so ("Corrected here; syncs to Shopify at first push").

## Reverse a whole shipment

If a whole receipt was wrong, open it and click <strong>Reverse</strong>. The dialog tells you how many units across how many products it will take back out of stock, and that the receipt will be deleted. Click <strong>Reverse shipment</strong>. Shopify stock comes back down, and you can receive again with the right counts.

The order's locked exchange rate and its POS publishing stay as they are. Reversing is a stock correction, not a rewind of the order.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Reverse before anything sells</div>
If some of that stock has already sold, talk to the Owner before reversing. To fix one size, use <strong>Correct</strong> instead of reversing everything.
</div>

## Make POS-ready

A received product should be sellable at the register. If a cashier can't find one, click <strong>Make POS-ready</strong> at the top of the Receive tab. It makes every received product on the order active and puts it on the Point of Sale channel again.

It only touches products with received stock. It can't be used to skip receiving.

## Sending stock back

If you need to return part of a delivery to the supplier, the **Returned to vendor** card on the Receive tab shows what has gone back so far. Click <strong>Transfer out / RTV</strong> to return stock from this order. See <a href="/workflows/transfer-out/">Transfer Out</a>.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Exchange rate at 1.0 on a foreign order</div>
If the order is in EUR and the rate reads 1.0, every cost is wrong. Fix the rate before you confirm the first shipment. It locks after that.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Receiving a reorder against the original order</div>
A reorder is its own order, even if the supplier shipped it with the original. Receive each against its own order so your numbers stay right.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Unreceiving by hand to fix a size</div>
Don't unreceive a unit and receive it again on another size. Use <strong>Correct</strong> on the receipt so the record keeps where the units are.
</div>
