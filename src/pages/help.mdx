# Help and Winston

Winston is ThreadCloud's Help desk. Ask him how to do anything in ThreadCloud and he answers from this manual. Anything he can't answer goes to Gregory, ThreadCloud's founder, and Gregory's reply shows up in your Help thread.

## Where to find him

- **On every screen.** Winston sits in the bottom-left corner. Click him to open his chat over the screen you're on. It stays open as you move between screens until you close it, so you can copy something from the screen into your question, or screenshot both together.
- **On Settings & Help.** The **Help** card at the top of **Settings & Help** has **Ask Winston**, which opens the same chat, and **Open Help**, which opens your whole Help thread on its own page.

## Asking a question

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click Winston in the corner, or <strong>Ask Winston</strong> on Settings &amp; Help.</li>
  <li>Type your question and press <strong>Enter</strong>. <strong>Shift</strong> and <strong>Enter</strong> starts a new line.</li>
  <li>Winston answers in a few seconds. If he can't answer, he says so and sends your question to Gregory.</li>
</ol>

## Winston reads your screen

When you ask from the chat in the corner, Winston also reads the text on the screen behind it, so he can see which order, product or warning you mean. He reads text only, not photos. It is used for that one answer and not kept.

To ask without showing him the screen, untick **Show Winston this screen** before you ask.

## When Gregory answers

Gregory answers on his phone, and his reply appears in your Help thread. When it lands, a red dot appears on Winston in the corner and the menu reads **Settings & Help (1)**. Open the chat or the Help page to read it.

## What Winston can't do

- He can't look up your store's numbers, orders or products beyond what's on your screen. Questions like "where is my order" go to Gregory.
- He doesn't change anything in your store. He only explains how.
