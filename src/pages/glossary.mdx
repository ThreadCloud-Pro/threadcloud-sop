# Glossary

Everything you'll see in ThreadCloud, translated into plain English. Bookmark this page.

## Core concepts

### Order
A buying order. One brand, one delivery window, one purchase order. Every order has a **season code** made from its delivery window and year, for example `FW-1-26` for Fall &amp; Winter Delivery 1 of 2026.

### Buying season
The group of orders for one selling season, for example **Fall &amp; Winter 2026** (code `FW-26`). ThreadCloud creates it the first time you build an order in that season, from the delivery window you pick. You plan it in **[Buying Plan](/planning/buying-seasons/)**.

### Delivery window
When an order is due to land, for example **Fall &amp; Winter Delivery 1** (`FW-1`, July to August). Windows are set in **[Delivery calendar](/settings/delivery-windows/)** and each one belongs to a season group.

### Style
One product within an order: one model in one colour. Each style has sizes.

### Variant
One size of one style. In Shopify, each variant has its own SKU and barcode.

### SKU
The unique code for one variant. ThreadCloud generates it, starting with the brand's 3-character code.

### Vendor Ref
The supplier's own style code, printed on the linesheet. Stored on the Shopify product as the supplier reference. **Never** confuse it with the SKU.

### Stock type
How a product comes back. Every product has one:

| Stock type | Means |
|---|---|
| **MTO** | New seasonal order. The supplier makes it for this store's buy. Every new seasonal order starts as MTO. |
| **STOCK** | Reordered this season. ThreadCloud changes MTO to STOCK when you reorder. Never set it yourself. |
| **NOS** | Never out of stock. An evergreen program your supplier backs indefinitely. The only type you set by hand. |
| **Special / Custom** | A one-off made for one customer, kept out of the normal reorder flow. |

The delivery window says when a product lands. The stock type says how it comes back. They are separate.

### Account owner
The person who owns the Shopify store. Some actions in ThreadCloud are for the account owner only, and the screen says so.

### Merchandising access
Permission to see **Buying Plan**, **Merchandising** and **Money**. The account owner always has it and grants it to staff in **[Team access](/settings/users-roles/)**.

## Workflow stages

### Parse &amp; Build
The first tab of an order. ThreadCloud reads the supplier's linesheet (PDF, spreadsheet or photo) into a table of styles, colours, sizes and prices that you check and correct.

### Enrich
The second tab. ThreadCloud drafts descriptions, fit notes, fabric, country of origin and the other product facts. You approve each product.

### Push to Shopify
Writes approved products to Shopify, with their sizes, photos and product facts.

### Receive
When the shipment arrives, you count each size received, record measurements and print labels.

### Budget
The fourth tab of an order: the order's planned cost against its brand allocation and the season plan.

### Reorder
A re-buy of products that are selling. It starts from your Shopify catalog and its costs.

## What an order row says

On [Home](/home/), each order shows its stage:

| Stage | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Building** | Still in Parse &amp; Build |
| **Enriching** | Product facts being reviewed and approved |
| **Push ready** | Approved styles waiting to go to Shopify |
| **Receiving** | In Shopify, with units still to arrive |
| **Live** | Everything ordered has been received |

## Push states on Enrich

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not pushed** | Not in Shopify yet |
| **Pushed but unverified** | In Shopify, but at least one ordered size is not linked to a Shopify variant. Receiving can't book that size yet. |
| **Pushed and verified** | In Shopify with every ordered size linked |

## Words on the figures

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sell-through** | Units sold as a share of units sold plus units left |
| **On hand** | Units physically in the store, from the daily count |
| **Available** | On hand that can still be sold, after units held for open Shopify orders |
| **Committed** (Stock) | Units held against open Shopify orders |
| **Committed** (Buying Plan) | Cost on orders whose PO has been sent |
| **Uncommitted** (Buying Plan) | Cost on orders still in build, PO not sent yet |
| **OTB** | Open to buy. The season budget not yet spent |
| **Recorded IMU** | The initial markup recorded on an order or season, from cost and retail |
| **Delivery fill** | Units received as a share of units ordered |
| **Weeks of cover** | How many weeks the current STOCK and NOS units last at the recent sell rate |
| **Withheld** | ThreadCloud will not show a number it cannot prove. The figure shows a mark instead, and the reason is in the notes at the foot of the page. |

A blank or withheld figure is not zero. It means the evidence for that number is not complete yet.

## Tags you'll see in Shopify Admin

Every product ThreadCloud pushes carries searchable tags, including:

- `tc-imported`: every product ThreadCloud pushed
- `tc-order-{id}`: every product from one order. Use it for batch work like reprinting labels for one order.
- the order's season code, for example `FW-1-26`
- `brand-{name}`, for example `brand-Acme`
- `category-{CATEGORY}`, for example `category-SUIT`

**Combine tags in Shopify Admin's product search:**
- `tag:tc-order-247`: only products from order 247
- `tag:tc-imported AND tag:brand-Acme`: every product ThreadCloud pushed for that brand

## Product facts in Shopify

ThreadCloud stores product facts on each Shopify product in its own groups (Shopify calls them metafields):

- **`tc_core`**: the main facts. Category, colour, fabric, fit, sizes and the facts your storefront filters read.
- **`tc_measure`**: garment measurements.
- **`tc_trade`**: supplier identifiers, such as the supplier reference.
- `shopify.*` and anything else not starting with `tc_`: **don't touch.** ThreadCloud doesn't write them either.

## Systems to know

- **Shopify Admin**: the store backend. ThreadCloud writes products and stock to it; you can always read from it.
- **Anthropic Claude**: the AI behind reading linesheets and writing descriptions. When it is down, those steps wait and retry.
