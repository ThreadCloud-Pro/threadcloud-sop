# First-time setup

Everything from installing ThreadCloud to a store that is ready to use. Setup is one guided walk in five parts. Each part is proven from your own store before ThreadCloud calls it done.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-01</div>
If a screen or button name here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Who does this</div>
The store's <strong>account owner</strong> (the person who owns the Shopify store) runs setup. Staff who open ThreadCloud during setup can watch progress but can't answer the questions; their screens say <strong>Only the account owner can answer this.</strong> Progress is saved as you go. You can close the tab and come back, and setup resumes where you left it.
</div>

## Before you start

- Owner access to your Shopify store.
- Your install link from ThreadCloud. ThreadCloud arranges the install with you directly.
- Optional: an Anthropic API key, so ThreadCloud can read linesheets and write product copy. You can add it during setup or later.
- Optional: your store logo.

## Install

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open the install link ThreadCloud sent you, signed in to your Shopify admin as the account owner.</li>
  <li>Approve the access ThreadCloud asks for.</li>
  <li>ThreadCloud opens full screen with the setup walk in place of Home.</li>
</ol>

## The setup rail

The left side of every setup screen is a rail with the five parts and a one-word status for each:

| Part | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **01 Connect** | ThreadCloud reads your store and catalog |
| **02 Brand** | Your logo, your description voice, your AI key |
| **03 Catalog** | ThreadCloud labels your existing catalog |
| **04 First workflow** | One real order, start to finish |
| **05 Wiring** | Sales history, stock, and storefront filters |

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Done** | Proven from your store |
| **Now** | The part you're on |
| **Later** | Not reached yet |
| **Held** | You skipped or set it aside. It waits for you. |
| **Attention** | Something needs fixing. The screen names the fix. |

Under the rail: **Your progress is saved. You can close this tab and come back.**

## 01 Connect: "Let ThreadCloud read your store."

### Welcome

<ol class="steps">
  <li>The first screen shows three things ThreadCloud unlocks: <strong>Smart filters</strong>, <strong>Buy analytics</strong>, <strong>58 data points</strong>.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Begin setup</strong>. It reads <strong>Warming the loom</strong> while it works.</li>
</ol>

**What Begin setup does:** installs ThreadCloud's product fact definitions in your Shopify store. No product data is touched.

### Catalog read

ThreadCloud reads your active Shopify catalog. A **Catalog read** panel shows progress. **Leaving this screen is safe. The read keeps its place.**

### What do you sell?

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Under <strong>What do you sell?</strong>, tick <strong>Menswear &amp; Accessories</strong>, <strong>Womenswear &amp; Accessories</strong>, or both. <strong>Tick all that apply.</strong></li>
  <li>Click <strong>Confirm</strong>. If your catalog already answers the question clearly, ThreadCloud states it instead of asking.</li>
  <li>If you answer while the read is still running, it says <strong>Saved. ThreadCloud applies it when the read lands.</strong></li>
  <li>Click <strong>Continue to brand setup</strong>.</li>
</ol>

## 02 Brand: "Make it sound and look like your store."

One screen, three blocks. **Choose a voice and confirm your mark. Skip if you like; you can return.**

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>01 Your mark</strong>: add the logo your team already uses. ThreadCloud prepares it for purchase orders without changing the original. When saved it reads <strong>MARK CONFIRMED</strong>.</li>
  <li><strong>02 Your voice</strong>: pick one of the prepared description voices. Click <strong>Preview on one of your products</strong> to see how it reads. Optional: <strong>Your website</strong> and up to three <strong>Inspiration</strong> sites. If you already have written guidance, click <strong>I already have detailed guidance</strong> to go to <a href="/settings/ai-models/">AI &amp; Pipeline</a>.</li>
  <li><strong>03 Your engine key</strong>: paste your Anthropic API key and click <strong>Save the key</strong>. The screen has a short <strong>How to get a key</strong> guide.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Confirm brand</strong>, or <strong>Skip for now</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">AI billing</div>
AI is billed to your own Anthropic account. ThreadCloud never marks it up. The store sets up without a key; you just won't get linesheet reading or product copy until one is saved. Add or change it any time in <a href="/settings/ai-models/">AI &amp; Pipeline</a>.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">A wrong key</div>
ThreadCloud saves the key without calling Anthropic, so a wrong key shows up the first time ThreadCloud writes copy, not on this screen. Anthropic keys start with <code>sk-ant-</code>.
</div>

## 03 Catalog: "Let ThreadCloud label your catalog."

ThreadCloud fills in what it can prove from your own store: what each product is, its fabric and care, construction, colour, sizes and season. **Anything it cannot prove it leaves exactly as you have it. Nothing is guessed, and nothing is deleted.**

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Optional: click <strong>Set your always-in-stock tags</strong> and enter the Shopify tags you use for NOS products, separated by commas. Click <strong>Save</strong>. Products with those tags are marked always-in-stock; everything else is a seasonal buy.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Continue</strong>. That is your whole part of this step.</li>
  <li>Labelling runs in the background. The screen says <strong>Labelling your catalog. Nothing needs your eye.</strong> You can leave the page.</li>
</ol>

If labelling finishes with some details that didn't go through, or stops, the screen says so and offers **Refine catalog** (and **Try again** where that will help). The full review lives in [Catalog setup](/settings/legacy-enrichment/).

## 04 First workflow: "Do the work once, for real."

One real order, parsed, ordered, enriched, pushed and received. The panel **Your first real buying cycle** lists six steps, each with its own button:

| Step | Button |
|---|---|
| **Create the first buying order** | **Create order** |
| **Read the first linesheet** | **Open Parse &amp; Build** |
| **Commit the purchase order** | **Open purchase order** |
| **Enrich and publish one product** | **Open Enrich** |
| **Confirm storefront filters** | **Check storefront filters** |
| **Receive the first shipment** | **Open receiving** |

Each step opens once the step above it is done. A finished step reads **Witnessed**. You can set a step aside for later. The workflow pages cover each step: [Parse & Build](/workflows/build-order/), [Enrich](/workflows/enrich/), [Push to Shopify](/workflows/push-to-shopify/), [Receiving](/workflows/receiving/).

## 05 Wiring: "Connect the numbers and the storefront."

Sales, stock and shopper filters, each proven from your store or named as waiting.

### Store readiness

The **Store readiness** panel has one card each for **Catalog**, **Catalog enrichment**, **Sales**, **Inventory** and **Storefront filters**. Each card shows **Ready**, **Ready with review**, **Needs attention**, **Stale** or **Not proven**, with a button to the fix where there is one. The badge at the top reads **CORE READY** or **ATTENTION**. You can keep working while attention items are repaired.

### Guided storefront setup: "Help shoppers narrow the rack"

Shopify's filter app is called Search &amp; Discovery. Follow the three numbered steps:

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>1. Install Search &amp; Discovery</strong>: the free app from Shopify. Click <strong>Open App Store</strong>. Skip this if it is already installed.</li>
  <li><strong>2. Add the filter sources</strong>: click <strong>Open Shopify filters</strong>, choose Filters, Add filter, then add each source in the list below the steps.</li>
  <li><strong>3. Check the live result</strong>: come back and click <strong>Check now</strong> (the first time it reads <strong>Connect checker</strong>). ThreadCloud checks Shopify directly.</li>
</ol>

The **Core filters** list names nine sources that must pass before setup can finish: Size, Color, Brand, Category, Type, Fit, Pattern, Material and Made In. A source that is wrong shows what to do: **Add this source**, **Use Variant scope**, or **Rename**. More sources are under **Add when relevant to your assortment**.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">The theme embed</div>
Your theme also needs to support filtering to show them. Optional: switch on the <strong>TC Smart Filters</strong> embed under App embeds in the theme editor (<strong>Open theme editor</strong>), then Save. See <a href="/settings/theme/">Storefront</a>.
</div>

## Ready: "Your store is set."

When every part is proven, the walk says **Your store is set.** From the Catalog part on, **Back to the desk** under the rail takes you to [Home](/home/). Until everything is proven, Home shows a quiet **Finish setting up** tile with the progress line, and the account owner may also see:

- **Confirm delivery calendar**: check the default delivery windows in [Delivery calendar](/settings/delivery-windows/), then click **This calendar is right**.
- **Finish storefront**: the theme embed and fit callout in [Storefront](/settings/theme/).
- **Refine catalog**: check what ThreadCloud found in your catalog.

## Starting setup over

<div class="callout callout-danger">
<div class="callout-title">Reset is for test stores only</div>
A <strong>Reset this walkthrough</strong> link appears under the setup rail only on stores ThreadCloud has set up for testing. On your live store you will not see it. If you think your live store needs to start setup again, email <a href="mailto:hello@threadcloud.io">hello@threadcloud.io</a>. Don't try to undo setup by hand in Shopify.
</div>

If a store reset was started, **Settings → Automations** shows a **Store reset** row until it finishes. See [If something breaks](/runbook/#store-reset).

## What to check when you're done

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Verification checklist</div>
- Store readiness reads <strong>CORE READY</strong>.
- The storefront shows filter facets with real values.
- <a href="/settings/store-health/">Settings store health</a> shows <strong>Current</strong> on Storefront filters, Catalog and Shopify sync.
- <a href="/settings/legacy-enrichment/">Catalog setup</a> shows your catalog as published.
- (If you saved an API key) a new order's linesheet reads in Parse &amp; Build.
</div>

## Common issues

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Descriptions or linesheet reading do nothing</div>
No Anthropic key saved. Add it in <a href="/settings/ai-models/">AI &amp; Pipeline</a>.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">The catalog read or labelling looks stalled</div>
Both run in the background and the bar at the top of the page shows progress. You can leave the page. If counts never move, open <a href="/settings/store-health/">Settings</a> and read the <strong>Catalog</strong> tile. Persistent issues: <a href="/runbook/">If something breaks</a>.
</div>
