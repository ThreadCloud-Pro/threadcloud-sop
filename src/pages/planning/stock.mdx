# Stock

The **Stock** tab in Merchandising answers "what do we hold?" It reads the daily count of your Shopify stock, so every row and the totals agree on one on-hand number. It replaced the old Inventory page: a saved Inventory link now opens Stock.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-01</div>
If a label here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
Before a reorder, before planning markdowns, and whenever someone asks "how much do we have of this brand?"
</div>

## Opening it

**Merchandising → Stock**, or the **Stock** link beside **Now** on the Overview or a brand page (that one opens already filtered to the brand).

Stock has no period. The header says when the count was taken: **As of today**, or the date of the latest count.

## Filters

**+ Filter** adds **Brand**, **Category** or one **Stock type**. If some products have no stock type, the stock type filter shows how many units are **Unclassified**, and you can filter to just those.

## The figures across the top

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **On hand** | Units in the store at the last count |
| **At cost** | Those units at cost |
| **At retail** | Those units at retail |
| **Available** | On hand you can still sell, after units held for open Shopify orders |

## The Stock table

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Pick the cut at the top: <strong>Brand</strong>, <strong>Department</strong> or <strong>Stock type</strong>.</li>
  <li>Type in the find box (<strong>Find a brand</strong>, and so on) to narrow the rows.</li>
  <li>Click a column heading to sort: <strong>Name</strong>, <strong>On hand</strong>, <strong>Available</strong>, <strong>Committed</strong>, <strong>Incoming</strong>, <strong>At cost</strong>, <strong>At retail</strong>.</li>
  <li>Click a row to open what is behind it: its categories, or its products with their pictures and links to the product in Shopify and on your storefront. Products load 50 at a time; click <strong>Show N more</strong> for the next.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>CSV</strong> to download the table as sorted and filtered.</li>
</ol>

The top row is the total. Rows that hold nothing are folded into one line (for example "3 brands hold nothing"); click it to show them. Service lines sit under their own **Services** divider.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Committed** | Units held against open Shopify orders |
| **Incoming** | Shopify's own incoming stock. This is not your open ThreadCloud purchase orders. |

## Age and Movement

| Panel | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Age** | Units by days since first receipt, as a bar and a list of bands |
| **Movement** | **Sell rate** (units a week), **Weeks of cover** and **Turns**, over the trailing 13 weeks. These cover STOCK and NOS only. A line under them gives the mix by units. |

## Notes and what is not yet available

The foot of the page has **Notes** (anything you should know about the figures), **Not yet available** (each figure that can't be stated yet, and what would clear it), and **Where these figures come from**.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Why Turns can be missing on a new store</div>
Turns needs 92 days of daily counts in a row before it shows. A new store sees it under <strong>Not yet available</strong> with the date it will publish. It clears on its own.
</div>

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Reading Incoming as your open orders</div>
Incoming is Shopify's incoming stock figure. For units still to come on ThreadCloud orders, use the order's <strong>Receive</strong> tab or Home's <strong>Coming to the floor</strong>.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Using Stock for a season question</div>
Stock is what you hold now, with no season. For how a season sold, use <a href="/planning/trading/#seasons">Merchandising → Seasons</a>.
</div>

## If the figures look wrong

- The count is taken nightly, with an hourly on-hand sweep. If the header date is old, open [Settings](/settings/store-health/) and read the **Nightly capture** tile.
- A product in the wrong brand or category is fixed on its source: Enrich for a ThreadCloud order product, [Catalog setup](/settings/legacy-enrichment/) for an older catalog product.
