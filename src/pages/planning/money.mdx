# Money

**Money** is the store's cash view: what came in, what went back, what it cost and what you kept, for any period. It is always the whole store. It replaces the old Financials pages.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-02</div>
If a figure name here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
Monthly close, quarterly review, and any time someone asks "what did we make last month?" It is also where Home sends you before your first buying season exists.
</div>

## Opening it

The **Money** tab in **Merchandising**. It needs Merchandising access.

The period opens on your **Opening period** from the Merchandising gear: **Last 30 days** or **Current season**. Change it with the period picker in the header:

| Group | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Seasons** | One buying season's selling window |
| **Quick ranges** | **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Year to date** |
| **Custom** | Two dates (both days count) and **Apply** |

Where the filters would be on other tabs, Money says **Store-wide**.

## The figures

| Figure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Gross revenue** | Merchandise value before discounts and refunds |
| **Discounts** | Shopify line discounts, in store currency |
| **Refunds** | Merchandise value given back on returns, before tax |
| **Net revenue** | Gross revenue less discounts and refunds |
| **Average transaction** | Net merchandise value per order |
| **Cost of goods sold** | Units sold, net of returns, at the cost frozen when they were received |
| **Gross margin value** | Net revenue (where the cost is known) less cost of goods sold |
| **Gross margin** | Gross margin value as a share of that net revenue |
| **GMROI, trailing 13 weeks** | Gross margin earned for each dollar of average STOCK and NOS inventory at cost |

Each figure has a fold that explains it. Open it for the **Source**, the **Window** it was counted over, and how the currencies were reconciled.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">These are merchandise figures, not your books</div>
Tax, duty, shipping and order-level charges are not in these figures. Use Money for buying and margin decisions, and your accounting system for the books.
</div>

## Exports

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Set the period first. The export carries the period on the screen.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>CSV</strong> beside <strong>Store-wide</strong> for every figure with its basis and coverage.</li>
  <li>Or click <strong>CSV evidence</strong> on one figure for the Shopify order lines behind it. The contribution column adds up to the figure on screen.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">For the accountant</div>
Send the CSV, not a screenshot. It keeps raw numbers rather than rounded ones, and it marks a blank figure as blank rather than zero.
</div>

## Blank and withheld figures

- A figure that can't be counted from the evidence shows a mark, with the reason. **A blank is not zero.**
- **GMROI** needs 92 days of daily stock counts in a row. On a new store it waits, and says when it will publish.
- If the figures changed while an evidence CSV was being prepared, ThreadCloud refuses the file rather than mixing two readings. Refresh the page and ask again.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Comparing with a report that counts differently</div>
Money counts merchandise value, nets refunds, and uses ThreadCloud's own period dates. A Shopify sales report with different rules will not match to the dollar. Use the CSV to see exactly what was counted.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Comparing a finished period with a running one</div>
A season that is six weeks in and a finished one will look very different. Compare like with like, for example the same months a year apart.
</div>
