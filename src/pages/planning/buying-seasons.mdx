# Buying Plan

**Buying Plan** in the ThreadCloud menu is the buyer's main planning workspace. It lists every buying season, and each season opens to its plan, its brands, its delivery cost and a comparison with an earlier season.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-01</div>
If a tab or tile name here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Who sees it</div>
Buying Plan needs Merchandising access. The account owner always has it and grants it to staff in <a href="/settings/users-roles/">Team access</a>.
</div>

## How buying seasons are created

You don't create a buying season by hand. ThreadCloud creates it the first time you build an order in it:

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On <strong>New buying order</strong> you pick a delivery window and year, for example Fall &amp; Winter Delivery 1, 2026.</li>
  <li>The window belongs to a season group in your <a href="/settings/delivery-windows/">Delivery calendar</a>, here Fall &amp; Winter.</li>
  <li>ThreadCloud files the order under the buying season for that group and year, <strong>Fall &amp; Winter 2026</strong> with the code <code>FW-26</code>, and creates the season if it is the first order in it.</li>
</ol>

So the delivery calendar is what shapes your seasons. Set it up before the first appointment of the year.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">No buying seasons yet</div>
An empty Buying Plan reads <strong>No buying seasons yet.</strong> Orders created before a delivery calendar existed can be rolled into seasons from <strong>Settings → Delivery calendar → Buying Order Backfill</strong>.
</div>

## The Buying Plan list

Seasons are grouped by status: **In planning**, **Buying**, **Receiving** and **Closed**. The top right shows the **portfolio planned at cost** across every season, and the total **planned**.

Each season card shows:

| Line | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Code · year, then the name | For example `FW-26 · 2026`, Fall &amp; Winter 2026 |
| Planned at cost | What the season's orders cost so far. **not yet started** before any order has a cost. |
| Counts | Orders, brands and units in the season |
| **Recorded IMU** and **% received** | Markup recorded so far, and how much of the buy has landed |
| Variance chip | **N% used · room** (under 90% of plan), **close** (90 to 100%), **over** (100% or more), or **plan unset** |

Click a card to open the season.

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">"needs Stock Type"</div>
If a season shows <strong>needs Stock Type</strong>, or the portfolio total reads <strong>withheld</strong>, some styles have no stock type yet. Open budget figures are held back until they do. Set the stock type on Enrich. See the <a href="/glossary/#stock-type">glossary</a>.
</div>

## The season page

### Tiles across the top

| Tile | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Total OTB** | The season's open-to-buy target. Click it to set or change it. See [Budget &amp; OTB](/planning/budget-otb/). |
| **Planned at cost** | What the orders cost, with the share of OTB used. The line under it splits **PO sent** from **in build**, then shows what is still open. |
| **Locked pre-season** | The share of the OTB placed as seasonal (MTO) buys. The target is 70% or more; reorders through the season cover the rest. |
| **Planned cost, next 30 days** | Cost of orders whose delivery starts in the next 30 days. A warning line shows any cost with no delivery date. |

If the season's orders cost more than its OTB, a warning reads **Planned-at-cost allocation is over the OTB target by** that amount.

### The four tabs

| Tab | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Plan** | Category, subcategory and brand budgets against what you have placed. Covered on [Budget &amp; OTB](/planning/budget-otb/). |
| **By Brand** | The same figures turned brand first: brand, then category, then subcategory. |
| **Delivery Cost** | Planned cost by month of delivery start, with **Next 30 days by delivery start**, **Dated planned cost** and **No delivery date**. Use it to see cash going out before it goes. |
| **Compare** | Pick a season under **Compare against** and click **Compare**. Shows the season-over-season comparison and the **Category Mix Comparison**. |

**Refresh** reloads the numbers without leaving the tab you're on.

### A brand inside a season

Click a brand name on **Plan** or **By Brand** to open that brand's page for the season. It lists the brand's orders in the season (order, **PO #**, **Delivery**, **Status**, units, styles, **Planned at cost**, **Recorded IMU**), its plan by category and subcategory, and the brand's people and supplier.

## When to use it

- **Before the season's first appointment**: set the Total OTB and the category plan. See [Budget &amp; OTB](/planning/budget-otb/).
- **Before every appointment**: check what's left in the categories you're about to buy.
- **Weekly during buying**: watch **PO sent** against **in build**, and the variance chips.
- **Once product is selling**: use [Merchandising → Seasons](/planning/trading/#seasons) for how the season is selling.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Picking the wrong delivery window</div>
The delivery window decides which buying season an order belongs to. Choose it carefully on a new order. If a delivery moves, change the order's delivery window rather than starting a new order.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Reading a partly costed season as complete</div>
When some units have no cost yet, totals cover only the costed units and say so ("Totals cover N of M units"). Variance chips show a dash instead of a percentage. Finish costing before trusting the variance.
</div>
