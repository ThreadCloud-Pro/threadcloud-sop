# Brand page and letter

Every brand in Merchandising has its own page: how it sold in the period, how its season is going, what is on hand now, and every product you bought from it. Its **Letter** tab turns that into a page you can share with the brand.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-01</div>
If a tab or label here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

## Opening a brand

- From **Merchandising → Overview**, click a brand name in the **Brands** table.
- Or use the search in the Merchandising header and pick a brand.

The header reads **Merchandising › Brand name**, and **Overview** stays marked in the tab row. The period picker works as on the Overview. See [Merchandising](/planning/trading/#the-period-picker).

## Performance

The **Performance** tab is the default.

### The figures

| Group | Figures |
|---|---|
| The period you picked | **Units sold**, **Net sales**, **Gross margin** |
| The season | **Sell-through**, **Delivery fill**. **Season page** opens the season in Merchandising. |
| **Private to you** (click to open) | **Season net sales**, **Season margin**, **Plan** (the brand's target, OTB left and OTB used) |
| **Now** | **On hand**, **Cover** (weeks). **Stock** opens the Stock tab filtered to this brand. |

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Private to you</div>
The figures under the lock are your own finance and plan. They never appear on the Letter.
</div>

### Products

Every style you bought from the brand. Columns: **Product**, **Ref**, **Landed**, **Delivery**, **Received**, **Sold**, **Left**, **Sell-through**, **Last sale**. Rows show the product picture and link to the product in Shopify and on your storefront where those exist. Product sell-through is units sold over units sold plus units left.

### The rail and Sizes

**The rail** shows the brand's products as cards, ranked for the period. **Sizes** shows the season's sold size curve. Pick a season to see it.

### Export and more

- **Export CSV** downloads the brand's figures.
- **⋯** opens **Brand history** and **Performance drill-down** for this brand.

## The Letter

The **Letter** tab prepares a page for the brand: what sold, sizes, sell-through and delivery, for the period you picked. The tab label shows the letter's state, and the **Letter** column in the Overview's Brands table reads **Draft** or **Shared**.

The left side, **What Brand will see**, is the preview. The right side holds every action, in the order you take them.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Pick the period in the period picker. The heading reads <strong>Letter · period</strong>.</li>
  <li>Under <strong>Sections</strong>, tick what the brand should see: <strong>Products, sizes, sell-through</strong>, <strong>Delivery</strong>, <strong>Sales</strong>, <strong>Margin</strong>.</li>
  <li>Under <strong>Preview</strong>, click <strong>Preview letter</strong>. Read the preview on the left as the brand will see it.</li>
  <li>Under <strong>Share</strong>, click <strong>Create share link</strong>. Then <strong>Copy link</strong> and send it to the brand, or <strong>Open shared letter</strong> to check it. The link shows when it expires.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Margin is your choice</div>
The <strong>Margin</strong> section shares margin with the brand. Leave it unticked unless you mean to share it.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Changed a section after previewing</div>
If you change the sections after previewing, the screen says <strong>Refresh preview before creating this share link.</strong> Click <strong>Refresh preview</strong> first.
</div>

### Versions and links

- **Versions** lists every letter you shared for the period, with its status: **Active**, **Superseded**, **Expired** or **Disabled**. **Show share link** recovers the link for the active version.
- A link lasts 90 days. Sharing a new version supersedes the old one.
- **Disable this period's links** turns off the links for this period.
- **Disable every link for** the brand turns off all of them. It asks you to confirm; **Keep them** cancels.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Sharing a period that isn't finished</div>
The letter states figures for the period you picked. A period that is still running will change after you share it. Share a finished month or season, or say so to the brand.
</div>
