# Merchandising

The selling-window operations surface — was called <strong>Trading</strong>, renamed and rebuilt around six tabs. Once inventory lands, Merchandising tells you what's selling, what's aging, and what to act on this week.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-07-10</div>
If a tab or card name here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale — tell the team.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
Weekly. Monday morning is the natural cadence — review prior week's sales, identify reorder candidates, identify markdown candidates.
</div>

## The six tabs

Sidebar → Merchandising. Across the top: <strong>Overview</strong>, <strong>Brands</strong>, <strong>Categories</strong>, <strong>Orders</strong>, <strong>Buying Seasons</strong>, <strong>Inventory Snapshot</strong>.

## Overview

The home tab, scoped to one buying season at a time. Six cards:

- <strong>Open to Buy</strong> — committed spend against target, split into seasonal buy vs reorder spend, with a concentration flag if you're overweight in one place.
- <strong>Sell-Through</strong> — % sold of what's landed, either for this season or across your whole portfolio.
- <strong>Act Now</strong> — one merged queue of markdown candidates, reorder candidates, and margin flags, so you aren't checking three separate lists.
- <strong>Delivery Watch</strong> — a progress bar of what's actually landed against what's on order.
- Two cards marked <strong>Coming</strong> (Size Curve Alarm, Margin Truth) — placeholders for features not shipped yet. Safe to ignore for now.

Click any row in Act Now to jump straight to acting on it (reorder or markdown).

## Brands

A whole-store scorecard, not scoped to one season: total brand count, gross sales, sell-through, and on-hand value at the top, then a sortable table of every enriched brand with its gross margin % and year-over-year sell-through trend. Use this to compare brands against each other, not just within one season's buy.

## Categories

The same scorecard shape as Brands, grouped by category instead — gross margin and trend per category, whole-store.

## Orders

Per-order buy detail — unchanged from before the rebuild.

## Buying Seasons

Season and OTB planning — unchanged from before the rebuild.

## Inventory Snapshot

Was called <strong>Assortment</strong>. On-hand inventory (received minus sold), in three cuts on one page:

- <strong>Headline table</strong> — on-hand units, cost, retail, margin %, and style count, grouped by Brand, Category, or Subcategory (toggle at the top). If a snapshot predates retail-price capture, the retail/margin columns show a prompt to refresh instead of a dash.
- <strong>By size</strong> — a bar chart per category showing on-hand units across that category's full size run (different scales for the same category, e.g. raw EU sizes vs reorder sizes, are normalized onto one consistent scale so the curve reads cleanly).
- <strong>By colour</strong> — on-hand units and value by colour group; any colour holding 40%+ of a category's value is flagged <strong>Concentrated</strong> — a markdown-exposure warning.

Filter by Brand, Category, and Season at the top of the page — the filters are in the URL, so a filtered view is bookmarkable and shareable.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Refreshing the snapshot</div>
Click <strong>Refresh catalog</strong> to pull current on-hand counts from Shopify. It runs in the background with a live progress bar and ETA (an ambient-bar notice shows "X of Y variants" as it goes) — you can navigate away and it keeps running. If a refresh gets stuck, the lock auto-clears after a few minutes and you can just click Refresh again; there's no need to wait it out or ask for a manual reset.
</div>

## Season scoping

Overview, Brands, and Categories share the same season picker (a row of season pills, plus "All" on the scorecard tabs — Overview always needs one specific season). Inventory Snapshot's Season filter is a dropdown alongside Brand and Category instead of pills, but does the same job.

## Sync schedule

Sell-through numbers sync from Shopify orders automatically every 6 hours in the background. If the numbers look stale, trigger a manual sync from <strong>Settings → AI & Models → "Sync sell-through now"</strong> — should complete in under a minute. On-hand inventory (Inventory Snapshot) is separate: it only updates when you click <strong>Refresh catalog</strong> on that tab.

## Common patterns

### Monday morning review

15 minutes:

1. Open Merchandising → Overview for the current season.
2. Check <strong>Act Now</strong> — work top to bottom: reorder, markdown, and margin flags all live here now.
3. Check <strong>Delivery Watch</strong> for anything that should have landed and hasn't.
4. Switch to <strong>Sell-Through</strong> — sort for the top and bottom performers.
5. If a brand or category looks off across the whole store (not just this season), check the <strong>Brands</strong> or <strong>Categories</strong> tab for the wider trend.

That's the week's tasks set.

### End-of-season post-mortem

After a season closes, use:

- <strong>Brands</strong> / <strong>Categories</strong> — whole-store scorecards for next year's buying plan (lean into high sell-through, high-margin performers).
- <strong>Inventory Snapshot</strong> — what's still sitting, by size and by colour, to plan markdowns before the next delivery window.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Acting on stale sell-through</div>
If the last sync ran a few hours ago and a big batch of orders came through, the numbers underrepresent reality. Hit "Sync now" before acting on close-to-the-line numbers.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Reordering off sell-through alone</div>
A high-sell-through style that's still well-stocked doesn't need a reorder yet — cross-check against Open to Buy and Delivery Watch before committing more spend.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Comparing a season-scoped number against a whole-store one</div>
Overview's cards are scoped to one season; Brands and Categories are whole-store. A brand that looks flat on this season's Overview can still be a strong performer whole-store on the Brands tab — check both before writing a brand off.
</div>
