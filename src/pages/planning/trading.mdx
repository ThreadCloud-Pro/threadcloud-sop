# Merchandising

**Merchandising** in the ThreadCloud menu answers "how are we selling, and what needs attention?" It has four tabs, each with one job:

| Tab | Its job |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | How the store is doing this period, and which brands need attention |
| **Seasons** | How one buying season has performed over its life |
| **Stock** | What you hold, what it is worth, how old it is and how fast it moves. See [Stock](/planning/stock/). |
| **Money** | Store financials for a period. See [Money](/planning/money/). |

## The header, on every tab

- **Left**: the page title, or a breadcrumb like **Merchandising › Brand** on a brand page.
- **Right**: the **period picker**, a search button, and a gear for your settings.
- **Below**: the four tabs, and **⋯** for the older reports.

### The period picker

One button that states the period in words, for example **Last 30 days** or **FW-26 season**. Click it to choose:

| Group | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Seasons** | Your buying seasons. Choosing one reads the page over that season. |
| **Quick ranges** | **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Year to date** |
| **Custom** | Two dates (both days count) and **Apply** |

When you pick a date range, a second button beside it names the season shown next to the period (**Season beside** the period). The choice is saved in the page address, so a view can be bookmarked or shared.

### Filters

Click **+ Filter** to add a filter for **Brand**, **Category** or **Stock type**. Each filter becomes a chip. Click a chip to change it, or **×** to remove it. Stock type options are **Seasonal MTO**, **Seasonal Stock**, **Never Out of Stock** and **Special Orders**, plus **Unclassified** for products with no stock type.

### Search and the gear

- **Search** finds a brand, product or order. A brand result opens that brand's page.
- **The gear** (**Merchandising settings**) holds your **Home shortcut** (whether the Merchandising tile on Home opens **Merchandising** or **Money**), your **Opening period** for Money (**Last 30 days** or **Current season**), and the store's **Season calendar**: when **Spring &amp; Summer** and **Fall &amp; Winter** start and end, as month and day. Only the store owner can change the season calendar.

## Overview

### The figures

The figures are grouped by what they are counted over, with one heading per group:

| Group | Figures |
|---|---|
| The period you picked | **Net sales**, **Units sold** |
| The season | **Sell-through**, **Delivery fill**. **Season page** links to the season. |
| **Now** | **On hand**. **Stock** links to the Stock tab. |

If no buying season exists yet, the page says **No buying season is ready.** and offers **See whole-store numbers** (Money) and **Open Buying Plan**.

### Brands

The store table: one row per brand, the heart of the Overview.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Brand** | Click to open the [brand page](/planning/brand-page/) |
| **Sold**, **Net sales** | In the period you picked |
| **Sell-through**, **Fill** | Over the season's life |
| **On hand**, **Cover** | Now. Cover is weeks of cover, for STOCK and NOS only. |
| **Letter** | The state of the brand letter for the period. See [Brand page](/planning/brand-page/#the-letter). |

- **Filter brands** narrows the list as you type.
- **Sort brands** by **Sold**, **Net sales** or **Season sell-through**.
- **Show all N brands** shows the full list.
- **CSV** downloads the table as you see it.

### The rail

**The rail** shows products as cards with their picture, sorted by **Sold**, **Left**, **Velocity**, **Margin** or **Age**. Each card links to the product in Shopify admin and on your storefront where those exist.

### Breakdown

One row per **Category**, **Subcategory** or **Stock type** (pick the cut at the top right). Columns: **Units sold**, **Net sales**, **Units left**, **On hand at cost**, **Sell-through**. Click a column heading to sort. On the Category cut, click a row's arrow to open its subcategories. Click a row's name to filter the whole page to it. **CSV** exports the rows in the order you sorted them. On a date range, the breakdown is by **Season** only.

### Sizes

Sold size curves per category, so you can see which sizes sell and which sit. **Check these sizes** flags sizes worth a look.

### Notes

Any figure ThreadCloud can't prove shows a mark instead of a number. The reason, and what would clear it, is in the **Notes** at the foot of the page. A withheld figure is not zero.

## Seasons

One buying season over its whole life. Pick the season in the period picker. Filters: Brand, Category, and one Stock type.

| Chapter | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **01 The season in figures** | **Net sales**, **Net units**, **Sell-through**, **Gross margin**, **Received** |
| **02 Sell-through by cohort, category and brand** | Cut by **cohort** (stock type), **category** or **brand**. Columns include **Net units**, **Net sales**, **Sell-through** and **Shortfall**. Rows link to the brand's **scorecard**. **CSV** exports it. |
| **03 The delivery drill** | Each order in the season: **Order**, **Delivery window**, **Ordered**, **Received**, **Fill** |
| **04 Carryover at cost** | Current STOCK and NOS at cost. This one is not limited to the season. |
| **05 Not published, and why** | Every figure that is withheld, with its reason |

If no order exists for the season yet, the delivery drill says so. If the store has no buying season, the page reads **No season to read.**

## More reports (⋯)

The **⋯** at the end of the tab row opens the older reports. They keep their own tab row.

| Report | Use it for |
|---|---|
| **Performance overview** | The earlier whole-store summary |
| **Brand history** | Brand scorecards over every season |
| **Category history** | Category scorecards over every season |
| **Order scorecards** | One row per order: styles, units, **Planned at cost**, **Recorded retail**, **Recorded IMU%**, **OTB Var**, **Receipt%**, status |
| **Performance drill-down** | Product-level performance and current inventory together |

## Where the numbers come from

- **Sales** come from your Shopify orders. ThreadCloud syncs orders every hour. See **Settings → Automations**.
- **On hand** comes from a nightly count of your Shopify stock, plus an hourly sweep.
- **Season** figures use the season calendar in the gear.

If numbers look stale, open [Settings](/settings/store-health/) and read the **Shopify sync** and **Nightly capture** tiles.

## Monday morning review

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Merchandising</strong>. Set the period to <strong>Last 30 days</strong> or the current season.</li>
  <li>Read the <strong>Brands</strong> table. Sort by <strong>Season sell-through</strong> and look at the top and bottom.</li>
  <li>Open a brand that looks off for its products and sizes.</li>
  <li>Check <strong>Stock</strong> for what is old or not moving.</li>
  <li>Reorder what is working from <a href="/workflows/reorder/">Reorder</a>.</li>
</ol>

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Comparing a period figure with a season figure</div>
Net sales and Units sold are for the period you picked. Sell-through and Delivery fill are for the season's whole life. The group headings say which is which. Don't read a season figure as if it covered last month.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Reordering off sell-through alone</div>
A style with high sell-through that is still well stocked doesn't need a reorder yet. Check On hand and Cover first.
</div>
