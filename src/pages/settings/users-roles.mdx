# Team access

ThreadCloud uses your Shopify staff accounts, but opening the app is not enough. **A staff member sees nothing in ThreadCloud until the account owner lets them in.** The owner then picks a level for them, and each level opens only the screens it needs.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-11-09</div>
If a screen or label here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

## Who is who

| Person | What they get |
|---|---|
| **Account owner** | The person who owns the Shopify store. Everything, always. Only the account owner can open **Team access**. |
| **Staff, let in** | Whatever the owner switched on for them (levels and switches below). |
| **Staff, waiting** | Someone on your Shopify staff who has opened ThreadCloud but has not been let in. They see one line: **Ask [the owner's name] to invite you to ThreadCloud.** Nothing else in ThreadCloud opens for them. |

Staff who were already using ThreadCloud before this change stayed in, with Sell, Receive and Buy on. Their row may read **Buyer** or **Custom**. Check each one and set the level you want.

## Let someone in

To give someone access, first add them as staff in Shopify and give them the ThreadCloud app there. They show up in ThreadCloud the first time they open it.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>The account owner opens <strong>Settings → Store &amp; team</strong>. The first tab is <strong>Team access</strong>.</li>
  <li>Under <strong>Waiting to be let in</strong>, find the person. It shows their name, email and when they last tried.</li>
  <li>Pick a level in the list beside their name. It starts on <strong>Floor</strong>, the least.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Let in</strong>.</li>
</ol>

Nobody waiting reads **Nobody is waiting.** Under the list the page says **Staff show up here the first time they open ThreadCloud from the Shopify admin.**

After you click, the page says one of:

- **[Name] is in. Email isn't set up yet, so let them know.** Tell the person yourself that they can open ThreadCloud now. This is the line to expect today.
- **[Name] is in. The welcome email is on its way.** Shown only where ThreadCloud's staff email is set up for the store.

Their next page load opens ThreadCloud.

## The four levels

A level is a starting point. Choosing one sets the person's switches. You can change any switch afterwards, and a mix that matches no level reads **Custom**.

| Level | What it is for | Switches on |
|---|---|---|
| **Floor** | Sells: scans tags, finds clients and holds sizes on the phone, takes special orders. | Sell, Supplier updates |
| **Receiver** | Receives at the desk and on the phone, fixes receipts, prints tags. | Receive, Supplier updates |
| **Buyer** | Orders, Enrich, purchase orders, reorders and Merchandising, plus receiving and selling. | Sell, Receive, Buy, Merchandising, Supplier updates |
| **Manager** | Everything a buyer does, plus store settings. | All six, **Store settings** included |

## Change a switch or a level

On **Settings → Store &amp; team → Team access**, each person you have let in has a level list, a **Remove** button and six switches. Each switch reads **On** or **Off**.

| Switch | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Sell** | Custom orders and clients' fit, and the phone's Sell tab. |
| **Receive** | The Receiving log, an order's Receive page and receipts, tags, Transfer Out, and the phone's Receive tab. |
| **Buy** | Buying orders, reorders, Photography, supplier answers, Correct season, the Network, and the phone's Shoot tab. |
| **Merchandising** | Buying Plan, Merchandising and Money. |
| **Supplier updates** | The **Seen** buttons on Home's supplier updates. Without it the person still sees every update. |
| **Store settings** | Change store-wide settings like pricing, labels and catalog rules. |

The Logistics page opens for anyone with Sell, Receive or Buy, and shows each part to the switch it belongs to. Home, Help and the two personal settings pages (My preferences, and Phones where the phone app is on) open for everyone let in. Anyone without Buy or Store settings is sent to one of those two from the Settings front door instead of the Settings hub.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When someone opens a screen their switches don't cover</div>
They land on Home with a line such as <strong>Receiving is not turned on for your account. Ask the store owner to turn it on in Settings › Team access.</strong> The line names the switch: Selling, Receiving, Buying, Merchandising or Store settings.
</div>

A switch change takes effect on the person's next page load.

## What Floor and Receiver do not see

Floor and Receiver do not see what the store pays: unit costs, margins and supplier prices are left out of the Receiving page and receipts, Transfer Out, custom orders and Home's figures. Retail prices stay, because they are on the tag. Buyer, Manager (anyone with Buy or Merchandising) and the owner see costs.

A first receipt on an order in another currency locks the exchange rate. Only someone with **Buy** can type that rate. For anyone else the Receive page shows a line like **1 EUR = 1.5705 CAD, today's rate. It locks when you receive.** If ThreadCloud could not fetch the rate, it says so and asks them to try again in a minute or ask a buyer to enter it.

## Take someone out

Click **Remove** beside the person. ThreadCloud asks **Take [name] out of ThreadCloud? They see nothing until you let them in again, and their phones are signed out.** Confirm, and they go back to waiting. The account owner can never be taken out.

Remove staff who leave in ThreadCloud and in Shopify. Access follows the Shopify staff account too.

## Actions for the account owner only

Some actions change the whole store or reach into Shopify, so ThreadCloud keeps them for the account owner, whatever level a person has. Where a staff member would see one, the screen says who can do it, for example **Store account owner only** or **Only the account owner can answer this.** They include:

- Everything on **Team access**.
- **Settings → Claude**: connecting Claude. See [Connect Claude](/settings/claude/).
- Answering the setup walk's questions and finishing setup. See [First-time setup](/setup/onboarding/).
- Testing, replacing or clearing the AI key. See [AI &amp; Pipeline](/settings/ai-models/).
- Saving **Store codes** (see [Catalog setup](/settings/legacy-enrichment/#store-codes)).
- **Order qty to qualify as Essential** in Storefront (see [Storefront](/settings/theme/#essentials-page)).
- Changing the store's season calendar in the Merchandising gear.
- **Run the filter check** on the Storefront filters health line, and **Receiving readiness** in Settings.
- The sales history recovery buttons and **Resume the reset** in **Settings → Connections &amp; sync**. See [Settings and store health](/settings/store-health/).
- **Barcode check** and **Brand check** on the Custom orders list. See [Custom orders](/workflows/mtm/).

## Read-only stores

If a screen says your shop role is read-only, ThreadCloud has set the whole store to view only. Email [hello@threadcloud.io](mailto:hello@threadcloud.io).

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Letting everyone in as Manager</div>
Start people on the least they need. Money shows the store's sales, costs and margin, and Store settings changes pricing, labels and catalog rules.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Forgetting to tell the person they are in</div>
Where the page says email isn't set up, nobody is told for you. Let them know yourself.
</div>
