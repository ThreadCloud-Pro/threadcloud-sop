# Network

**Network** in the ThreadCloud menu (the page itself is titled **Brand network** in the new design) is the directory of every brand you carry, the suppliers who bill you, and the agents who introduce brands. Each brand opens to its **brand record**: its facts, its fit, its photography and its people.

## How brands, suppliers and agents fit together

- A **brand** is what shows on the storefront.
- A **supplier** is who bills you: the entity on the invoice. A brand points to its supplier.
- An **agent** is the firm that introduces a brand to your store, such as a sales agency or showroom. Optional.
- **Biller pending** on a brand means its supplier isn't set yet. That's normal for an agent-introduced brand until you know who invoices.

## The registry

Three tabs: **Brands**, **Suppliers**, **Agents**. In the new design the search box comes first on the page, with the **Add** button for the tab you are on beside it. One search finds brands, codes, suppliers and people at once (it reads "Search brands, suppliers, people" on **Brands**, and the matching words on the other two tabs). There is no row of counts above the list: the tab names the total, and a count appears over the list only while a search or filter narrows it.

Each brand tile shows its code, its name, **· inactive** if it is no longer active, **biller pending** if it has no supplier, and **Foundation ✓** or **Draft** for the state of its brand facts. Click a tile to open the brand record.

### Add a brand

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On the <strong>Brands</strong> tab, click <strong>Add Brand</strong>.</li>
  <li>Enter the brand name as shown on the storefront.</li>
  <li>Enter the code: the 3-character code used in SKUs. ThreadCloud suggests one as you type the name: the first three letters. When that code is already taken by another brand, it suggests the next unused one instead (for example from the first letter of each word, then the first letter plus the next consonants), so you don't have to invent one. Type your own and it stops suggesting.</li>
  <li><strong>Default supplier</strong>: pick who invoices. For an agent-introduced brand, choose the skip option and set the biller later.</li>
  <li><strong>Agent</strong>: optional, the firm that introduces the brand.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Add Brand</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Pick the code carefully</div>
The brand code goes into every SKU for the brand. Changing it later means re-stamping SKUs. Email <a href="mailto:hello@threadcloud.io">hello@threadcloud.io</a> before changing a code on a brand that already has products.
</div>

### Add a supplier

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On the <strong>Suppliers</strong> tab, click <strong>Add Supplier</strong>.</li>
  <li>Fill in <strong>Name</strong> (required), <strong>Country</strong>, <strong>City</strong>, <strong>Currency</strong>, <strong>Payment Terms</strong>, <strong>Website</strong>, <strong>Notes</strong>.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Add Supplier</strong>.</li>
</ol>

Each order carries its own currency, chosen when you create it.

### Bulk import

Under **Bulk import** at the foot of the registry:

- Download the **CSV TEMPLATE**, fill it in, then **Import or update from CSV**. A first import creates suppliers, brands and agents; a re-import updates existing rows in place.
- **Export current registry** downloads what is in ThreadCloud now. Edit it and import it back.

<div class="callout callout-danger">
<div class="callout-title">Danger zone</div>
<strong>Reset all suppliers &amp; brands</strong> wipes the registry and asks you to type a confirmation phrase. Don't use it on a live store without talking to ThreadCloud first.
</div>

## The brand record

A brand opens to rooms: **About**, **Models**, **Kit**, **People** and, in the new design, **Buying**. Switch with the tabs under the brand name.

### About

- **Brand details**: brand name and brand code. Click **Save details**.
- **The Foundation**: source-cited facts about the brand, such as **Headquarters**, **Founders**, **Heritage**, **Known for**, **Typical materials** and **Typical construction**. Click **Research with Claude** to fill them from the brand's own sources (it needs an Anthropic key under **Settings → Connections & sync → AI connection**; see [AI &amp; Pipeline](/settings/ai-models/)). Edit anything, then **Save draft** or **Approve**. Approved facts feed product descriptions. **Do not claim** lists things the copy must never say.
- **Description intro**: your own opening line for this brand's product descriptions.
- **Billed via** and **Agency**: the brand's supplier and agent. **Billed via** in the brand's header opens the same **Billed via** sheet as **Change biller**: pick **Biller pending**, **Direct** (the brand bills you itself) or one of your suppliers. When a purchase order has no one to send to, the PO page links straight to this sheet, already open.
- **Order portal** (shown as **B2B portal** in the classic design): the brand's wholesale ordering website and its login, if it has one. **Add B2B portal** saves the platform, link and login (stored encrypted; any staff can reveal it). Once saved the card reads **Open [portal]** and **Manage portal**. This is your login to the brand's ordering site; it is not something the brand sees.
- **Publish to store**: publishes the brand story to your Shopify store.

#### Set a live product's stock type

Buying staff see a **Stock type** row on the brand's **Buying** room, under **How you buy** (for example "Stock type · 6 products · 1 disagree"). It opens a sheet: pick the products first, grouped by their current stock type with **Select all** or **Select none** for each group, then choose the type under **Set to** (**MTO**, **STOCK**, **NOS** or **SPECIAL**) and confirm with **Set N to [type]**. The confirm step says exactly what changes:

- **ThreadCloud**: every style linked to each picked product takes the new stock type.
- **Shopify**: the stock type field and the storefront tags (the `nos` and `stock-service` tags are swapped as needed), and the product type where it names a stock type. A product that ThreadCloud has never written to in Shopify changes in ThreadCloud only, and its first push carries the type.
- **Sales history**: past sales keep the type they sold under until **Rebuild sales history** (Settings, Automations).

Each product row starts with the supplier's reference ("Ref", then the style code and colour, or the first variant SKU for a product only in Shopify), so two products with the same title can be told apart.

Where a product's readings disagree (Shopify, its styles, its tags), the sheet says so on the row, and **Select them** picks every product that disagrees. A product the reorder rules or its ownership forbid is refused, with the reason. Each product is changed on its own: a refusal on one never stops the others, and the sheet lists what was set and what was not. The same control is on a single product's style page as a **Stock type** row, with **Set to** the type you want.

### Models

The garments you keep buying from the brand, their bodies, size runs and measurements, and the supplier's spec sheets. See [Brand fit](/settings/brand-fit/).

### Kit

- **Resources**: logos, linesheets, lookbooks, campaign and seasonal images, documents and web links, filtered by season (**Add resource**).
- **Product photography**: drop photos here. Name them by style and colour code (for example `AB1234_010_front.jpg`) and ThreadCloud matches each to the style. Photos it can't match show under **Unmatched**.

### Buying

**Buying** (it was called **Orders**, and an old link to it still lands here) lists the brand's whole buying history, newest first: **Season** (with the delivery), **PO number** (and the supplier's own number under it), **Status**, **Units**, **Cost** (for people who see costs, with "N of M costed" where some units have no cost) and **Received**. Status and received read the receiving contract, so a fully received reorder reads Received whatever it was last saved as. Historical and archived orders are included and marked; pre-owned lots are left out. Click a row to open the order. It loads the first time you open the tab and reads only. It reads **No orders for this brand yet.** when there are none.

Under the orders, a group called **How you buy** holds the **Stock type** row (see [Set a live product's stock type](#set-a-live-products-stock-type)). It used to sit on About.

### People

Everyone you deal with for this brand, in three groups: the brand's own team, the supplier's people and the agency's people.

A person you add from a brand belongs to that brand. They get this brand's POs and nobody else's. When the same person also works for the supplier's other brands, tick **For every brand at this supplier** and they are shared. If you correct a shared person's email on one brand and leave that box off, the brand gets its own contact with the new address. The shared person stays for the other brands, and the old address stops being copied on this brand's POs.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click <strong>Add person</strong>. Enter name, role, email and phone, and where they work.</li>
  <li>Tick <strong>CC on order emails</strong> to copy them when you email an order for this brand.</li>
  <li>Filed in the wrong group? Use <strong>Move to another group</strong>. Their details move with them.</li>
</ol>

**Who are they with?** When you type a supplier contact on an order and that person is new to the brand, ThreadCloud asks one question: **Who is [name] with?** The choices are the brand's biller (marked **Billed via**), its agency (marked **Agency**), **A new agency** (type its name), the brand's own head office, or **Just this order**. Where the company already has reps, you can tick **Replaces [name]** for any of them that this person takes over from. **Save** makes the person a rep on the brand under that company.

**Former.** When you change a brand's biller or agency, ThreadCloud saves the change and then asks: "Is [old company] still repping [brand]?" **Still repping** changes nothing. **No longer** moves that company's people on this brand to a quiet **Former** list on the People room, showing who repped the brand and until when. **Link again** on a row brings someone back. Nobody is deleted.

## The Brands list: who gets each PO

The **Brands** tab is the master list of how every brand is ordered. Each row shows the **Brand**, **Orders through**, the **Contact** a PO goes to and a **Status** pill. **Orders through** says who the brand orders through: **Brand direct**, an **Agency** or a **Distributor** (a supplier), with a quiet "On portal" when the brand has an order portal. A pill appears only when a PO could not reach anyone:

| Pill | What it means |
|---|---|
| **No email** | Nobody on this brand has an email, so a PO has no one to go to. |
| **Shared Default** | The PO would be addressed to the catch-all supplier called Default, not a real company. |
| **Agency has no rep** | The brand orders through an agency and nobody is named there. |

The list opens on **Needs attention**, the brands with a pill. It reads **Every brand can receive a PO** when there are none. Switch to **All brands** for the whole list. The **Status** chip narrows the list to brands **On portal** or, for people who see brand research, by the state of the brand's record (such as **Draft** or **Approved**).

### Fix a brand in one place

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click a brand's row. A sheet opens with <strong>Open brand page</strong> on top.</li>
  <li>Pick <strong>Orders through</strong>: <strong>Brand direct</strong>, <strong>Agency</strong> or <strong>Distributor</strong>, and choose the company. A shared company reads "Used by 12 brands".</li>
  <li>Fill in the <strong>Contact</strong>, <strong>Email</strong> and <strong>Phone</strong>, and tick <strong>CC on orders</strong> if they should be copied. For a distributor the sheet also shows <strong>Terms</strong> and <strong>Currency</strong>.</li>
  <li>If you changed a shared person's details, decide whether <strong>For every brand at this supplier</strong> should be ticked.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Save</strong>. Nothing is written until you do.</li>
</ol>

Choosing another agency or supplier changes nothing until **Save**, and a line above **Save** says where the POs would go. A save that takes the brand off its old supplier or agency moves that company's people on this brand to the brand's **Former** list (see People above), so they stop getting its POs and can be linked again.

### Paste a list

When the answers are in a spreadsheet or an email, click **Paste a list**. Paste rows of brand, the company they order through, the email and the person (commas, tabs or semicolons all work). A header row is fine. ThreadCloud matches each row to a brand and shows a preview. A row with **No match** has a picker so you can choose the brand yourself. A company it does not know is marked **(new)** and is created on Apply. Nothing is written until you click **Apply to N brands**. A blank cell stays blank: a row with no company never turns the contact into a new biller.

### Who a PO goes to

A real PO, a reorder PO, the print view, the supplier link and **Message supplier** all use one rule. They go to the people linked to this brand, plus the supplier's people who are linked to no brand at all (an order desk). They never go to a former rep of this brand, or to a person who reps only other brands. A brand with no links and no former reps at its supplier uses the supplier's whole list. A document already sent keeps the people it went to. The sheet, Paste a list and this list read the same rule, so the contact you see is the contact the PO uses.

## Orders from an agency

A rep from an agency that uses TC Wholesale can write an order for you in the store and send you a recap to confirm. Once you have confirmed that agency for the brand and switched on **Bring confirmed orders in**, every order you confirm with them comes into ThreadCloud by itself, as a draft order. Nobody retypes it. Only the account owner can do this.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>From <strong>Network</strong>, open <strong>Agency confirmations</strong>.</li>
  <li>If the agency is under <strong>Needs you</strong> (it reads "<em>agency</em> says they represent <em>brand</em>"), press <strong>Confirm</strong>. If they do not represent that brand for you, press <strong>Not us</strong>. If the agency has not asked, use <strong>Name the agency for a brand</strong> to name it yourself.</li>
  <li>Under <strong>Your answers</strong>, find the agency and brand and press <strong>Bring confirmed orders in</strong>. The row then reads <strong>Orders you confirm with them come into ThreadCloud as draft POs.</strong></li>
</ol>

From then on:

- An order arrives only after you confirm the rep's recap. Orders confirmed before you switched it on do not come in.
- It arrives as a draft order for that brand and season, with the brand's photos from the rep's book where there are any. Check it, then enrich it and send the PO like any other order.
- If the rep sends you a changed version and you confirm it, the draft follows the change, while its PO has not gone out.
- You must carry the brand in ThreadCloud. If you do not, the order stays with the agency, and the rep sees why.
- A recap priced in more than one currency does not come in, so ask the rep for one currency per order.
- To stop, press **Stop bringing orders in**. The row then reads **Orders you confirm with them stay with them.** Orders already in ThreadCloud stay where they are.

If the page says **Your store is not registered with the ThreadCloud network yet, so agencies cannot ask you to confirm them.**, ask Winston in **Help**.

## Details from your agency

If one of your brands comes through an agency that uses TC Wholesale, and you have confirmed that agency, you can copy its current details into your own contacts. Open **Agency confirmations**, a page under **Network** (the page where you also name the agency for a brand). A confirmed agency shows as **From** its name, with what it shares: its name, website, phone, order email and address, and the name and email of the people who are your buyer's contact there. A person's phone shows only if they chose to share it.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Press <strong>Update my contacts</strong>.</li>
  <li>Under <strong>Your agent</strong>, check which of your agents it is. ThreadCloud suggests one by website, then by name, and says so. Change it if it is wrong.</li>
  <li>Each field that differs shows your value and the agency's. A field is ticked for you only where yours is empty. Tick what you want to copy.</li>
  <li>Save. Nothing is copied that you did not tick, and an empty agency field never clears yours.</li>
</ol>

When your contacts already match, the sheet says **Your contacts already match the agency.** Notes, terms and active status are never changed. To tell ThreadCloud which agency represents a brand, use **Name the agency for a brand** on that page.

## Adding a brand from a new order

On a new order, if you type a brand that isn't in your Network, the page reads **No brand called "[name]".** with **Add it**. It opens the Network with that name already in the search so you can see whether the brand exists under a slightly different name before adding it. A brand you rename keeps its link to the shared brand network.

On the Network, each brand card also shows how many orders are open with it. An order counts as open until it is fully received (**Nothing more is coming** counts as received). Imported history, archived and cancelled orders, custom orders and lots don't count.

## Deleting a brand

**Danger Zone → Delete Brand** on the brand record. It can't be undone and affects every purchase order that references the brand. If you've simply stopped carrying a brand, don't delete it. Its orders and history stay with the record.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">No supplier email for orders</div>
If nobody on the brand's People room has an email, <strong>Email brand contact</strong> on the PO tab can't address the order. Add the order contact before sending the first PO.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Two records for one brand</div>
Search the registry before adding a brand. If two records exist for one brand, email <a href="mailto:hello@threadcloud.io">hello@threadcloud.io</a> rather than deleting one.
</div>

## On the PO page

Before you send, the PO page names a brand that **Goes to no one** (no ordering email) or a PO **Addressed to Default**. Each line has a button (**Add one**, **Choose the supplier**) that opens the same brand sheet right there. It never blocks **Send**, and an issued PO never prints "Default" as the addressee.

When you edit a supplier or one of its contacts that more than one brand uses, a line above **Save** warns "Used by 3 brands. Saving changes all their POs." **See which** lists them.
