# Brand Registry

**Brand Registry** in the ThreadCloud menu is the directory of every brand you carry, the suppliers who bill you, and the agents who introduce brands. Each brand opens to its **brand record**: its facts, its fit, its photography and its people.

If a tab or field here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>

## How brands, suppliers and agents fit together

- A **brand** is what shows on the storefront.
- A **supplier** is who bills you: the entity on the invoice. A brand points to its supplier.
- An **agent** is the firm that introduces a brand to your store, such as a sales agency or showroom. Optional.
- **Biller pending** on a brand means its supplier isn't set yet. That's normal for an agent-introduced brand until you know who invoices.

## The registry

Three tabs: **Brands**, **Suppliers**, **Agents**. The search box finds brands, codes, suppliers and contacts.

Each brand tile shows its code, its name, **· inactive** if it is no longer active, **biller pending** if it has no supplier, and **Foundation ✓** or **Draft** for the state of its brand facts. Click a tile to open the brand record.

### Add a brand

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On the <strong>Brands</strong> tab, click <strong>Add Brand</strong>.</li>
  <li>Enter the brand name as shown on the storefront.</li>
  <li>Enter the code: the 3-character code used in SKUs.</li>
  <li><strong>Default supplier</strong>: pick who invoices. For an agent-introduced brand, choose the skip option and set the biller later.</li>
  <li><strong>Agent</strong>: optional, the firm that introduces the brand.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Add Brand</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Pick the code carefully</div>
The brand code goes into every SKU for the brand. Changing it later means re-stamping SKUs. Email <a href="mailto:hello@threadcloud.io">hello@threadcloud.io</a> before changing a code on a brand that already has products.
</div>

### Add a supplier

<ol class="steps">
  <li>On the <strong>Suppliers</strong> tab, click <strong>Add Supplier</strong>.</li>
  <li>Fill in <strong>Name</strong> (required), <strong>Country</strong>, <strong>City</strong>, <strong>Currency</strong>, <strong>Payment Terms</strong>, <strong>Website</strong>, <strong>Notes</strong>.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Add Supplier</strong>.</li>
</ol>

Each order carries its own currency, chosen when you create it.

### Bulk import

Under **Bulk import** at the foot of the registry:

- Download the **CSV TEMPLATE**, fill it in, then **Import or update from CSV**. A first import creates suppliers, brands and agents; a re-import updates existing rows in place.
- **Export current registry** downloads what is in ThreadCloud now. Edit it and import it back.

<div class="callout callout-danger">
<div class="callout-title">Danger zone</div>
<strong>Reset all suppliers &amp; brands</strong> wipes the registry and asks you to type a confirmation phrase. Don't use it on a live store without talking to ThreadCloud first.
</div>

## The brand record

A brand opens to four rooms. Switch with the tabs under the brand name.

### About

- **Brand details**: brand name and brand code. Click **Save details**.
- **The Foundation**: source-cited facts about the brand, such as **Headquarters**, **Founders**, **Heritage**, **Known for**, **Typical materials** and **Typical construction**. Click **Research with Claude** to fill them from the brand's own sources (it needs an Anthropic key in [AI &amp; Pipeline](/settings/ai-models/)). Edit anything, then **Save draft** or **Approve**. Approved facts feed product descriptions. **Do not claim** lists things the copy must never say.
- **Description intro**: your own opening line for this brand's product descriptions.
- **Billed via** and **Agency**: the brand's supplier and agent.
- **B2B portal**: the brand's wholesale portal link, if it has one (**Add B2B portal**).
- **Publish to store**: publishes the brand story to your Shopify store.

### Models

The garments you keep buying from the brand, their bodies, size runs and measurements, and the supplier's spec sheets. See [Brand fit](/settings/brand-fit/).

### Kit

- **Resources**: logos, linesheets, lookbooks, campaign and seasonal images, documents and web links, filtered by season (**Add resource**).
- **Product photography**: drop photos here. Name them by style and colour code (for example `AB1234_010_front.jpg`) and ThreadCloud matches each to the style. Photos it can't match show under **Unmatched**.

### People

Everyone you deal with for this brand, in three groups: the brand's own team, the supplier's people and the agency's people.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click <strong>Add person</strong>. Enter name, role, email and phone, and where they work.</li>
  <li>Tick <strong>CC on order emails</strong> to copy them when you email an order for this brand.</li>
  <li>Filed in the wrong group? Use <strong>Move to another group</strong>. Their details move with them.</li>
</ol>

## Deleting a brand

**Danger Zone → Delete Brand** on the brand record. It can't be undone and affects every purchase order that references the brand. If you've simply stopped carrying a brand, don't delete it. Its orders and history stay with the record.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">No supplier email for orders</div>
If nobody on the brand's People room has an email, <strong>Email brand contact</strong> on the PO tab can't address the order. Add the order contact before sending the first PO.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Two records for one brand</div>
Search the registry before adding a brand. If two records exist for one brand, email <a href="mailto:hello@threadcloud.io">hello@threadcloud.io</a> rather than deleting one.
</div>
