# Storefront

**Settings → Storefront** is where ThreadCloud meets your shop window: the theme embed and blocks, your Search &amp; Discovery filters, the Essentials page, brand stories and the size guide.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-01</div>
If a card or row here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
Once at setup, then when you change theme, add a filter, or after a big catalog change. Theme changes are for the store owner.
</div>

## Storefront activation

| Row | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Connection** | Connected automatically when you installed. It reads **Done for you**. If it doesn't, click **Reconnect on the integration page**. |
| **1 Smart Filters embed** | Click **Open theme editor**, switch on the ThreadCloud app embed under App embeds, then save your theme. |
| **2 Fit Callout block** | Click **Open theme editor**, add the block to your product template, then save. |

Each row reads **Saved** once confirmed.

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">Size chart and size note blocks</div>
ThreadCloud's theme app also has a <strong>Size chart</strong> block, which shows the measurements from <a href="/settings/brand-fit/">Brand fit</a> on products that have a model, and a <strong>Size note</strong> block. Add them to your product template in the theme editor the same way.
</div>

## Search &amp; Discovery filters

Your storefront filters run through Shopify's free Search &amp; Discovery app, reading the product facts ThreadCloud publishes.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click <strong>Check now</strong>. ThreadCloud checks Shopify directly.</li>
  <li>Read <strong>Core sources live</strong> (for example 9/9) and <strong>To fix</strong>.</li>
  <li>For each source to fix, follow the instruction on its row and click <strong>Open Search &amp; Discovery</strong>.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Check now</strong> again. When every core source is live, the row reads <strong>All core sources · Live on your storefront</strong> and <strong>Complete</strong>.</li>
</ol>

The core filters, as shoppers see them:

| Filter label | Product fact it reads |
|---|---|
| Size | Customer sizes (per size) |
| Color | Colour group |
| Brand | Brand |
| Category | Category |
| Type | Subcategory |
| Fit | Fit type |
| Pattern | Pattern type |
| Material | Main fibre |
| Made In | Country of origin |

More are available when your assortment needs them, such as Chest, Waist, Collar, Length, Sleeve, Shoe Size, Formality and Knit Weight. The setup walk lists them under **Add when relevant to your assortment**.

Before a first check it reads **Not checked yet · Run the first check once your catalog is labelled.** After a check it shows when it was last checked; it rechecks after catalog changes.

## Essentials page

ThreadCloud decides which products show on your storefront's Essentials page. NOS, year-round and seasonal pieces you keep buying get there on their own. A deep buy is another way in, and the **Essentials page** card's **Order qty to qualify as Essential** sets how deep.

- The default is **12**. A product joins once one season's buy reaches that many units, and stays until that season ends.
- **0** turns this rule off. Products then join only as NOS, year-round, seasonal or kept pieces.
- Special orders never count.

Only the account owner can change it: type the number and click <strong>Save</strong>. Everyone else sees the current value. The Essentials page picks up a change at 3am store time.

## Brand stories

Approved brands publish to Shopify, linked to their collections. Switch on **Publish on approval** to publish each brand's story when you approve its Foundation in [Brand Registry](/settings/brands-suppliers/). If Shopify needs new permissions, the card says **Approve ThreadCloud's updated permissions in Shopify.**

## Display choices

- **Filter labels &amp; order**: click **Edit** to open **Filter style**. Here **Category Groups** bundle raw categories into one shopper-facing group (for example Sport Jacket, Blazer and Suit Jacket under Jackets &amp; Blazers). **Seed defaults** fills the standard groups.
- **Size guide**: click **Open**. See below.

## Size guide

**Storefront size guide** shows how ThreadCloud groups native sizes for shoppers, for jackets and tailoring, trousers and dress shirts. Native sizes describe the garment; the storefront group helps shoppers browse across brands. ThreadCloud keeps the native size and also writes an alpha size group plus exact chest, waist or collar values.

- **Copy staff link** to share it with the floor.
- **Print or save PDF** for a paper copy.

## Restock plans

The **Restock plans** card shows **Active plans**, **Brands covered** and the **Last run**. **+ New plan** starts one and **Manage plans** lists them. See [Reorder](/workflows/reorder/).

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">A filter that shows almost nothing</div>
A filter only lists values products actually carry. If most products have no pattern yet, the Pattern filter is nearly empty. Publish your catalog first in <a href="/settings/legacy-enrichment/">Catalog setup</a>.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Editing ThreadCloud's tags or facts in Shopify Admin</div>
Filters read what ThreadCloud wrote. Change a product's category or colour in ThreadCloud, not in Shopify Admin, so the two don't disagree.
</div>
