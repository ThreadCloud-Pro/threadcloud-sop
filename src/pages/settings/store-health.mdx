# Settings and store health

**Settings & Help** in the ThreadCloud menu opens on a welcome page. It greets you, tells you whether ThreadCloud is working, and lists every setting in three groups. Winston's **Help** is on the same page (see [Help and Winston](/help/)).

If a tile or label here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.

<div class="callout callout-info">

## Search

The box under the greeting reads **What can I help you find?**. Type the words you use in the store (try "logo", "price rounding", "filters" or "sync"). It lists the settings that match, each with the group it is in. With nothing matching it says **No settings match that search.** and offers **Ask Winston**.

## What needs you

The heading says how many things need you, for example **Two things need you**, or **Nothing needs you**. Each problem is one line with a **Fix** at its end:

- Where the fix is a check, **Fix** runs it in place and reads **Checking** while it works.
- Otherwise **Fix** opens the row that holds the problem. Some lines carry up to three named buttons instead of one.

The last, muted row shows the readings: for example **Shopify orders synced 12 min ago**, **Inventory swept 3 h ago**, **AI connected** and **Storefront filters ready**. It is headed **Everything is current** when every reading is current, and **Where things stand** while something is running or unknown. A line that says **Not checked** is not a fault: ThreadCloud hasn't looked yet.

The six parts it watches are Storefront filters, AI engines, the Catalog, Shopify sync, Sales history and the Nightly capture. Only the account owner can run some checks, and the page says so where it applies.

## The three groups

| Group | Tiles |
|---|---|
| **How you buy** | **Buying** (pricing, ship windows and the [delivery calendar](/settings/delivery-windows/)), **Brands & suppliers** (the [Network](/settings/brands-suppliers/)), **Receiving** (tag printing and measuring) |
| **How you sell** | **Product pages** (brand voice and writing rules, see [AI &amp; Pipeline](/settings/ai-models/)), **Catalog** (required facts, occasions and size guide, see [Catalog setup](/settings/legacy-enrichment/)), **Storefront** (theme setup and shopper filters, see [Storefront](/settings/theme/)) |
| **Your store** | **Store &amp; team** (store details, [Team access](/settings/users-roles/) and data sharing), **Connections &amp; sync**, **Claude** (see [Connect Claude](/settings/claude/)), **Phones** (only where the phone app is on, see [Phone app](/workflows/phone-app/)), **My preferences** (start page, opening period and display) |

Each tile shows a few live values, such as the store currency or how many brands you carry. A person sees only the tiles their switches open: Floor and Receiver see Phones and My preferences, not the buying, catalog or store pages (see [Team access](/settings/users-roles/)). Where setup isn't finished, a **Store setup** row (**Your first catalog**) sits under the page.

## My preferences

**Settings → My preferences** holds choices that follow you, not the store. Nobody else sees them. Under **Follows you** are **Design**, **Home shortcut** and **Opening period** (see [Merchandising](/planning/trading/) for the last two). Under **This browser** are **Appearance** (**Light** or **Dark**) and **Density** (**Comfortable** or **Compact**), which are kept on the device you are using.

**Design** has three options:

| Option | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Store default (new)** or **Store default (classic)** | Whatever the store is set to. The label names which one that is. |
| **New** | The redesigned screens, called Clarity. |
| **Classic** | The screens as they were. |

Your choice applies as soon as you click it, and the phone app follows it too. Without a choice of your own you follow the store default, which is classic. The new design is still being refined, so screens will keep changing while it is.

If the Design options are switched off, ThreadCloud does not know who you are. Reopen it from Shopify admin to keep a choice.

## Receiving readiness

**Settings → Receiving → Open orders check** (account owner) has a **Receiving readiness** row with an **Open the check** button. It checks every open order against Shopify before the goods arrive. It is read only: nothing is written to ThreadCloud or Shopify.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click <strong>Run the check</strong>. Run it before the first delivery of each season.</li>
  <li>Read the findings: each style with units to come that would be blocked at receiving, and why (for example, not on Shopify yet).</li>
  <li>Use <strong>Repair</strong> on a finding, or fix it on the order. Then <strong>Run again</strong>.</li>
</ol>

When nothing is wrong it says **Every style with units to come will receive cleanly.**

## Automations

**Settings → Connections &amp; sync** has four tabs: **AI connection**, **Shopify** (Orders, Shopify stock, Store reset), **Sales history** and **Inventory**, with one row per job. A tab shows how many rows failed. Open a row that needs attention.

| Row | What it runs | Recovery buttons you may see |
|---|---|---|
| **Orders** | Shopify order sync, every hour | **Run the sync now** |
| **Sales history** | The record of past sales | **Capture the baseline**, **Rebuild sales history**, **Retry skipped orders**, **Resume historical ledger**, **Resume from saved checkpoint** |
| **Inventory** | The nightly stock position and the hourly on-hand sweep | **Capture today's position**, **Resume sweeping this store** |
| **Shopify stock** | Every stock change receiving sends to Shopify | **Read Shopify back** |
| **Catalog** | Products published, and refinements waiting | **Refine catalog** (opens [Catalog setup](/settings/legacy-enrichment/)) |
| **Store reset** | Shown only while a store reset is unfinished | **Resume the reset** |

Recovery buttons say who can press them. The sales history buttons and **Resume the reset** are for the store's account owner (**Store account owner only**); others need owner access (**Owners only**).

### Shopify stock

The row reads **Every stock write confirmed by Shopify** when all is well, or **N stock writes awaiting confirmation**. Receiving refuses those sizes until each one is settled. They are settled with **Read Shopify back**, which needs owner access. The full procedure is on [Shopify stock sync](/workflows/shopify-stock-sync/).

### Store reset

A store reset is only possible on stores ThreadCloud has set up for testing. If one is unfinished, the row says when it started and that catalog capture, Clean Catalog and Shopify stock writes are standing down until it finishes. See [If something breaks](/runbook/#store-reset).

## The bar at the top of every page

The thin bar at the top of ThreadCloud pages shows background AI work as it runs, for example **Combing the rack** while the catalog scan runs. When nothing is running it reads **Nothing on the loom**. That's about AI work only; the tiles here are the store's health.
