# Delivery calendar

A delivery window is the stretch of months an order is due to land, for example **Fall &amp; Winter Delivery 1**, July to August. Every order is placed in one. Each window belongs to a season group, and the group decides which buying season the order counts toward.

## Where it is

**Settings → Buying &amp; planning → Delivery calendar**. It is also one of the **Quick settings** tiles on the Settings page. The page is titled **Delivery windows**.

## The default calendar

A new store starts with six windows:

| Window | Code | Months | Season group |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spring &amp; Summer Delivery 1 | `SS-1` | January to February | Spring &amp; Summer |
| Spring &amp; Summer Delivery 2 | `SS-2` | March to April | Spring &amp; Summer |
| Pre-Fall | `PRE_FALL` | May to June | Pre-Fall |
| Fall &amp; Winter Delivery 1 | `FW-1` | July to August | Fall &amp; Winter |
| Fall &amp; Winter Delivery 2 | `FW-2` | September to October | Fall &amp; Winter |
| Resort | `RESORT` | November to December | Resort |

Resort has a year shift of -1: Resort 2026 ships in November and December 2025.

Pre-Fall orders count toward the Fall &amp; Winter buying season, and Resort orders toward Spring &amp; Summer.

## Confirm the calendar

During setup, Home shows a **Confirm delivery calendar** tile. Open it, use the defaults or adjust them, then click **This calendar is right**. It says **Delivery calendar confirmed. You can still change it anytime.**

## What a window holds

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What staff see when picking the window |
| **Code** | Short code used in the season identifier and SKU |
| **Start Month** | When the window opens |
| **End Month** | When the window closes |
| **Season group** | Groups deliveries into a buying season, for example `FW-1` + `FW-2` = Fall &amp; Winter |
| **Year shift** | Years between the delivery year and the buying-season year. Most windows are 0. |
| **Position** | The order windows are listed in |

## Changing the calendar

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click <strong>Add delivery window</strong> for a new window, or edit a row in place. Click <strong>Save</strong>.</li>
  <li>In the calendar view, drag a window to move it, or drag an edge to resize it.</li>
  <li>To remove a window, click <strong>Delete window</strong> and confirm.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Changing a code that orders use</div>
If orders already use a window's code, ThreadCloud asks before changing it and offers <strong>Change code and update</strong> those orders, so they stay linked. Products already in Shopify keep the season tag they were pushed with.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-danger">
<div class="callout-title">Reset windows</div>
<strong>Reset windows</strong> puts every window back to the defaults above. Your own changes to those windows are lost.
</div>

## Tools at the foot of the page

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Buying Order Backfill** | Links existing orders to buying seasons from their season code and window group. Orders already linked are skipped. Custom orders without a window group are left out. |
| **Consolidate season groups** | Folds a standalone Pre-Fall or Resort buying season into Fall &amp; Winter or Spring &amp; Summer. Preview first; nothing changes until you apply. |

## Moving one order to another window

Use **Change delivery window** in the order's header, on any of its tabs. The order moves to the matching buying season.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Too many windows</div>
Every window is a choice staff make on each new order. Keep to the windows you really buy in.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">A window in the wrong season group</div>
The season group decides which buying season the order counts toward in <a href="/planning/buying-seasons/">Buying Plan</a>. Check it when you add a window.
</div>
