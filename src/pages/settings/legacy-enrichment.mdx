# Catalog setup

Catalog setup brings the products already in your Shopify store up to ThreadCloud's standard: category, colour, size, pattern, season and stock type. Once published, they show up in your storefront filters and in Merchandising. The page is titled **Set up your catalog**. Settings and Home call the same page **Clean Catalog**.

<div class="callout-title">Last verified against the app: 2026-10-01</div>
If a tab or button here doesn't match the app, it changed and this page is stale. Tell the team.

<div class="callout callout-info">
<div class="callout-title">When to use</div>
ThreadCloud labels your catalog during <a href="/setup/onboarding/">first-time setup</a>, so most stores only come here to review what it left for a person, to scan products added since, or when Home shows a <strong>Catalog enrichment</strong> banner.
</div>

## Where it is

- **Settings** → the **Clean Catalog** card → **Open Clean Catalog**.
- **Settings → Automations** → the **Catalog** row → **Refine catalog**.
- Home's **Catalog enrichment** banner or Needs you row.

## What it never changes

Publishing adds ThreadCloud's category, colour, pattern and size facts to your Shopify products. **Titles, prices, photos, inventory, and existing variants stay unchanged.** Anything ThreadCloud isn't sure of is held for you to review, not written.

## The page at a glance

The top card names where your catalog stands:

| Heading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Make your existing products filterable** | Nothing scanned yet |
| **Checking your catalog** | ThreadCloud is working through your products |
| **Checking is stopped** | Some products are still unchecked and nothing is running |
| **Your catalog needs a few decisions** | Some products are waiting for you |
| **Your catalog is ready to publish** | Review is done; publish when ready |
| **Your catalog is filter-ready** | Published |
| **Your catalog needs attention** | Some products could not be checked |

Under it, **Your catalog status** counts **Ready to publish**, **Needs your review**, **Published** and **Problems**.

## First run

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Click <strong>Scan my catalog</strong>. ThreadCloud checks your active Shopify products for category, colour and size. You can leave the page; it carries on.</li>
  <li>When the check finishes, it says how many are ready to publish and how many need your review.</li>
  <li>Review the products in <strong>Needs your review</strong> (below).</li>
  <li>Publish the ready ones (below).</li>
</ol>

## Review product decisions

The table has five tabs: **All**, **Needs your review**, **Ready to publish**, **Published**, **Problems**.

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Needs your review</strong>.</li>
  <li>Narrow it with <strong>Brand</strong>, or with <strong>Fix together</strong>, which groups products missing the same thing.</li>
  <li>Click a row to open it. Check <strong>Category</strong>, <strong>Subcategory</strong>, <strong>Colour Group</strong>, <strong>Colour Name</strong>, <strong>Pattern</strong>, <strong>Size Scale</strong>, <strong>Season</strong>, <strong>Stock Type</strong> and <strong>Vendor Ref</strong>.</li>
  <li>If ThreadCloud's suggestions are right, click <strong>Keep these suggestions</strong>. If not, correct the fields and click <strong>Save product details</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout callout-tip">
<div class="callout-title">"Needs your review" doesn't mean wrong</div>
It means a fact is missing or ThreadCloud isn't sure. Often the suggestion is right and one click keeps it.
</div>

### Many products at once

- Tick rows (shift-click selects a range on the page), then **Edit selected products**. Pick each field to change, or **Don't change**, then **Apply changes to N products**.
- **Dismiss** in the same dialog drops low-quality rows from the review queue without writing anything to Shopify.
- **Choose catalog family** then **Group selected** sets Menswear or Womenswear for the selected products.
- Changing the brand or the tab clears your selection, so a bulk action never includes rows you can't see.

### Decide once per brand

Most brands are bought one way. The **Decide once per brand** card lists each brand with how its reviewed products split. Pick a **Set stock type** for the brand (**Leave for review**, **MTO**, **Stock**, **NOS**, **Special**) and click **Apply to N products**. Where a brand's history is lopsided, the likely type is already selected. It never overwrites a stock type you set by hand.

### Seasons for old products

Click **Suggest seasons from product age** (it reads **Dating the rack** while it runs). It fills only empty Season cells from each product's Shopify creation date. Every suggestion still goes through your review.

For genuinely old product with no meaningful season, type **LEGACY** in the Season field.

## Publish to Shopify

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open <strong>Run a step yourself</strong> → <strong>Publish filters to Shopify</strong>.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Select All Ready (N)</strong>. It selects the ready rows you can see.</li>
  <li>Click <strong>Publish selected (N)</strong> and confirm.</li>
</ol>

Published products move to the **Published** tab and show up in your storefront filters.

## Run a step yourself

The card under the status runs each stage on its own:

| Step | Button |
|---|---|
| **Scan products** | **Scan products**, or **Scan new products** to add products created since the last scan |
| **Check products** | **Check products**. **Stop** halts it. |
| **Review uncertain products** | **Suggest seasons from product age**, and **Reset All** |
| **Publish filters to Shopify** | **Select All Ready (N)**, **Publish selected (N)** |
| **Link products to ThreadCloud** | **Import N Products**. Brings published products into ThreadCloud's own records, so they count in Merchandising and catalog repair. Available after publishing. |

**Check footwear sizing** is for after publishing, when footwear sizes need a check.

## Rolling back

<ol class="steps">
  <li>Open the <strong>Published</strong> tab.</li>
  <li>Tick the products to undo, then <strong>Rollback Selected (N)</strong>.</li>
  <li>Confirm <strong>Rollback N products</strong>. ThreadCloud restores the values the products had before it published.</li>
</ol>

## Problems

The **Problems** tab lists products that could not be checked, with the reason. Fix the product in Shopify if the reason points there, then scan or check again. If a repair from Reorder failed, it shows here with **Retry repair**.

## Common mistakes

<div class="callout callout-warn">
<div class="callout-title">Publishing without reviewing</div>
Publish only what's in Ready to publish. Walk Needs your review at least once; old catalog data is thinner than a supplier linesheet.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-danger">
<div class="callout-title">Reset All</div>
<strong>Reset All</strong> clears the review queue and starts fresh. It throws away every decision you made. Don't use it to fix a few rows.
</div>
