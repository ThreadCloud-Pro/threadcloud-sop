## Finding your way around

ThreadCloud opens inside your Shopify admin. The menu on the left of Shopify lists ThreadCloud's sections:

| Menu item | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **ThreadCloud** | [Home](/home/): the Merchandising desk and the Sales floor desk |
| **Buying Plan** | [Buying Plan](/planning/buying-seasons/): each buying season's plan and budget |
| **Merchandising** | [Merchandising](/planning/trading/): how the store and each brand are selling |
| **Money** | [Money](/planning/money/): store financials for a period |
| **Brand Registry** | [Brand Registry](/settings/brands-suppliers/): brands, suppliers and agents |
| **Photography** | [Photography](/workflows/photography/) |
| **Logistics** | [Logistics](/workflows/logistics/): shipments in motion, label reprints, and the register of sent and unsent purchase orders |
| **Transfer Out** | [Transfer Out](/workflows/transfer-out/): returns to vendor and the Returns Counter |
| **Settings** | [Settings and store health](/settings/store-health/) |

Buying Plan, Merchandising and Money appear only for people the store owner has given Merchandising access. See [Team access](/settings/users-roles/).

## What ThreadCloud is for

ThreadCloud runs the buying work from the supplier appointment to the sales floor:

<ol class="steps">
  <li>
    <strong>Build an order</strong>: drop a supplier linesheet into <strong>Parse &amp; Build</strong>. ThreadCloud reads every style, colour, size and price into a table you review and correct.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Send the purchase order</strong>: issue the PO and send the supplier their confirmation link. Log their confirmation when it comes back.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Enrich</strong>: descriptions, fit notes, fabric, country of origin and the product facts that drive your storefront filters, drafted by ThreadCloud and approved by you.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Push to Shopify</strong>: products, sizes, photos and product facts written to Shopify in one go.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Receive</strong>: when the shipment arrives, count what came in, record measurements and print labels.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Sell and reorder</strong>: read sell-through, stock and money in Merchandising, then reorder what is working.
  </li>
</ol>
